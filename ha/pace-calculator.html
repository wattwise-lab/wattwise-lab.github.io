Sauri, a kallo

Sandunan taki lakabi ne, ba kwatantawa ba: mil ɗaya ya fi kilomita ɗaya tsayi, don haka Lokacin kowace mil koyaushe yana da girma a daidai gudun.

Ƙa’ida

Jimlar lokaci = awanni × 3,600 + mintuna × 60 + daƙiƙa. Tashi a kowace kilomita = jimlar lokaci ÷ nisa (km). Tashi a kowace mil = tafiya a kowace kilomita × 1.609344. Sauri = nisa ÷ jimlar sa'o'i.

Misalin aiki

Nisa 10 km, lokacin ƙarewa 50 mintuna (3,000 daƙiƙa): taki = 3,000 ÷ 10 = daƙiƙa 300 = 5:00 /km. Kowace mil wato 300 × 1.609344 = daƙiƙa 483 = 8:03 /mil. Sauri = 10 ÷ (3,000 ÷ 3,600) = 12.0 km/h (7.46 mph). A daidai wannan gudun, 5 km yana ɗaukar 25:00 kuma gudun marathon yana ɗaukar 3:30:59. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa da ke sama don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Sakamakon shine matsakaici ɗaya a kan dukkan nisan: yana ɗaukar daidai gwargwado kuma yana watsi da dakatawa, ƙasa, yanayi, tsayi, da kuskuren auna GPS, duk waɗanda ke sa ainihin rabuwa ya bambanta. Lokutan da aka yi hasashen wasu nisa suna ɗauka cewa za a iya riƙe wannan gudun, wanda ya zama ƙasa da gaskiya yayin da nisa ke ƙaruwa.

FAQ

Me yasa gudun mil ɗaya yake raguwa fiye da kowace kilomita?

Domin mil ɗaya (1.609344 km) ya fi kilomita ɗaya tsayi, don haka yana ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo daidai gwargwado a daidai wannan gudun.

Shin wannan yana la'akari da tuddai ko tasha?

A'a. Yana raba jimillar lokacin da ya shude ta hanyar nisa, don haka ana daidaita dakatawa da ƙasa zuwa adadi ɗaya na tafiya.

Yaya daidaiton lokacin marathon da aka yi hasashensa?

Kawai a matsayin hasashen lissafi: riƙe gudun 10 km don 42.195 km ya fi wahala fiye da riƙe shi don 10 km, don haka a ɗauki hasashen a matsayin lambobin tunani.

Me zai faru idan lokacina ya haɗa da daƙiƙa sama da 59?

Shigar da su azaman mintuna maimakon - an rufe filin daƙiƙa a 59, daidai da yadda ake rubuta lokutan akai-akai.

An adana bayanai na?

A'a. Wannan lissafin yana aiki a cikin gida a cikin burauzarka kuma ƙimar da ka shigar ba a aika ta zuwa ga bayanan CalcMintly ba.

Zan iya amfani da mil azaman shigarwar nisa?

Canzawa da farko: raba mil da 0.621371 don samun kilomita, ko ninka kilomita da 0.621371 don tafiya ta wata hanyar.
