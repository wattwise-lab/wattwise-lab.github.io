Formula

Count dates from start to end, including both endpoints. Remove weekly rest days for the pattern wey you choose, then subtract holiday deduction wey you enter. Deduct only holidays wey for otherwise be working days.

Example wey show the calculation

From 1 September 2026 to 30 September 2026, including both ends: 30 calendar days, with 22 weekdays and 8 weekend days. With 0 holidays entered, net workdays = 22. Use Reset example above to get dis same result, then enter your own holiday count if holidays fall on weekdays inside di range.

Where e fit work and limits

Di page NO dey remove public holidays or organisation-specific closures automatically. E no dey use country or regional holiday table because holiday calendars dey differ by jurisdiction and change with time. Enter di number of holidays wey fall on weekdays inside your range yourself, or leave am at 0 to count only weekdays. Di calendar dey handle leap years. If end date dey before start date, no count go show.

E use proleptic Gregorian calendar and date-only arithmetic, independent of daylight saving time. Complete years and months use di original day of month, but move am to di last valid day if necessary. Other anniversary conventions fit different; dis no be legal age determination.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Which days count? Choose your weekly rest-day pattern. Di default leave out Saturday and Sunday; di date range count di two end dates.

Holiday deductions must cover only days wey for be working days, without counting weekly rest days twice. Fractional deductions fit mean half days; di total no fit pass di working-day count.
