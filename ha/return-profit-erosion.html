Rushewa

Adadin

Lalaci yana ƙidaya sassa uku a kowace dawowa: ribar da oda zai riƙe, farashin jigilar kaya ta baya, da rabon ƙimar samfur da aka rasa. A guji ƙididdige farashin da ya riga ya shiga cikin adadin riba.

A kallo ɗaya

An zana sandar zaizayar ƙasa akan riba ba tare da riba ba, don haka rabon ribar da aka rasa yana bayyane.

Zaizayar ƙasa a farashin dawowa daban-daban

Yawan dawowaRiba ta lalace

Umarni da farashin dawowa ana riƙe su ne a ƙimar da ke sama; ƙimar dawowa kawai ke canzawa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Zaizayar riba = oda × ƙimar dawowa% × (riba a kowace oda + jigilar kaya ta baya + ƙimar dawowa × asara%). Oda da aka dawo = oda × ƙimar dawowa%.

Misalin aiki

Umarni 3,000, ƙimar dawowa 12%, riba a kowace oda $45, jigilar kaya ta baya $18, ƙimar dawowa $120, asara 25%: oda da aka dawo = 3,000 × 0.12 = 360, zaizayar ƙasa a kowace dawowa = $45 + $18 + $120 × 0.25 = $93, da kuma jimlar zaizayar ƙasa = 360 × $93 = $33,480.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ya haɗa da ribar da aka riga aka bari, jigilar kaya ta baya, da asarar samfura a ƙarƙashin zato. Ba a tsara kuɗin sake haja da aka dawo da su ba, sake sayar da kayan da aka dawo da su, da farashin adana kayayyaki masu ƙayyadadden tsari. Idan adadin ribar ku ta kowane oda ya riga ya ƙare jigilar kaya ko dawowar da ake tsammani, ku fara daidaita shi don guje wa ƙidaya sau biyu.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Zaizayar ƙasa = oda × ƙimar dawowa% × (riba a kowace oda + jigilar kaya ta baya + ƙimar dawowa × asara%). Kowace riba tana kashe ribar da ta ajiye, tare da mayar da ita, tare da rubuta ƙimar samfurin.

Menene ma'anar kaso na asarar?

Kason ƙimar samfurin da aka dawo da shi wanda ba za a iya dawo da shi ba - lalacewa, ɓatattun sassa, ko yanayin da ba za a iya sayar da shi ba. Rasa 25% akan abu $120 shine $30 a kowace riba.

Menene rabon zaizayar ƙasa?

Ribar da ta lalace ta raba da riba duk oda za su samu ba tare da dawowa ba. A ƙimar misali shine $33,480 ÷ $135,000 = 24.80%.

Shin wannan jigilar kaya sau biyu ne?

Sai dai idan adadin riba-kowace-oda ya riga ya cire jigilar kaya. Idan ya yi, saita jigilar kaya zuwa $0 ko daidaita adadin riba - bayanin kula na FAQ da girman yana nuna wannan.

Yaya batun dawo da kayayyaki da aka sake sayarwa?

Ba a yi masa ƙira ba: dawowar da aka sake sayarwa tana dawo da mafi yawan ƙimar samfurin, don haka kashi na asarar waɗannan na'urori ya kamata ya kasance kusan sifili. Yi amfani da ƙimar asarar da aka haɗa a duk ribar da aka samu.

Ta yaya zan rage zaizayar ƙasa?

Levers guda uku sune ƙimar dawowa, farashin jigilar kaya na baya, da ƙimar asarar samfur. Teburin yanayi yana nuna nawa kowanne ma'aunin ƙimar dawowa yake kashewa a cikin girman ku.
