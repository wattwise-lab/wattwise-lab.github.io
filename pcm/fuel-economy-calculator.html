All the fuel-use figures

MeasureValue wey e get

US MPG dey use US gallons (3.785411784 L); UK MPG dey use imperial gallons (4.54609 L). For the same car, the UK figure go always higher.

Fuel wey trips go need at this rate

The bars dey use the 600 km trip as their scale. Fuel for trip = rate (L/100km) × trip distance ÷ 100.

Formula

L/100 km = litres ÷ kilometres × 100. To get US MPG, change kilometres and litres to miles and US gallons: MPG (US) = (km ÷ 1.609344) ÷ (L ÷ 3.785411784). For UK MPG, use 4.54609 L for each gallon instead. Km/L = kilometres ÷ litres.

ConstantValue wey e get
1 mile1.609344 km
1 US gallon3.785411784 L
1 UK (imperial) gallon4.54609 L

Example wey show the calculation

600 km with 45 L: 45 ÷ 600 × 100 = 7.5 L/100km; 600 ÷ 45 = 13.333333 km/L. Miles = 600 ÷ 1.609344 = 372.822715; US gallons = 45 ÷ 3.785411784 = 11.887742, so US MPG = 372.822715 ÷ 11.887742 = 31.361944. UK gallons = 45 ÷ 4.54609 = 9.898616, so UK MPG = 372.822715 ÷ 9.898616 = 37.664125.

Where e fit work and limits

Single tank-to-tank figure noisy: fill level, route, weather, load, and driving style all move am. Use fuel added over comparable full-tank interval, as source tool advised, and average several tanks for reliable figure. Manufacturer figures measured on test cycles and usually beat real-world results. Display values rounded to 6 decimal places.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Which L/100km figure we fit call good?

As rough guide: below 6 dey economical, 6–9 na middle range for family petrol car, and above 9 dey use plenty fuel. You no fit compare diesels, hybrids and electric cars directly with this same scale.

Why UK MPG dey higher than US MPG?

Na the same car, but the gallon bigger: imperial gallon (4.54609 L) hold about 20% more fuel than US gallon (3.785411784 L). So the same distance per litre go show a higher UK MPG.

How I fit change L/100km to MPG?

US MPG = 235.214583 ÷ L/100km. For 7.5 L/100km, e go give 31.361944, the same as the worked example.

I suppose measure one tank or several tanks?

Use several tanks. How full the pump fill one tank fit change the figure. Take the average of at least three fill-ups wey you fit compare.

This one include fuel cost?

No. This page only dey measure fuel use. The fuel-cost calculator for the link below go turn that fuel use into money.
