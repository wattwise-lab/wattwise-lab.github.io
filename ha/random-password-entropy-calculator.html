Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da H = L×log₂(A). L shine tsawon kalmar sirri da aka yi amfani da ita daban-daban, A shine girman haruffa, kuma H entropy ne a cikin bits.

Amfani da iyakoki
