Sa'o'in Mutum a kowace shekara—

Sa'o'in Mutum = mutane × mintuna ÷ 60, wanda aka auna ta hanyar tarurruka a mako da makonnin aiki.

Ƙa’ida

Darajar lokacin shekara = mutane × kudin sa'a × mintunan taro ÷ 60 × tarurrukan mako-mako × makonnin aiki. Kudin sa'a ga kowane taro = mutane × kudin sa'a ga kowane lokaci × mintuna ÷ 60. Kudin sa'a ga kowane taro = kudin sa'a ga kowane taro × tarurrukan mako-mako.

Misalin aiki

9 mutane a $180/awa na mintuna 60: farashi a kowace taro = 9 × 180 × 60 ÷ 60 = $1,620. A tarurrukan 3 a kowane mako, darajar lokacin shekara shine $4,860 a kowane mako, kuma sama da makonnin aiki na 48 shine $1,620 × 3 × 48 = $233,280, ko kuma sa'o'in mutum 1,296 a kowace shekara. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake maimaita wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan kimantawa ce ta kuɗi bisa ga matsakaicin kuɗin sa'a da kuka shigar, ba lallai bane kuɗin kuɗi da za a iya gujewa: ana biyan ma'aikatan albashi ko sun halarta ko ba su halarta ba. Lokacin shiri yana cikin haɗin ne kawai idan kun ƙara shi zuwa tsawon lokacin taron. Ba a haɗa da kuɗin ɗaki, kayan aiki, da tafiya ba. Adadin sa'a yakamata ya zama cikakken kuɗin da aka cika (albashi da farashi) idan sakamakon yana nufin wakiltar jimlar kuɗin ma'aikata.

FAQ

Shin wannan tanadin kuɗi ne idan na soke taron?

Ba lallai ba ne. Darajar lokacin da ma'aikata suka rage shi ne. Ana adana kuɗi ne kawai idan za a biya wannan lokacin ƙarin lokaci ko lokacin ɗan kwangila.

Shin kuɗin sa'a ya kamata ya haɗa da kuɗin da ake kashewa?

Eh, idan kana son jimillar kuɗin ma'aikata. Yi amfani da cikakken kuɗin da aka cika — albashi da fa'idodi, harajin albashi da kuma ƙarin kuɗi — maimakon albashin tushe kawai.

Yaya batun lokacin shiri?

Ƙara shi zuwa tsawon lokacin taron, ko gudanar da lissafi na biyu don shiri sannan ka ƙara sakamakon biyu.

Me yasa 48 makonnin aiki?

Misalin zato ne (52 makonni ƙasa da hutu da hutu). Canza shi don ya dace da kalandarka.

Shin ƙaramin taro yana adana daidai gwargwado?

A cikin wannan samfurin, eh: farashin yana aunawa a layi tare da mintuna, mutane, da mita, don haka rabi ɗaya daga cikinsu ya raba farashin.
