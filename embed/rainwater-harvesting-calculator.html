Formula

Use V = Are. A is roof catchment area in m², r is rainfall in mm, and e is collection efficiency as a fraction. V is potential collected litres; actual storage is capped by entered spare tank capacity.

Scope and limitations

Estimates collected water from one rainfall event using your collection efficiency and remaining tank space. Does not forecast rainfall or assess water quality and drinking safety.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
