Formula

Use y = Σ yᵢ ∏ⱼ≠ᵢ (x−xⱼ)/(xᵢ−xⱼ) over the three entered points (xᵢ,yᵢ). x is the target coordinate. The input horizontal coordinates must be distinct; outside them, the polynomial is an extrapolation.
