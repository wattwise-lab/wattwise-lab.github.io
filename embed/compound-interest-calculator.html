What drives the total

Bars are scaled to the future value. Interest is the remainder after principal and contributions: it grows fastest late in the period, when the balance is largest.

Year-end balance

Contributions are added at the end of each month and interest compounds monthly.

Formula

Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12. Future value = principal × (1 + monthly rate)months + contribution × [(1 + monthly rate)months − 1] ÷ monthly rate. With a 0% rate, the future value is simply principal + contributions.

Worked example

Principal $10,000, $500 a month, 6% a year for 10 years (120 months): the principal grows to $18,193.97, the $60,000 of contributions grows to $81,939.67, for a future value of $100,133.64. Interest earned is $30,133.64 on $70,000 put in.

Scope and limitations

The rate is assumed constant for the whole period, contributions are end-of-month and never missed, and taxes, fees, and inflation are not included. Real returns vary year to year, so treat the result as a projection under one fixed assumption, not a promise.

FAQ

Are contributions added at the start or end of the month?

End of the month, matching the source calculator's convention. Start-of-month contributions would earn one extra month of interest each and give a slightly higher total.

What happens at a 0% rate?

The future value is just principal plus all contributions, with zero interest. The calculator handles that case directly instead of dividing by zero.

Does a higher rate or a longer time matter more?

Both compound, but time has a distinctive effect: interest earned late in the period applies to a much larger balance, which is why starting earlier often beats contributing more later.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax, and before fees and inflation. A taxable account keeps less than the headline figure.

Can I model irregular contributions?

This form uses a fixed monthly contribution. For irregular deposits, grow each cash flow over the time remaining until the same end date, then add those future values. Do not add results with different end dates.
