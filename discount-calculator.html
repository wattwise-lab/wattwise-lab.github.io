Formula

Final price = original price × (1 − discount rate) − additional fixed discount, floored at zero. Amount saved = original price − final price.

Worked example

Original price $100, discount 20%, extra discount $5: final price = $100 × (1 − 0.20) − $5 = $80 − $5 = $75, so you save $25, an effective 25.00% off.

Scope and limitations

Tax, shipping, minimum-spend rules, coupon order, and exclusions may change the checkout total. If a store applies the fixed discount before the percentage discount, the result differs slightly — this calculator applies the percentage first, as stated in the formula.

FAQ

How does the discount calculator work?

Final price = original price × (1 − discount rate) − additional fixed discount, floored at zero. The percentage comes off first, then the fixed amount.

In what order are the discounts applied?

Percentage first, then the fixed discount. Reversing the order changes the result when both are present, so check the store's order if the total differs by a small amount.

Can the final price go below zero?

No. If the discounts exceed the price, the result is floored at $0.00 and the saving is capped at the original price.

Does this include tax?

No. The taxable amount depends on local rules and the type of discount or coupon. It may differ from the discounted amount paid. Confirm the applicable tax base before using the sales tax calculator.

What is the effective discount?

Total saving divided by the original price. With a 20% discount plus $5 off $100, the effective saving is 25%, not 20%.

What about minimum-spend or excluded items?

Not modeled. If the discount only applies above a spend threshold or excludes some items, the checkout total will differ.
