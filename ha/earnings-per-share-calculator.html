Ribar asali ga kowace hannun jari

—

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato; babu farashin rayuwa, ƙa'idodin haraji ko shawarwarin saka hannun jari. Dawowar na iya zama mara kyau kuma ba a tabbatar da ita ba. Babban EPS kawai; ba a tsara hannun jarin da aka narkar, ƙa'idodin rahoto da gyare-gyaren lissafi ba.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da EPS = (E−D)/S. E shine ribar da aka samu, D shine rabon riba da aka fi so, kuma S shine matsakaicin hannun jari na gama gari. Wannan shine ribar asali ga kowane hannun jari.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato; babu farashin rayuwa, ƙa'idodin haraji ko shawarwarin saka hannun jari. Dawowar na iya zama mara kyau kuma ba a tabbatar da ita ba. Babban EPS kawai; ba a tsara hannun jarin da aka narkar, ƙa'idodin rahoto da gyare-gyaren lissafi ba.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
