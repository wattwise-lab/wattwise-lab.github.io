Formula

Use U = Σ(Aᵢ/Pᵢ) and P̄ = (ΣAᵢ+F)/U. Aᵢ is each purchase amount excluding fees, Pᵢ is its unit price, F is total fees, and U is total units bought. P̄ includes fees.
