UnitValue

All seven units describe the same length; the inch and foot factors below are exact definitions.

Formula

Target value = input × source-unit factor ÷ target-unit factor, where factors are metres per unit.

UnitMetres per unit
Metre (m)1
Kilometre (km)1,000
Centimetre (cm)0.01
Millimetre (mm)0.001
Inch (in)0.0254
Foot (ft)0.3048
Mile (mi)1,609.344

The inch is defined as exactly 0.0254 m, so the foot (12 inches) and the international mile (5,280 feet) are exact too.

Worked example

1 m ÷ 0.3048 m/ft = 3.280840 ft, and 1 m ÷ 0.0254 m/in = 39.370079 in. A 5 km run is 5,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = 3.106856 miles.

Scope and limitations

Negative lengths are rejected as invalid. Display values are rounded to 6 decimal places; the underlying calculation is not rounded. This page uses the international mile; the old US survey mile (about 2 parts per million longer) is not used.

FAQ

How many feet are in a metre?

3.28083989501 ft, shown as 3.280840 after display rounding.

Is the inch exact?

Yes: since 1959 the international inch is defined as exactly 25.4 mm.

What about yards?

Not listed separately: 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m. Enter feet and divide by 3, or enter metres and use the foot result.

Why does the mile row show so many decimals for 1 m?

Because 1 m is a tiny fraction of a mile (0.000621 mi); 6 decimal places keep it visible instead of rounding to zero.

Does this cover area or volume?

No — length only. Area and volume have their own converters in the related tools below.
