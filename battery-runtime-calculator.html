RUNTIME REALITY CHECK

Average load is the number that controls the answer.

A refrigerator does not draw its rated power every minute. A central air conditioner can cycle, and a well pump may run only briefly. The best estimate comes from a monitored average load or a carefully built appliance schedule.

Capacity is not the same as deliverable energy

A battery can hold back a reserve to protect itself or keep emergency capacity available. Its inverter also loses some energy while converting battery DC power to household AC power. This tool deducts both before calculating time.

What this calculator cannot certify

It does not verify breaker sizes, transfer equipment, surge capability, generator integration or installation code. Use the result to plan questions, then confirm equipment and wiring with the manufacturer and a qualified electrician.
