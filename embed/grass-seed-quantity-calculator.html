Formula

Use M = Ar(1+w). A is area in m², r is labelled sowing rate in g/m², and w is the waste fraction. M is grams before conversion to kilograms; packs are rounded up using entered pack mass.

Scope and limitations

Uses the product seed rate and pack mass you enter, with an optional waste allowance. Does not choose a seed variety, sowing season or agronomic application rate.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
