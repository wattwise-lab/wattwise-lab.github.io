How the shortfall dey grow with time at your nightly rate

Dem scale di bars reach di 30-day value. Dem dey grow in straight proportion because di nightly shortfall no change.

Formula

Nightly shortfall = max(target hours per night − actual average hours, 0). Sleep debt = nightly shortfall × days. If actual sleep reach or pass di target, di shortfall na zero and no debt dey gather for dis calculation.

Example wey show the calculation

Target na 8 hours, actual average na 6.5 hours, for 14 days: nightly shortfall = 8 − 6.5 = 1.5 hours. Debt = 1.5 × 14 = 21 hours — na 10.5 hours per week, or 2.63 nights of 8-hour sleep. Use Reset example above to get dis same result.

Where e fit work and limits

Dis page only dey calculate hours: e dey multiply di same nightly shortfall by di number of days. E no dey measure sleep quality, handle different nights one by one (di actual figure na average), or calculate recovery. Extra sleep no simply dey repay missing hours hour for hour, and dis page no claim say e dey. Dis no be diagnosis, treatment plan or clinical category, and e no dey assess any sleep condition.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin sleep debt mean here?

Na di difference wey gather between your nightly target and actual average sleep, multiplied by di number of days. Na only hour calculation; no be medical measurement.

If I sleep extra one night, the debt go reduce?

For dis calculation, yes: if di actual average increase, di nightly shortfall go reduce. For real life, recovery no be simple hour-for-hour repayment. Na one limit of dis model be dat.

Wetin if I sleep pass my target?

Dem go hold di shortfall at zero, so no debt go gather. Di page no dey store extra sleep as credit.

Why actual sleep na average?

Because di amount wey person sleep dey change each night. Enter di average for di whole period so di multiplication go represent all di days, instead of only one night.

Una dey store my data?

No. This calculation dey run locally inside your browser; values wey you enter no dey reach any CalcMintly database.

Big number mean say I get sleep disorder?

No. Dis page no fit assess any condition. Plenty hours for di result only mean plenty hours for di calculation.
