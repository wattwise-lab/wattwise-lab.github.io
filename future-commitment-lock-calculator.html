Formula

Use S = max(a,e,c+n). a is start month, e is earliest stop month, c is cancellation-request month, and n is notice months. If S is within the item's end month, charge active months before it and the exit fee in the stop month; otherwise the original end remains.

Scope and limitations

Month 1 is your first planning month. Costs and time are uniform within each active month; cancellation takes effect at the start of the stop month. Comparisons include only the horizon, and each cancels one item. Contract terms must be checked separately.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
