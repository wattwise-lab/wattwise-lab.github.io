Ribar Mai Sayarwa da Kasafin Talla

Kwatanta farashi, jigila kyauta da rangwame. Ana iya gyara bayanai, lissafi a bayyane.

Ƙara zuwa wurin aiki
Ribar gudummawa / oda—Bayan talla da maidowar da ake tsammani, kafin tabbatattun kuɗaɗe da harajin samun kuɗi.
Iyakacin talla tare da kiyaye manufar riba—Ana raba tabbatattun kuɗaɗe kan odar da ake tsammani, sannan a bar ribar da ake so ga kowace oda.
Kimanta ribar wata—Gudummawa × odar da ake tsammani − tabbatattun kuɗaɗe. Ba hasashen tallace-tallace ba ne.

Gyara bayanan da aka nuna. Ba a bar tsohon sakamako ba.

Sunan yanayi da saitin kuɗi

Kuɗi iri ɗaya, babu canjin kuɗaɗe. Harajin da aka karɓa baya cikin kuɗin shiga; ba a lissafa harajin samun kuɗi.

1 · Kaya da isarwa

2 · Biyan kuɗi da maidowa

Tushen caji = kayan bayan rangwame + jigilar abokin ciniki + harajin da aka karɓa. Shigar da kason kwangilarka.

Bayanin maidowa da sauran kashe kuɗi

Lissafi ne ga kowane odar asali da aka biya. Ƙimar maidowa ita ce rabon odar da ake mayar wa kuɗi; kason maidowa da dawo da ƙimar kaya ya shafi kowace irin odar.

Maidowar kaya, jigila, ƙimar kuɗin kayan da aka dawo da su da cajin biya da aka dawo zato ne masu zaman kansu ga odar maidowa. Harajin da aka karɓa ba kuɗin shiga ba ne ko asarar maidowa. Ƙimar da aka dawo ba tsabar kuɗi ba ce; duba a hankali haɗin maidowar wani sashe da ƙimar dawowa mai yawa.

Rikodin kason caji da zato
Rubuta kwangila, tayin farashi ko tushen kimantawa; ba bayanan abokin ciniki, lambar kati ko bayanan odar sirri ba.

3 · Talla da manufofi

Manufar ribar wata da ta kowace oda ana lissafa su dabam, ba a cire su tare ba.

Lissafa baya daga manufa

Odar da ake bukata don manufar wata

—

An ɗauka kuɗin kowane raka'a, talla da ƙimar maidowa ba su canza yayin ƙarin oda. Ba hasashen buƙata ba ne.

Mafi ƙarancin farashin jeri don manufar da aka zaɓa

—

Sauran bayanai ba su canza; ana zagaye farashi sama zuwa ƙaramin rukunin kuɗin. Ba a sake lissafa harajin da aka karɓa.

Wane canji ne ke sauya riba?

Abu ɗaya ne yake canzawa a kowane lokaci. Ba hasashen yiwuwa ko buƙata ba ne.

Kwatanta yanayi gefe da gefe

Zaton da ka shigar kawai ake kwatantawa. Ba a ɗauka jigila kyauta ko rangwame zai ƙara saye. Riba mafi yawa a samfurin ba tabbatacciyar shawarar kasuwanci ba ce.

Jigila kyauta ko rangwame yana bukatar ƙarin oda nawa?

Zaɓi yanayin tushe, ka lissafa odar da kowane zaɓi yake bukata don daidaita ribar watansa. Oda sun haɗa da waɗanda za a mayar wa kuɗi daga baya. Kuɗin kowane raka'a, talla da kason maidowa suna nan; ba hasashen sakamakon tayin rangwame ba ne.

Farashi × oda: gwada zato biyun

Farashi 90%, 100% da 110% na shigarwarka; adadi 80%, 100% da 120%, an zagaye zuwa cikakkun oda. Kowane akwati yana nuna ribar wata. Farashi da adadi suna canzawa daban; rangwame ba yana tabbatar da ƙarin buƙata ba. Ba a sake lissafa harajin da aka karɓa.

Saka wannan kalkuleta a cikin maƙala

Harshe da kuɗi kaɗai ake haɗawa. Ba a raba shirye-shiryenka ko ƙimomin da ka shigar.

Duba misalin ƙaramin kayan aiki
Duba rabe-raben kowace oda da ƙa'idoji

Gudummawa = kuɗin kayan bayan rangwame + jigilar abokin ciniki − maidowar kuɗin shiga da ake tsammani − kuɗin kaya + ƙimar kayan da aka dawo da ita − marufi − tura kaya − sauran kuɗaɗe masu canzawa − cajin biya + cajin da aka dawo − kula da dawowar da ake tsammani − talla. Ribar wata = gudummawa × oda − tabbatattun kuɗaɗe. Oda da ake bukata = zagaye sama [(tabbatattun kuɗaɗe + manufar ribar wata) ÷ gudummawa].

Iyaka: kimantawar shiri ga kaya ɗaya ko kwando tsayayye, ba rahoton lissafin kuɗi, hasashen gudanawar kuɗi ko takardar haraji ba. Ba a saka harajin samun kuɗi, kwastam, ƙalubalantar biya, biyan rajista da asarar canjin kuɗi kai tsaye. Ƙara kuɗaɗen da ba za a dawo da su ba cikin sauran kuɗaɗe masu canzawa ko tabbatattu ba tare da ƙirgawa sau biyu ba. Ba a zagaye caji ga kowace ma'amala; zagayen takardar biya na iya bambanta.
