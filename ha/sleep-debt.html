Ƙarar bashi akan lokaci, akan ƙimar dare

Ana auna sanduna zuwa ƙimar 30-rana kuma suna girma a layi saboda raguwar dare tana dawwama.

Ƙa’ida

Ragewar dare = matsakaicin (sa'o'in dare da aka yi niyya - matsakaicin sa'o'i na gaske, 0). Bashin barci = ƙarancin dare × kwanaki. Idan ainihin barci ya cika ko ya wuce abin da aka yi niyya, raguwar sifili ce kuma babu wani bashi da ya taru a cikin wannan lissafin.

Misalin aiki

Sa'o'in da aka yi niyya 8, matsakaicin sa'o'i na gaske 6.5, kwanaki 14: ƙarancin dare = 8 − 6.5 = awanni 1.5. Bashi = 1.5 × 14 = awanni 21 — awanni 10.5 a kowane mako, ko dare 2.63 na barci 8-awa. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan shafin yana yin lissafin awa kawai: yana ninka raguwar dare da rana da yawa da kwanaki da yawa. Ba ya auna ingancin barci, yana lissafin dare daban-daban (ainihin adadi matsakaici ne), ko kuma murmurewa ta samfuri - ba a biyan bashin sa'o'i awa-awa ta hanyar ƙarin barci a kowace hanya mai sauƙi, kuma wannan shafin bai yi iƙirarin akasin haka ba. Ba ganewar asali ba ne, tsarin magani, ko nau'in asibiti, kuma ba ya kimanta kowace yanayin barci.

FAQ

Menene bashin barci a nan?

Bambancin da aka tara tsakanin abin da ake nufi da dare da ainihin barci, wanda aka ninka da adadin kwanaki. Babu wani abu kuma: lissafin sa'a ne, ba ma'aunin likita ba.

Idan na yi barci fiye da dare ɗaya, shin bashin yana raguwa?

A cikin wannan lissafi, eh - haɓaka matsakaicin ainihin yana rage ƙarancin dare. A zahiri, murmurewa ba biyan sa'a-awa ba ne mai sauƙi, wanda shine iyaka ɗaya ta samfurin.

Me zai faru idan na yi barci fiye da abin da nake nufi?

Sannan ƙarancin ya ragu zuwa sifili kuma babu wani bashi da ya taru. Shafin bai tara ƙarin kuɗi ba.

Me yasa bacci na gaske matsakaici ne?

Domin barcin dare ya bambanta. Shigar da matsakaicin a tsawon lokacin don haka ninkawa yana nuna lokacin gaba ɗaya maimakon dare ɗaya.

An adana bayanai na?

A'a. Wannan lissafin yana aiki a cikin gida a cikin burauzarka kuma ƙimar da ka shigar ba a aika ta zuwa ga bayanan CalcMintly ba.

Shin adadi mai yawa yana nufin ina da matsalar barci?

A'a. Wannan shafin ba zai iya tantance kowace yanayi ba; jimlar sa'a mai yawa jimlar sa'a ce kawai.
