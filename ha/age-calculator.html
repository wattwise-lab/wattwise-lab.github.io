Ƙa’ida

Shekaru = ranar tunawa − ranar haihuwa, an kimanta ta a matsayin bambancin kalanda: an kammala shekaru da farko, sannan watanni da suka cika, sannan sauran kwanakin. Kwanakin da suka rayu sune ƙidayar rana tsakanin ranakun biyu. Kwanaki har zuwa ranar haihuwa ta gaba shine ƙidayar rana daga ranar tunawa zuwa faruwar wata da rana ta haihuwa ta gaba.

Misalin aiki

An haife 10 Mayu 1990, a ranar da aka ambata 28 Oktoba 2026: 36 shekaru da suka cika (zuwa 10 Mayu 2026), da kuma watanni da suka cika 5 (zuwa 10 Oktoba 2026), da kuma kwanaki 18 = shekaru 36, watanni 5, kwanaki 18. Kwanaki da suka rayu = kwanaki 13,320. Ranar haihuwa ta gaba ita ce 10 Mayu 2027, wanda shine kwanaki 194 bayan 28 Oktoba 2026. Saita ranar da aka ambata zuwa 2026-10-28 don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Duk ranakun ranakun kalanda ne na gida; lokutan ranaku da yankunan lokaci ba a yin watsi da su, don haka ranar haihuwa daga baya a yau har yanzu tana ƙidaya kamar yadda aka kai a ranar. Ana kula da shekarun tsalle-tsalle ta kalanda da kanta: mutumin da aka haifa a ranar 29 Fabrairu yana da shekaru da suka cika, kuma a cikin shekarar da ba ta tsalle ba, wannan shafin yana ɗaukar 28 Fabrairu a matsayin ranar haihuwa don ƙidayar ranar haihuwa ta gaba (an bayyana a nan don haka al'adar ta bayyana, ba a ɗauka ba). Idan ranar haihuwa ta kasance bayan ranar tunawa, babu wani shekaru da aka nuna.

Yana amfani da kalandar Gregorian mai saurin aiki da lissafi na kwanan wata kawai, ba tare da la'akari da lokacin adana hasken rana ba. Cikakkun shekaru da watanni suna amfani da ranar asali ta wata, an matse su zuwa ranar ƙarshe mai inganci idan ya cancanta. Sauran tarurrukan cika shekaru na iya bambanta; wannan ba ƙayyadadden shekaru na shari'a bane.

FAQ

Shin shekarun daidai ne a cikin shekaru, watanni da kwanaki?

Eh, a matsayin shekarun kalanda: shekaru da aka kammala, sannan watanni da aka kammala, sannan kwanaki. Ba jimlar kwanaki da aka raba da 365 ba ne, wanda zai ratsa cikin shekarun tsalle.

Yaya ake tafiyar da shekarun tsalle-tsalle?

A bisa ga kalandar gaske. Adadin rana tsakanin ranaku biyu ya haɗa da 29 Fabrairu ta atomatik a cikin shekarun tsalle, kuma rarraba shekara/wata yana bin watannin kalanda masu tsayi daban-daban.

Yaya batun ranar haihuwar 29 ta watan Fabrairu?

Shekaru har yanzu suna ci gaba da ƙaruwa ta hanyar shekarun kalanda da aka kammala. Don ƙidayar ranar haihuwa ta gaba a cikin shekarar da ba ta da tsalle, wannan shafin yana amfani da 28 Fabrairu kuma yana faɗi haka a cikin sashin ma'auni.

Shin lokacin rana yana da muhimmanci?

A'a. Kwanakin kalanda ne kawai ake kwatantawa, don haka yankunan lokaci da lokutan haihuwa ba sa canza sakamakon.

Me zai faru idan ranar haihuwa ta zo nan gaba?

Ba a nuna sakamako ba; shafin yana buƙatar ranar haihuwa a ranar ko kafin ranar da aka ambata.
