Formula

Use K = Σ[k(k+1)CFₖ/(1+y/m)^(k+2)]/(Pm²). CFₖ is the payment in coupon period k, P is bond price, y is the nominal annual yield fraction, and m is payments per year. K estimates local price curvature with respect to yield.
