Formula

For r = 0, cut length is c = h+t; for r > 0, use c = r ceil((h+t)/r). Rolls are N = ceil[ceil(P/w)/floor(L/c)]. h is wall height, t is trim, r is pattern repeat, P is wall perimeter, w is roll width, and L is roll length, all in m. ceil rounds up and floor rounds down.
