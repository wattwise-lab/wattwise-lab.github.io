Ƙidaya mara kyau yana nufin cewa abin da aka yi niyya ya wuce; sannan shafin ya ba da rahoton kwanaki nawa da suka gabata.

Ƙa’ida

Kwanaki da suka rage = ranar kalandar gida da aka yi niyya − daga ranar kalandar gida. Makonni = makonni gaba ɗaya a cikin wannan ƙidayar, tare da sauran kwanakin da aka nuna daban.

Misalin aiki

Daga 28 Oktoba 2026 zuwa manufa 1 Janairu 2027: kwanaki 65, wanda shine makonni 9 da kwanaki 2. 1 Janairu 2027 Juma'a ce. Saita ranar daga zuwa 2026-10-28 don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon; a bar shi babu komai daga yau.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan ƙidayar rana ce ta kalanda, ba ƙidayar sa'o'i da mintuna kai tsaye ba, kuma ya dogara da ranar gida ta na'urar lokacin da aka bar ranar farawa babu komai. Ana sarrafa shekarun tsalle ta kalanda: ƙidayar tsakanin ranakun biyu ta atomatik ta haɗa da 29 Fabrairu a cikin shekarun tsalle. Ana yin watsi da lokutan rana da yankuna na lokaci; ana ɗaukar ranakun biyu a matsayin kwanakin kalanda na gida gaba ɗaya.

Yana amfani da kalandar Gregorian mai saurin aiki da lissafi na kwanan wata kawai, ba tare da la'akari da lokacin adana hasken rana ba. Cikakkun shekaru da watanni suna amfani da ranar asali ta wata, an matse su zuwa ranar ƙarshe mai inganci idan ya cancanta. Sauran tarurrukan cika shekaru na iya bambanta; wannan ba ƙayyadadden shekaru na shari'a bane.

FAQ

Shin wannan ƙidayar kai tsaye ce?

A'a. Yana ƙirga kwanakin kalanda gaba ɗaya tsakanin ranakun biyu. Ba ya yin ƙasa a cikin awanni, mintuna ko daƙiƙa.

Idan ranar da aka nufa ta wuce fa?

Ƙidayar ba ta da kyau kuma shafin ya ba da rahotonta kamar yadda kwanaki suka gabata - misali, −10 kwanaki ana nuna su a matsayin '10 kwanaki da suka wuce'.

Me 'daga yau' ke amfani da shi?

Ranar gida ta na'urarka. Idan kana buƙatar sakamako mai maimaitawa, shigar da ranar farawa a sarari maimakon barin ta babu komai.

Shin yana kula da shekarun tsalle?

Eh. Domin yana cire ainihin kwanakin kalanda, ana ƙirga 29 Fabrairu a cikin tazara kamar kowace rana.

Me yasa makonni da kwanaki, ba watanni ba?

Watanni suna da tsayi daban-daban, don haka ƙidayar rana tana canzawa zuwa makonni daidai amma zuwa watanni kawai kimanin. Makonni da kwanaki daidai ne; ba a nuna watanni ba.
