Biyan kuɗi na wata-wata

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato da aka shigar; babu farashi mai rai, ƙa'idodin haraji ko tabbacin dawowa.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da A = [P−B/(1+r)^n]/a(r,n). P shine babban abu, B shine ma'aunin balan-balan, r shine rabon riba na wata-wata, kuma n shine watanni. a(r,n) yana taƙaita abubuwan da ke haifar da rangwame na ƙarshen wata; ana biyan balan-balan a ƙarshe.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato da aka shigar; babu farashi mai rai, ƙa'idodin haraji ko tabbacin dawowa.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
