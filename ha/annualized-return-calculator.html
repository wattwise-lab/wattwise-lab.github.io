Fara vs ƙarshe, a takaice

An auna sanduna zuwa mafi girma daga cikin ƙimomin biyu. Matsakaicin girma ɗaya yana ɓoye hanyar: hawa mai tsayi da kuma hawa mai canzawa na iya raba adadi iri ɗaya na shekara-shekara.

Hanyar ci gaba a wannan ƙimar shekara-shekara

Shekaradarajar

Hanyar tana amfani da ƙimar shekara-shekara daidai gwargwado kowace shekara. Ra'ayi ne mai santsi, ba rikodin abin da ya faru kowace shekara ba.

Ƙa’ida

Dawowar shekara-shekara = (ƙimar ƙarshe ÷ ƙimar farko)1 ÷ shekaru − 1. Jimlar dawowa = (ƙimar ƙarshe − ƙimar farko) ÷ ƙimar farko.

Misalin aiki

Ƙimar farko $10,000 tana girma zuwa $15,000 sama da shekaru 4: jimillar ribar $5,000, jimillar ribar 50.00%, da kuma ribar da aka samu a kowace shekara (15,000 ÷ 10,000)1/4 − 1 = 10.67%. Wannan shine daidaitaccen ƙimar shekara-shekara wanda zai kai $10,000 zuwa $15,000 a cikin shekaru 4.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan wani tsari ne mai santsi, mai kama da baya: yana ɗaukar cewa ci gaban yana haɗuwa daidai gwargwado kuma bai faɗi komai game da canjin yanayi, kuɗaɗe, haraji, hauhawar farashi, ko kuɗin da aka ƙara ko aka cire a lokacin ba. Ba za a iya kwatanta shi kai tsaye da matsakaicin riba mai sauƙi a kowace shekara ba, kuma ba hasashen ba ne.

FAQ

Shin ribar da aka samu a kowace shekara iri ɗaya ce da ribar da aka samu a kowace shekara?

A'a. Matsakaicin riba mai sauƙi na shekara-shekara ba ya yin la'akari da haɗakar riba. Adadin shekara-shekara (haɗakar riba) shine ƙimar da ta tsaya cak wanda ke sake haifar da jimlar ƙaruwa a tsawon lokacin.

Me zai faru idan ƙimar ƙarshe ta yi ƙasa da ƙimar farko?

Sakamakon ya zama mara kyau, wanda hakan daidai ne: ƙimar da aka ƙididdige ta kowace shekara tana nufin ƙimar ta ragu a daidai wannan saurin shekara.

Zan iya amfani da shi na ɗan lokaci fiye da shekara guda?

Eh, da shekaru a matsayin ƙalilan (misali 0.5 na tsawon watanni shida), amma yin shekara-shekara na ɗan gajeren lokaci yana ƙara girman motsi na ɗan gajeren lokaci, don haka a yi taka tsantsan da sakamakon.

Shin ya haɗa da rabon riba ko kuɗaɗen shiga?

Sai dai idan an riga an nuna su a cikin ƙimar ƙarshe da ka shigar. Raba hannun jari da aka sake sakawa yana ƙara ƙimar ƙarshe; kuɗin da aka biya yana rage shi.

Me yasa ya yi ƙasa da jimillar ribar da aka raba da shekaru?

Domin haɗakarwa tana yin wani ɓangare na aikin: 50% sama da shekaru 4 yana haɗuwa 10.67%, ba 12.50% a shekara ba, tunda ci gaban kowace shekara yana samun ci gaba a cikin shekaru masu zuwa.
