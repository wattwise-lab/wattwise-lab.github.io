Rushewa

Adadin

Kowace na'ura da aka sayar bayan an gama cin nasara tana ƙara ragin gudummawar guda ɗaya ga riba, a waɗannan farashi da farashi.

A kallo ɗaya

A lokacin hutun ko da sandunan biyu daidai suke bisa ga ma'anarsu: jimlar gudummawar ta shafi daidai gwargwado na kuɗaɗen da aka ƙayyade.

Daidaito a farashi daban-daban

Farashin raka'aRukunin daidaitawa

Ana riƙe da farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi da farashi mai canzawa a ƙimar da ke sama; farashin kawai yana canzawa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Raka'o'in daidaitawa = farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi ÷ (farashin naúra − farashin mai canzawa naúra). Kudaden shiga na daidaitawa = raka'o'in daidaitawa × farashin naúra. Ribar gudummawa ga kowace naúra = farashin naúra − farashin mai canzawa naúra.

Misalin aiki

Kuɗaɗen da aka gyara $50,000, farashi $200, farashin da ke canzawa $120: iyaka ta gudummawar ita ce $200 − $120 = $80 kowace raka'a, don haka raka'a masu daidaitawa = $50,000 ÷ $80 = 625 raka'a, da kuma kuɗin shiga masu daidaitawa = 625 × $200 = $125,000.

Amfani da iyakoki

Farashin dole ne ya wuce farashin da ba ya canzawa, ko kuma karyewar farashi ba za ta taɓa zuwa ba. Haɗin samfura, haraji, farashin da suka tashi a kan iyakokin iya aiki, rangwamen girma, da canjin farashi ba a cire su ba - wannan samfurin samfuri ɗaya ne, mai farashi mai ɗorewa.

FAQ

Ta yaya kalkuleta mai karya yake aiki?

Raka'o'in daidaitawa = farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi ÷ (farashin naúrar − farashin mai canzawa naúrar). Yana nemo adadin tallace-tallace inda jimlar gudummawar ta biya daidai farashin da aka ƙayyade.

Me zai faru idan farashin ya ƙasa da farashin da aka canza?

Sannan kowace siyarwa tana asarar kuɗi kuma babu wani adadin da ya rage - siyarwar da ta fi yawa tana ƙara asara. Farashin dole ne ya biya farashin da ya dace kafin a iya dawo da farashi mai araha.

Menene ribar gudummawa?

Farashi a rage farashin da aka canza a kowace naúrar: adadin da kowace siyarwa ke bayarwa don biyan kuɗi da aka ƙayyade sannan a sami riba. Ga $80 kowace naúrar a ƙimar misali.

Shin sayar da abin da ya wuce yana nufin samun riba sosai?

A waɗannan zato, eh ga kowane ƙarin sashi - amma sai dai sai an rage farashi, iyakokin iya aiki, ko rage farashi. Kamfanoni na gaske ba kasafai suke riƙe da dukkan abubuwan guda uku ba.

An haɗa da haraji?

A'a. Ka ɗauki kuɗin shiga na ɗan lokaci a matsayin kuɗin shiga kafin haraji kuma ka yi amfani da tsarin harajinka daban.

Shin wannan zai iya yin aiki da samfura iri-iri?

A'a. Tare da haɗin samfura, kuna buƙatar matsakaicin rabon gudummawa. Wannan kalkuleta yana kwaikwayon samfur ɗaya akan farashi ɗaya.
