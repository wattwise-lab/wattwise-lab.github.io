COMPARISON NOTES

This is a fuel-cost comparison, not total cost of ownership.

The calculator measures energy used to drive the same distance. It does not include vehicle purchase price, depreciation, insurance, maintenance, charger installation or registration fees.

Use kWh per 100 miles

The U.S. Department of Energy explains EV fuel cost using electricity price and vehicle energy consumed per 100 miles. Charging loss is added here because the energy drawn from the wall is higher than the energy stored in the battery.

Home rate versus public charging

Use the rate from the time you normally charge. Some utilities offer off-peak EV pricing. Public stations may charge by energy, time, session or subscription and usually should be modeled separately.
