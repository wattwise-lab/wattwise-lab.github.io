Formula

Use b = Σ(xᵢ−x̄)(yᵢ−ȳ)/Σ(xᵢ−x̄)² and a = ȳ−bx̄. xᵢ and yᵢ are paired observations, bars denote means, b is slope, and a is intercept. Prediction at the entered target is a+bx.
