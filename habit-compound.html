Formula

Total practice hours = daily minutes ÷ 60 × planned days × completion%. Equivalent workdays = total practice hours ÷ workday length.

Worked example

30 minutes per day, 365 planned days, 80% completion, 8-hour workday: scheduled hours = 30 ÷ 60 × 365 = 182.5 hours; completed hours = 182.5 × 0.80 = 146 hours; missed hours = 182.5 − 146 = 36.5 hours; equivalent workdays = 146.00 ÷ 8 = 18.25 days. These are example inputs — completion is your own estimate, not a measured rate.

Scope and limitations

Equivalent workdays divide hours by the entered day length; they are a scale comparison, not a claim about output. Time accumulated does not establish learning quality, mastery or compound performance gains: an hour of focused practice and an hour of distracted repetition count the same here. Completion is treated as a flat rate across the whole period, so streaks, breaks and improving consistency are not modelled.

FAQ

How is this calculated?

Total practice hours = daily minutes ÷ 60 × planned days × completion%. Equivalent workdays = total practice hours ÷ workday length.

What does the completion rate mean?

The share of planned days you actually do the habit, averaged over the whole period. 80% over 365 days means about 292 days completed, not any particular pattern of streaks and breaks.

Does more hours mean better results?

Not necessarily. Time accumulated does not establish learning quality, mastery or compound performance gains — how you practise matters as much as how long.

What are equivalent workdays?

A scale comparison: total hours divided by the workday length you enter. 146 hours is 18.25 eight-hour days, which is easier to picture than a raw hour count.

What if my habit is not daily?

Convert it to a daily average first: for example, 3 sessions of 60 minutes a week averages about 25.7 minutes per day (180 ÷ 7).

Does CalcMintly store my inputs?

No. This calculation runs locally in your browser and the values you enter are not sent to a CalcMintly database.
