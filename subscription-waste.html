Formula

Estimate = max(monthly fee × 12 − uses per month × 12 × alternative price, 0) + idle months × monthly fee.

Worked example

Monthly fee 68, 2 uses per month, alternative price 12 per use, 4 idle months: annual fee = 68 × 12 = 816.00; value received = 2 × 12 × 12 = 288.00; base waste = max(816.00 − 288.00, 0) = 528.00; idle penalty = 4 × 68 = 272.00; estimated waste = 528.00 + 272.00 = 800.00. These are example inputs, not prices for any real service.

Scope and limitations

The idle-month term is an additional scenario penalty and may overlap with the annual comparison: idle months are already partly reflected in a low uses-per-month average, so the total can overstate waste if you count the same idle period twice. Do not treat the result as an exact amount recoverable by cancelling — cancellation may lose bundled benefits, and some subscriptions cannot be paused month by month.

FAQ

How is this calculated?

Estimate = max(monthly fee × 12 − uses per month × 12 × alternative price, 0) + idle months × monthly fee.

What is the alternative price?

What one use would cost you without the subscription — for example, a single rental, ticket or pay-as-you-go session. It converts uses into a money value you can compare with the fee.

Why is there a separate idle-month penalty?

To model stretches where you pay but do not use the service at all, such as holidays or off-seasons. It is a scenario input, not a measured fact.

Can the total overstate waste?

Yes. The idle-month term may overlap with the annual comparison, since idle months already lower your average uses. Read the scope note above before acting on the figure.

Does cancelling save the full waste amount?

Not necessarily. You may lose bundled benefits, face rejoining fees, or find the alternative price has changed. The figure is an estimate of waste, not a guaranteed saving.

Does CalcMintly store my inputs?

No. This calculation runs locally in your browser and the values you enter are not sent to a CalcMintly database.
