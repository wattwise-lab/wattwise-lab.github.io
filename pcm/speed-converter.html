UnitValue wey e get

All four units na di same speed dem describe. Di mile and knot factors below na exact definitions.

Formula

First change di original value to metres per second, then divide by di m/s factor for di unit wey you want: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

UnitMetres per second for each unit
Kilometre per hour (km/h)0.2777777778 (1/3.6)
Metre per second (m/s)1
Mile per hour (mph)0.44704
Knot (kn)0.5144444444 (1852/3600)

1 mph = 0.44704 m/s exactly (from 1 mile = 1,609.344 m). 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = 1,852 m per hour, exactly.

Example wey show the calculation

100 km/h = 100 × (1/3.6) = 27.777778 m/s, and 27.777778 ÷ 0.44704 = 62.137119 mph. Di same speed na 27.777778 ÷ 0.5144444444 = 53.995680 knots.

Where e fit work and limits

Di page no dey accept negative speed because e no dey calculate direction. Only use signed values where you dey track direction yourself. Dem round di displayed values to 6 decimal places; di actual calculation no dey rounded.

Questions wey people dey ask often

100 km/h na how fast for mph?

62.137119 mph — the worked example above dey show every step.

Wetin be knot?

Na one nautical mile per hour. One nautical mile na exactly 1,852 m, so 1 knot = 0.5144444444 m/s. Na standard unit for sea travel and aviation.

Why mph no be round number for m/s?

Because di mile (1,609.344 m) no divide evenly by di hour: 1,609.344 ÷ 3,600 = 0.44704 m/s exactly.

To change km/h to mph, na just multiply by 0.62?

Na approximation: di exact factor na 0.6213711922. If you multiply by 0.62, 100 km/h go give 62 mph. E close, but e low by 0.137 mph.

I fit convert pace (minutes per km)?

No. Pace na di inverse of speed, so e need im own calculation. Dis page only dey change speed units.
