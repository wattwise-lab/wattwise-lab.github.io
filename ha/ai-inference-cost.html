Rushewa

Abudarajar

tokens na wata-wata sune lokutan kira tokens akan kowace kira. Ana amfani da farashi akan kowace miliyan tokens, kamar yadda aka shigar.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sanduna zuwa babban ɓangaren farashi kuma suna nuna ko shigarwa ko fitarwa tokens sun mamaye lissafin.

Kudin wata-wata a cikin adadin kira daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowace layi tana sake amfani da tokens naka a kowace kira da farashi kuma tana bambanta ne kawai da adadin kira, don haka farashi yana daidaita daidai da girma.

Ƙa’ida

Kudin wata-wata = kira × (shigar tokens × farashin shigarwa + fitarwa tokens × farashin fitarwa) ÷ 1,000,000, tare da farashi a cikin USD kowace miliyan tokens. Kudin kowace kira = farashin wata-wata ÷ kira.

Misalin aiki

Shigarwa 2,500 tokens, fitarwa 800 tokens, kira 100,000, farashin shigarwa $5 da farashin fitarwa $20 a kowace miliyan tokens. Kudin shigarwa = 100,000 × 2,500 × 5 ÷ 1,000,000 = $1,250. Kudin fitarwa = 100,000 × 800 × 20 ÷ 1,000,000 = $1,600. Jimilla = $2,850 a kowane wata, ko $0.0285 a kowace kira. Farashin da ke cikin wannan misalin misalai ne kawai - shigar da farashin shirin ku don adadi mai amfani.

Amfani da iyakoki

Duk farashi misalai ne da za a iya gyarawa a cikin USD kowace miliyan tokens, ba ƙimar mai bayarwa na yanzu ba. Kiran sifili yana nufin sifili farashin wata-wata; farashin da aka yi tsammani a kowane kira har yanzu yana bin hasashen ku na token da farashin ku. Ajiyewa, tunani tokens, rangwame, wasu hanyoyi, haraji da iyakokin farashi suna buƙatar shigarwa ko kimantawa daban.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Ana shigar da farashin kowace kira sau tokens sau farashin shigarwa da kuma fitarwa sau tokens sau farashin fitarwa, wanda aka raba da 1,000,000; cewa adadin kuɗin kowace kira sau na ƙidayar kiran wata shine jimlar kowane wata.

Shin farashin da aka riga aka tsara shi ne ainihin farashin mai bayarwa?

A'a. Misalin shigarwa ne. Farashin mai bayarwa yana canzawa kuma ya bambanta dangane da tsari da matakin, don haka shigar da farashin daga tsarin ku ko takardar lissafin ku kafin amfani da sakamakon.

Me yasa farashin fitarwa ya fi tsada fiye da farashin shigarwa?

Farashin fitarwa a kowace token yawanci ya fi farashin shigarwa, kuma dogayen fitarwa suna haɗuwa. Teburin rarrabuwa yana nuna rabuwar don haka za ku iya ganin wanne gefe za ku inganta.

Me aka cire?

An adana ko aka rage farashin tokens, farashin rukuni, sake gwadawa, kayayyakin more rayuwa, da lokacin ma'aikata. Idan waɗannan abubuwa ne masu mahimmanci a gare ku, ƙara su daban maimakon ɗaukar wannan jimlar a matsayin cikakken kuɗin mallakar.

Ta yaya zan kimanta tokens a kowace kira?

Auna samfurin kira na gaske: rikodin adadin shigarwa da fitarwa token, matsakaicin su, da sake duba bayan canje-canjen umarni. Matsakaicin daga ƴan gajerun kiran gwaji yawanci yana rage ƙimar kiran samarwa.

Shin wannan yana hasashen lissafina daidai?

A'a. Kiyasi ne daga matsakaicin ku da farashin ku. Kwatanta shi da ainihin takardar kuɗi na wata ɗaya, sannan ku daidaita matsakaicin token ko farashi har sai samfurin ya bi diddigin gaskiya.
