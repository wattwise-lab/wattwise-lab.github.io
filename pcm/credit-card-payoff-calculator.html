Balance for each year

AfterBalance wey remain

For each month, interest na the opening balance times APR divided by 12. The payment go reduce the balance after that. The last payment no go pass wetin remain.

Formula

Each month: interest = opening balance × APR ÷ 12; ending balance = opening balance + interest − payment. Simulation dey repeat until balance reach zero.

Example wey show the calculation

Balance $10,000 at 18% APR with $300 a month: first month interest na $150.00, and card pay off in 47 months with $3,967.21 of interest, for $13,967.21 total.

Where e fit work and limits

Fixed APR, fixed monthly payment, no new purchases, no fees, no penalty rates, and no minimum-payment rules. Real cards dey vary APR, charge fees, and often set minimum as percentage of balance, so this na fixed-payment estimate, no be card statement forecast.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin if my payment lower pass monthly interest?

The balance go dey grow, and this payment no go clear the card debt. The calculator go flag am: increase your payment above the first month interest.

Why interest high like this compared with loan?

Na because APR high and the balance dey reduce slowly for the beginning. Plenty of the early payments dey cover interest instead of the original debt.

To pay more than minimum dey help?

Yes, and the effect fit big: bigger payments dey reduce the balance faster and shorten how long interest go dey gather.

Wetin about new purchases?

We no include new purchases. Dem go add to the balance and make payoff take longer; this calculator assume say you no buy anything else with the card.

APR na the same as interest rate?

APR na yearly rate used here, including card stated rate. Fees separate and dem no model am.
