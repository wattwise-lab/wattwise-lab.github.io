Rushewa

Abudarajar

Kudin da ake kashewa a kan kayan aiki shine bambanci tsakanin jimlar makamashi da ƙarfin da aka ƙayyade. Idan PUE shine 1.0, farashin da ake kashewa sifili ne.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sandunan zuwa babban adadin kuzari. Suna nuna adadin da PUE ke ƙarawa, ba kwatantawa da wani tsarin ba.

Fitowar hayaki a cikin kundin kira daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowace layi tana sake amfani da shigarwar kuzari-kowace-kira, PUE, da grid ɗinku kuma tana bambanta ƙidayar kira kawai, don haka alkaluman suna girma a layi ɗaya.

Ƙa’ida

Makamashi (kWh) = kira × Wh a kowace kira × PUE ÷ 1,000. Fitar da hayaki (kg CO₂e) = makamashi × grid grams a kowace kWh ÷ 1,000. Fitar da hayaki a kowace kira (g CO₂e) = Wh a kowace kira × PUE × grid grams a kowace kWh ÷ 1,000.

Misalin aiki

Kira 100,000, makamashi 0.5 Wh a kowace kira, PUE 1.2, grid 450 gCO₂e/kWh. Makamashi = 100,000 × 0.5 × 1.2 ÷ 1,000 = 60 kWh. Watsawa = 60 × 450 ÷ 1,000 = 27 kg CO₂e. Kowace kira: 0.5 × 1.2 × 450 ÷ 1,000 = 0.27 g CO₂e; kowace kira 1,000: 270 g CO₂e. Waɗannan shigarwar misalai ne kawai - maye gurbin alkaluman makamashi-a kowace kira da grid da naka kafin dogara ga sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan samfurin yanayi ne, ba sakamakon lissafin carbon ba. Ya ƙunshi makamashin da kuka bayyana ne kawai: horo, haɓaka samfuri, kera kayan aiki, hanyar sadarwa, sanyaya fiye da PUE, da kuma hayakin da aka haɗa. Kuzarin da aka yi amfani da shi ya bambanta ta hanyar samfuri, kayan aiki, bazuwar abubuwa, da tsawon lokacin da aka ɗauka, kuma ƙarfin grid ya bambanta ta wurin wuri da lokaci - duka shigarwar mai amfani ne a nan daidai saboda babu wani ƙimar da aka buga da ya dace da kowace shari'a. Ba a bayar da bayanan hayakin da ke rayuwa ko lissafin kuɗi mai inganci ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Makamashi shine lokutan kira Wh a kowace lokacin kira PUE, an raba shi da 1,000 don samun kWh; hayaki shine lokutan kira grid na makamashi gram a kowace kWh, an raba shi da 1,000 don samun kilogiram na CO₂e.

Me yasa ake iya gyara sinadarin carbon da makamashi a kowace kira?

Domin su zato ne, ba madaidaitan abubuwa ba. Wani tsari daban, GPU, ko yanki yana ba da ƙima daban-daban, don haka shafin yana ɗaukar su a matsayin shigarwar da dole ne ku bayar kuma yana yiwa tsoffin abubuwa alama a matsayin misalai kawai.

Me PUE ke nufi a nan?

Ingancin amfani da wutar lantarki: jimlar makamashin wurin da aka raba ta da makamashin IT. PUE na 1.2 yana nufin 20% na sama don sanyaya da sauran nauyin kayan aiki a saman makamashin da aka ƙayyade; 1.0 yana nufin babu wani abin da aka ƙididdige.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da horar da samfurin?

A'a. Ya ƙunshi kawai abubuwan da za a iya fahimta a cikin yanayinka. Horarwa, gyarawa, da kera kayan aiki sun bambanta kuma sun fi girma a wasu lokuta, kuma ba a yin koyi da su a nan ba.

Me yasa sakamakon yake layi a cikin ƙidayar kira?

Kowace layi a cikin teburin ƙara yana sake amfani da adadi ɗaya na kowace kira, don haka kiran ninkawa yana ninka kimantawa. Tsarin gaske na iya yin batch ko cache, wanda zai karya wannan layi - wani dalili na maye gurbin misali na kowace kira da bayanai da aka auna.

Zan iya amfani da wannan don rahoton hayaki mai gurbata muhalli na hukuma?

A'a. Kiyasin tsare-tsare ne daga zato naka. Rahoton da aka tsara yana buƙatar a auna bayanan makamashi, da kuma tsarin grid da aka rubuta don yankin da lokacin da ya dace, da kuma tsarin rahoton da ƙungiyar ku ke bi.
