Formula

Use fₒ = fₛ(c+vₒ)/(c−vₛ). fₛ is emitted frequency, c is sound speed in the medium, vₒ is observer velocity toward the source, and vₛ is source velocity toward the observer. Velocities share a unit; both moving speeds must be subsonic.
