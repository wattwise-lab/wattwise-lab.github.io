Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da N = floor[(L+k)/(p+k)]. L shine tsawon kayan aiki, p shine tsawon kayan aiki da aka gama, kuma k shine kerf ɗin da aka yanka a cikin naúrar ɗaya. floor yana zagaye ƙasa. Wannan yana ƙididdige kerf tsakanin guda; yankewar ƙarshe na iya buƙatar wani kerf.
