Iyakokin watanni uku—

Ana ƙididdige shekarun haihuwa na yau kai tsaye daga shigar LMP zuwa ranar da ake ciki, don haka yana canzawa kowace rana; ranar haihuwa da kanta ba ta canzawa da zarar an ƙayyade LMP.

Ƙa’ida

Ranar da aka kiyasta ranar haihuwa = ranar farko ta haila ta ƙarshe + kwanaki 280 (makonni 40). Wannan ita ce ƙa'idar Naegele. Tana ɗaukar zagayowar kwanaki 28 akai-akai tare da ovulation a ranar 14, shi ya sa aka nuna kimanin ranar ɗaukar ciki a matsayin kwanaki LMP + 14. Iyakokin watanni uku da aka nuna sune makonni LMP + 14 da makonni LMP + 28.

Misalin aiki

Lokacin haila na ƙarshe 2026-01-01: ranar da aka kiyasta = 2026-01-01 + kwanaki 280 = 2026-10-08. Kimanin ɗaukar ciki (28-zaton zagayowar rana) shine 2026-01-15. A ranar da aka ambata 2026-09-10, shekarun ciki shine (2026-09-10 − 2026-01-01) = 252 kwanaki = 36 makonni 0 kwanaki, tare da kwanaki 28 (4 makonni 0 kwanaki) da suka rage zuwa ranar da aka kiyasta. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake maimaita ranar haihuwa; shekarun ciki a yau akan shafin kai tsaye ana ƙididdige su ne daga ainihin ranar da ake ciki.

Amfani da iyakoki

Dokar Naegele kimantawa ce da aka gina akan zato biyu waɗanda galibi ba sa cika daidai: zagayowar kwanaki 28 na yau da kullun da ovulation a ranar 14. Zagaye masu tsayi ko marasa daidaituwa, kwanakin da ba a san su ba LMP, da kuma lokacin duban dan tayi duk zasu iya canza kimantawa, kuma ainihin ranar haihuwa ya bambanta daga mutum zuwa mutum - kaɗan ne kawai daga cikin haihuwa ke faɗuwa daidai akan ranar da aka kiyasta. Wannan shafin ba ya maye gurbin bayanan kula da mata masu juna biyu ko kuma saduwa ta asibiti, kuma ba ya da'awar lafiyar kowace ciki.

FAQ

Menene dokar Naegele?

Ƙara kwanaki 280 (40 makonni) zuwa ranar farko ta haila ta ƙarshe. Ita ce ƙa'idar ranar soyayya ta yau da kullun, ba ma'auni ba.

Me yasa ainihin ranar zai iya bambanta?

Saboda zagayowar ta bambanta: ovulation ba koyaushe yake a ranar 14 ba, tsawon zagayowar ya bambanta, kuma ranar LMP da kanta ba za a iya tabbatar da ita ba. Saurin kallon hotuna sau da yawa yana inganta kimantawa.

Me zai faru idan zagayowar ta ta fi kwanaki 28?

To ovulation wataƙila ya zo daga baya fiye da ranar 14, kuma ainihin ranar haihuwa na iya zama daga baya fiye da wannan kimantawa. Shafin bai daidaita tsawon zagayowar ba - cewa daidaitawa ya shafi soyayya ta asibiti.

Shin ranar haihuwa tana nufin jaririn zai zo a wannan ranar?

A'a. Kiyasta ne a tsakiyar wata iyaka; ainihin kwanakin haihuwa sun bambanta sosai a kusa da shi.

An adana bayanai na?

A'a. Wannan lissafin yana aiki a cikin gida a cikin burauzarka kuma ƙimar da ka shigar ba a aika ta zuwa ga bayanan CalcMintly ba.

Shin ya kamata in yi amfani da wannan don yanke shawara kan lafiya?

A'a. Yi amfani da bayanan kula da mata masu juna biyu da shawarwarin asibiti don yanke shawara kan lafiya; wannan shafin yana nuna lissafin dokar Naegele ne kawai.
