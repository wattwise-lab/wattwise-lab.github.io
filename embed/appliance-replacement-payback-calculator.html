Formula

Use S = (Eₒ−Eₙ)p. Eₒ and Eₙ are old and new annual energy use in kWh, and p is price per kWh. S is annual bill saving; simple payback divides net replacement cost by a positive saving.

Scope and limitations

Assumes constant annual consumption and electricity price. Payback includes energy savings only; maintenance, financing, resale and appliance lifetime are excluded.
