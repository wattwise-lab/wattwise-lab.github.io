UnitValue wey e get

All seven units na di same length dem describe. Di inch and foot factors below na exact definitions.

Formula

Target value = input × original-unit factor ÷ target-unit factor; di factors na metres for each unit.

UnitMetres for each unit
Metre (m)1
Kilometre (km)1,000
Centimetre (cm)0.01
Millimetre (mm)0.001
Inch (in)0.0254
Foot (ft)0.3048
Mile (mi)1,609.344

Di definition of inch na exactly 0.0254 m, so di foot (12 inches) and international mile (5,280 feet) na exact too.

Example wey show the calculation

1 m ÷ 0.3048 m/ft = 3.280840 ft, and 1 m ÷ 0.0254 m/in = 39.370079 in. One 5 km run na 5,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = 3.106856 miles.

Where e fit work and limits

Di page no dey accept negative lengths. Dem round di displayed values to 6 decimal places; di actual calculation no dey rounded. Dis page dey use international mile; e no dey use di old US survey mile, wey long pass am by about 2 parts per million.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How many feet dey inside one metre?

3.28083989501 ft; after display rounding, dem show 3.280840.

Di inch na exact unit?

Yes: since 1959, di definition of international inch na exactly 25.4 mm.

Wetin about yards?

Yards no dey listed separately: 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m. Enter feet and divide by 3, or enter metres and use di foot result.

Why di mile row show plenty decimals for 1 m?

Because 1 m na very small part of mile (0.000621 mi); 6 decimal places dey keep am visible instead of rounding am to zero.

Dis one cover area or volume?

No, na only length. Area and volume get their own converters for di related tools below.
