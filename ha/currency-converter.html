Rushewa

AbuAdadin

An cire kuɗin daga adadin da aka canza. Shigar da ƙimar da mai ba ku sabis ya ambata a zahiri, ba ƙimar daga ƙwaƙwalwa ba.

Ƙa’ida

Adadin da aka karɓa = adadin tushe × ƙimar musanya × (1 − ƙimar kuɗin). Daidai yake: jimlar = adadin × ƙimar kuɗi; adadin kuɗin = jimlar × kuɗin %; an karɓa = jimlar − adadin kuɗin.

ShigarwaMisalin ƙima
Adadin1,000
Kima (misali kawai)7.2
Kuɗi1%

Ba a samo farashi daga kowace tushen kasuwa ba. Asalin 7.2 misali ne mai riƙe wuri da aka ɗauka daga kayan aikin asali, wanda aka yiwa alama kamar haka - ba ƙima ba ce, ba na yanzu ba, kuma ba shawara ba ce.

Misalin aiki

1,000 × 7.2 = 7,200 jimilla. Kudin 1% shine 7,200 × 0.01 = 72, don haka adadin da aka karɓa shine 7,200 − 72 = 7,128. Matsakaicin tasiri shine 7,128 ÷ 1,000 = 7.128 kowace raka'a.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan shafin bai bayar da canjin kai tsaye ko na kasuwa ba, kuma ƙimar tsoho misali ne kawai. Bankuna da masu samar da biyan kuɗi suna ƙara shimfidawa da kuɗaɗen da aka ƙayyade waɗanda wannan kaso na kuɗi ɗaya ba zai iya kamawa ba; tabbatar da ainihin ƙimar, yaɗuwa, da duk wani caji mai ƙayyadadden lokaci kafin aika kuɗi. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6; masu samar da kayayyaki na gaske suna zagaye zuwa ƙaramin raka'a na kuɗin, wanda zai iya canza cents na ƙarshe.

FAQ

Shin ƙimar da aka saba amfani da ita a yanzu ƙimar kasuwa ce?

A'a. Misalin ƙima ne (7.2) da aka ɗauka daga kayan aikin asali, wanda aka yi masa alama a sarari a matsayin misali. Wannan shafin ba ya ɗaukar bayanan kasuwa - koyaushe yana maye gurbinsa da ƙimar da mai ba ku sabis ya bayar.

Shin kuɗin ya shafi yaɗuwar mai bada sabis ɗin?

Sai dai idan kun ninka yaduwar zuwa kashi na kuɗin da kanku. Mai bayarwa yana ambaton mafi munin ƙima fiye da kasuwa yana cajin ku wani ɓoye yaduwa a saman duk wani kuɗin da aka ambata.

Me zai faru idan kuɗin ƙayyadadden ƙima ne, ba kashi ba?

Cire shi daga jimlar kuɗin da kanka: an karɓa = adadin × ƙimar − kuɗin da aka ƙayyade. Wannan shafin yana ƙirar kuɗaɗen kashi kawai.

Me yasa adadin da aka karɓa ya yi ƙasa da adadin × ƙimar?

Domin ana cire kashi na kuɗin daga adadin da aka canza. Tare da kuɗin 0% duka biyun sun yi daidai.

Zan iya canzawa zuwa ɗayan gefen?

Eh: raba maimakon ninkawa. Idan raka'a 1,000 suka saya 7,128 a misalan ƙimar da kuɗin da aka biya, ƙimar da ta fi tasiri ita ce 1,000 ÷ 7,128 = 0.140292 a kowace raka'a.
