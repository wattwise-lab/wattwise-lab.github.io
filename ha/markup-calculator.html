Kalkuleta Alamar Shaida

Farashin siyarwa daga kashi na farashi da alamar shaida, tare da riba a kowace raka'a da kuma daidai gwargwado — da kuma bayanin bambancin alamar shaida-da-margin.

Ƙa’ida

Farashin siyarwa = farashi × (1 + alamar shaida ÷ 100). Riba a kowace raka'a = farashin siyarwa − farashi. Gefen = riba ÷ farashin siyarwa × 100 — lura cewa ma'aunin shine farashi, ba farashi ba, shi ya sa gefen da alamar farashi lambobi ne daban-daban ga samfur ɗaya.

Misalin aiki

Kudin $40 tare da alamar 50%: farashin siyarwa = $40 × 1.50 = $60.00. Riba a kowace raka'a = $60.00 − $40 = $20.00. Gefen daidai = $20.00 ÷ $60.00 = 33.33%. Samfurin iri ɗaya shine "50% alamar" da "33.33% gefen" a lokaci guda - duka daidai ne, an auna su akan tushe daban-daban.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kudin a nan yana nufin farashin raka'a (kayayyaki, samarwa, ko farashin siye). Bai haɗa da abubuwan da suka shafi kuɗi, jigilar kaya, kuɗin biyan kuɗi, ko haraji ba sai kun ninka su cikin shigar da farashi. Shafin yana farashin raka'a ɗaya; ninka ta adadi don rukuni. Alamar da ke ƙasa da 0% zai nufin sayarwa a ƙarƙashin farashi — shafin yana buƙatar sifili ko sama da haka, tunda farashin da ke ƙarƙashin farashi shawara ce ta shugaban asara da gangan, ba lissafin alamar ba.

FAQ

Alamar ko alamar - wanne ya kamata in yi amfani da shi?

Alamar da ke saita farashi daga farashi (tambayar da aka saba yi a kan shago); alamar da ke bayar da rahoton riba (riba a matsayin rabon kuɗin shiga). Canzawa tare da alamar = alamar ÷ (100 + alamar) × 100.

Shin alamar 50% daidai take da alamar 50%?

A'a. Alamar 50% akan $40 yana ba da farashi na $60 da alamar 33.33%. Alamar 50% akan farashin $60 zai buƙaci farashin $30. Rikitar su biyun ita ce kuskuren farashi mafi tsada da ƙaramin kasuwanci ke yi.

Me zai faru idan ina son alamar da aka yi niyya maimakon haka?

Yi aiki a baya: farashi = farashi ÷ (1 − gefe ÷ 100). Don riba ta 40% akan farashin $40, farashi = $40 ÷ 0.60 = $66.67.

Shin haraji ya kamata ya kasance cikin farashi ko kuma a ƙara shi daga baya?

Yi amfani da tushen haraji mai daidaito don farashi da farashi. Wannan misali yana ɗaukar farashin siyarwa a matsayin wanda ba ya haɗa da haraji; ko harajin siye yana cikin kuɗin ku ya dogara da yadda za a iya dawo da shi da ƙa'idodin gida. Yi amfani da tushen harajin tallace-tallace da suka dace kuma ku ƙididdige daban.

Shin riba ta wuce 100%?

Ee — kayayyaki masu tsada da tsada galibi suna yin hakan. Alamar 200% ta ninka farashin sau uku: $40 ya zama $120, tare da riba ta 66.67%.

Shin wannan yana rufe rangwame?

A'a. Rangwame yana fitowa daga farashin siyarwa bayan riba. Fara farashi da farko tare da alamar farashi, sannan a yi amfani da alamar farashi don ganin ribar ƙarshe.
