Rushewa

Abudarajar

Mai ninka haɗin gwiwa shine haɗin gwiwa da aka raba ta 4, an manne tsakanin 0.7 da 1.5, don haka haɗin gwiwa mai ƙasa ko mai girma ba zai iya motsa ƙimar ba tare da iyaka ba.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sanduna zuwa mafi girman darajar da farashin samarwa bisa ga CPM, wanda ke nuna rabin ƙimar da ya mamaye.

Ƙimar a ƙididdigar kallo daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowane layi yana sake amfani da kuɗin CPM, haɗin gwiwa, awanni, da kuɗin sa'a kuma yana bambanta ra'ayoyin da ake tsammani kawai. An gyara farashin samarwa, don haka ƙimar ba ta yin girma kai tsaye da ra'ayoyi.

Ƙa’ida

Ƙimar mai tallafawa = ra'ayoyi ÷ 1,000 × CPM × manne(haɗin gwiwa ÷ 4, 0.7, 1.5) + sa'o'in samarwa × farashin sa'a. Jerin 85%–120% da aka nuna shine lokutan kimantawa 0.85 da 1.20.

Misalin aiki

Ra'ayoyi 80,000, CPM $120, haɗin gwiwa 4.5%, sa'o'in samarwa 16 a $200. Mai ninkawa = 4.5 ÷ 4 = 1.125, a cikin matsewar 0.7–1.5. Ƙimar da aka dogara da CPM = 80,000 ÷ 1,000 × $120 × 1.125 = $10,800. Kudin samarwa = 16 × $200 = $3,200. Jimilla = $14,000. Jerin misalai: $14,000 × 85% = $11,900 zuwa $14,000 × 120% = $16,800.

Amfani da iyakoki

Mai ninkawa da kewayon 85%–120% ƙa'idodin yanayi ne na musamman daga shafin tushe, ba kimanta kasuwa ba. Haƙƙoƙi, keɓancewa, lokacin amfani, gyare-gyare, yin rajistar fari, da sake amfani da su ba a cire su ba, kuma kowane ɗayansu zai iya motsa ƙimar gaske fiye da yadda dabarar take yi. Ra'ayoyin da ake tsammani zato ne, ba garanti ba, kuma CPM ne ka shigar — CPM da aka aro daga wani wuri ko dandamali ba zai yi aiki ba. Ayyukan da suka gabata, dacewa da masu sauraro, da abubuwan da za a iya bayarwa har yanzu suna yanke shawara kan abin da mai tallafawa zai biya a zahiri.

FAQ

Yaya ake lissafin ƙimar?

Ra'ayoyi da aka raba da 1,000 sau CPM sau mai ninka haɗin gwiwa (haɗin gwiwa da 4, an manne tsakanin 0.7 da 1.5), tare da lokutan samarwa sau farashin sa'a guda.

Me yasa za a daidaita ta hanyar haɗin gwiwa kwata-kwata?

Domin masu ƙirƙira biyu masu ƙidayar kallo iri ɗaya ba sa ba da kulawa iri ɗaya. Ma'aunin 4% yana nufin haɗin gwiwa sama da 4% yana ɗaga ƙimar kuma ƙasa da 4% yana rage ta, a cikin matsewa.

Menene kewayon 85%-120%?

Ƙungiyar tattaunawa mai misali game da kimantawa, daga ƙa'idodin yanayin shafin tushe. Ba kewayon kasuwa bane kuma baya fitowa daga bayanan masu tallafawa.

Shin ya kamata in caje kuɗin samarwa daban?

Wannan shafin ya haɗa shi cikin ƙima ɗaya don haka jimlar tana nuna ƙimar masu sauraro da aiki. Idan mai tallafawa ya fi son ƙiyasin raba, teburin rarrabuwa yana ba ku rabi biyu don gabatarwa daban-daban.

Me zai faru idan haɗin gwiwata ya kai 20%?

Mai ninka har yanzu yana da iyaka a 1.5, don haka haɗin gwiwa sama da 6% (6 da aka raba ta 4) ba ya ƙara wani abu a cikin wannan samfurin. Ya kamata a yi jayayya game da haɗin gwiwa na musamman da shaida, ba ta hanyar faɗaɗa mai ninka ba.

Me ba a haɗa ba?

Haƙƙoƙin amfani, keɓancewa, zagayen gyara, faɗaɗawa da aka biya, da tsawon kwangila. Farashi waɗanda ke saman wannan ƙiyasin maimakon barin dabarar ta sha su a hankali.
