Formula

Use χ² = Σ(Oᵢ−Eᵢ)²/Eᵢ and df = k−1−p. Oᵢ and Eᵢ are observed and expected counts, k is category count, and p is parameters estimated from the same data. The p-value is the upper chi-square tail.
