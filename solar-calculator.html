HOW TO READ THE RESULT

A sizing estimate is not an installation quote.

The calculator converts your bill to estimated electricity use, applies your target offset, and divides that energy target by local peak-sun hours after system losses. A roof with shade, multiple orientations or limited usable area can produce less.

Why the default loss is 14%

NREL's PVWatts model uses system-loss inputs for factors such as soiling, shading, mismatch, wiring and availability. This simplified tool starts at 14%, but your installer or a detailed PVWatts run should replace it.

Why payback is deliberately simple

The payback number divides gross installed cost by first-year bill savings. It excludes loans, tax treatment, maintenance, degradation and changes to utility export compensation. That makes the math inspectable, not comprehensive.
