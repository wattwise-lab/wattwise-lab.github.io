Formula

Use vₐ = [(mₐ−mᵦ)uₐ+2mᵦuᵦ]/(mₐ+mᵦ) and vᵦ = [2mₐuₐ+(mᵦ−mₐ)uᵦ]/(mₐ+mᵦ). m denotes mass, u initial signed velocity, and v final signed velocity; subscripts distinguish the bodies. Use consistent mass and velocity units.
