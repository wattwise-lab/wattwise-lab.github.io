Rushewa

Adadin

Jimlar kuɗin da za a samu tana canzawa tare da farashin da za a samu. Rabon kuɗin da za a samu shine jimlar kuɗin da za a samu ta hanyar samun kuɗin shiga.

A kallo ɗaya

Ana auna sandunan zuwa mafi girma na jimlar kuɗin da za a samu. Lokacin da farashin ya wuce kuɗin shiga, ana nuna sandar riba a cikin launin ruwan kasa kuma hukuncin ya nuna asarar.

Ribar da za a samu a matakan samun kuɗi daban-daban

Kuɗin shigaRibar da za a samu

Ana riƙe da kuɗaɗen a ƙimar da ke sama; kawai canjin kuɗin shiga yana faruwa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Ribar da aka samu = kudaden shiga − farashin da aka canza − farashin da aka gyara. Jimlar riba = ribar ÷ kudaden shiga × 100%. Jimlar farashi = farashin canji + farashin da aka ƙayyade. Rabon farashi = jimlar farashi ÷ kudaden shiga × 100%.

Misalin aiki

Kuɗin shiga $100,000, farashin canji $68,000, farashin da aka ƙayyade $8,000: jimillar farashi = $76,000, ribar da aka samu = $100,000 − $76,000 = $24,000, ribar da aka samu = $24,000 ÷ $100,000 × 100% = 24.00%, da rabon farashi = 76.00%.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan ra'ayi ne na aiki: riba, haraji, raguwar farashi, da abubuwa na lokaci ɗaya ba a raba su ba. Idan alkaluman kuɗin ku sun riga sun haɗa da su, ribar tana nuna hakan; idan ba haka ba, ba ta faruwa. Ana ɗaukar farashi a matsayin jimillar da aka ƙayyade na lokacin, ba a ƙayyade ƙimar kowane raka'a ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya mai lissafin riba ke aiki?

Ribar da aka samu = kuɗin shiga − farashin da aka ƙayyade − farashin da aka ƙayyade; ribar da aka samu = ribar da aka ƙayyade ÷ kuɗin shiga. Yana raba kuɗin shiga na lokaci zuwa ga farashin da aka cinye da abin da ya rage.

Menene bambanci tsakanin farashin canzawa da na dindindin?

Kudaden canzawa suna tafiya tare da fitarwa - kayan aiki, kuɗin kowane oda. Kudaden da aka gyara ba sa - haya, albashin asali. Raba yana da mahimmanci don nazarin daidaiton daidaito; don ribar da aka tara kawai jimlar tana da mahimmanci.

Menene ma'anar ribar da ba ta da kyau?

Kuɗin da aka kashe ya zarce kuɗin shiga na wannan lokacin: asarar wannan adadin. Ribar ta kasance mara kyau da wannan rabo.

Shin ribar da aka tara iri ɗaya ce da jimlar riba?

A'a. Jimlar riba tana tsayawa akan farashin kayan da aka sayar; ribar da aka tara tana ƙidaya duk farashin canzawa da na dindindin da aka shigar a nan, don haka yawanci yana ƙasa.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da haraji?

A'a, sai dai idan haraji ya riga ya kasance cikin alkaluman farashin da kuka shigar. Yi amfani da ma'aunin haraji daban ga ribar da aka tara.

Me yasa ribar da aka tara take nuna dash a sifili kudaden shiga?

Raba ta hanyar samun kuɗi, don haka a sifili kuɗin shiga ba a bayyana shi ba. Ribar kanta har yanzu ana nuna ta a matsayin jimillar kuɗaɗen da ba su da kyau.
