Rushewa

Abudarajar

Kuɗin shiga shine lokacin oda darajar oda; jimlar riba tana amfani da ribar da ke tsakanin kudaden shiga. ROI shine ribar da aka raba ta hanyar farashin samarwa.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sandunan zuwa mafi girman farashin samarwa da kuma ribar da aka samu. Ribar dole ne ta share fagen farashi don ta zama mai kyau.

ROI a ƙidayar kallo daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowane layi yana sake amfani da ƙimar juyawa, ƙimar oda, gefe, da farashi kuma yana bambanta ra'ayoyi kawai, don haka zaka iya samun adadin ra'ayoyin da suka karya daidai.

Ƙa’ida

ROI = (dubawa × juzu'i% × darajar oda × riba% − farashin samarwa) ÷ farashin samarwa × 100%. Umarni = ra'ayoyi × juyawa%. Babban riba = oda × darajar oda × riba%.

Misalin aiki

Kudin $1,800, ra'ayoyi 50,000, juyawa 0.8%, darajar oda $199, riba 55%. Umarni = 50,000 × 0.8% = 400. Kudaden shiga = 400 × $199 = $79,600. Babban riba = $79,600 × 55% = $43,780. Tsafta = $43,780 − $1,800 = $41,980. ROI = $41,980 ÷ $1,800 × 100% = 2,332.22%. Ra'ayoyin da suka yi daidai a waɗannan ƙimar suna kusan ra'ayoyi 2,056 (kuɗin da aka raba ta hanyar riba ga kowane ra'ayi na $0.8756: 0.8% x $199 x 55%).

Amfani da iyakoki

Ra'ayoyi da juyawa zato ne naka, ba hasashen zirga-zirga ba; ƙidayar ra'ayoyi ba ta tabbatar da cewa ƙimar juyawa ba ce, kuma abubuwan da suka gabata jagora ne kawai mai wahala. Kuɗin shiga kaɗai ba riba ba ne - shigar da riba yana yin wannan aikin, don haka ribar da aka ambata ta wuce gona da iri ta wuce ROI kai tsaye. Ba a tsara ba: oda da aka sanya wa ɓangaren na iya shafar wasu tashoshi, kuma farashin samarwa a nan adadi ɗaya ne, ban da farashin rarrabawa, haɓakawa, da gyara sai dai idan kun haɗa su a ciki.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige ROI?

Umarni lokaci ne na ra'ayoyi lokacin ƙimar juyawa; ribar da aka samu shine lokacin oda lokacin ƙimar oda lokaci; ROI shine ribar da aka samu ta hanyar rage farashin samarwa, an raba ta da farashin samarwa, lokaci 100%.

Wane ƙimar canzawa ya kamata in yi amfani da shi?

An auna shi daga abubuwan da suka gabata iri ɗaya tare da tayin iri ɗaya da masu sauraro, ba matsakaicin dandamali ba. Idan ba ku da ƙimar da aka auna, gudanar da shafin a farashi da yawa kuma ku ga yadda ROI ke motsawa.

Me yasa riba take a cikin dabarar?

Domin samun riba ba riba ba ce. Ba tare da riba ba, babban riba, samfurin da ke da ƙarancin riba yana da kyau fiye da yadda yake. Shigar da jimlar riba akan oda da kansu, bayan farashin samfur da cikawa.

Me ake ɗauka a matsayin kuɗin samarwa?

Duk abin da aka kashe wajen yin aikin: lokacin mai ƙirƙira ko kuɗi, gyara, kayan tallafi, wurare, da lasisi. Ƙara tallan da aka biya daban idan kuna son a ƙidaya shi - ko dai a haɗa shi a cikin farashi ko gudanar da yanayi na biyu.

Shin ROI zai iya wuce 1,000%?

A lissafi, eh, idan wani abu mai rahusa ya canza da kyau, kamar yadda yake a cikin misalin da aka yi aiki. Wannan shine sifar rabon, ba shaida ba cewa kashi na gaba zai maimaita shi - ƙananan farashi suna sa kaso ya yi ta juyawa sosai.

Shin wannan hasashen ra'ayoyi?

A'a. Ra'ayoyi shigarwa ce da kuke bayarwa. Shafin yana gaya muku adadin ra'ayoyin da aka bayar zai yi daidai da canjin ku da kuma ribar ku, ba adadin ra'ayoyin da za ku samu ba.
