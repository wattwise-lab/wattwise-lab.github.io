Ruwan Baker

A haɗa garin da ruwan da ke cikin kullun da aka riga aka bari ya yi ferment a lissafin jimlar rabon ruwa ga gari na kullun gaba ɗaya.

Ƙara zuwa wurin aiki

Ƙimomin shigarwa

Ana lissafi a na’urarka. Sakamako kimantawa ne, ba shawarar ƙwararre ba.

Jimillar ruwan da aka fi so

—

A wannan lissafi, kullun da aka riga aka bar shi ya yi ferment yana ƙunshe da gari da ruwa kawai. Ba a haɗa ruwan ƙwai, madara da sauran sinadarai ba. Ana kwatanta jimlar nauyin ruwa da jimlar nauyin gari wajen lissafin hydration.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da Fₚ = S/(1+h), Wₚ = S−Fₚ da H = (W+Wₚ)/(F+Fₚ). S na nauyin kullun da aka riga aka bar shi ya yi ferment, h na rabon ruwansa ga garinsa a matsayin ƙima ta goma, F da W kuma na gari da ruwan babban kullu; duk nauyi a g. Ana nuna jimlar hydration H a matsayin kashi.

Amfani da iyakoki

A wannan lissafi, kullun da aka riga aka bar shi ya yi ferment yana ƙunshe da gari da ruwa kawai. Ba a haɗa ruwan ƙwai, madara da sauran sinadarai ba. Ana kwatanta jimlar nauyin ruwa da jimlar nauyin gari wajen lissafin hydration.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
