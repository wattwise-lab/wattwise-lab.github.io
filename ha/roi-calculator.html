Rushewa

Adadin

Riba shine abin da kuka ƙare da shi, ban da abin da kuka saka. Ribar kuɗi tana ƙidaya ne kawai idan kun same ta da gaske.

A kallo ɗaya

Sandunan suna kwatanta jarin farko da darajar ƙarshe da kuɗin shiga na kuɗi. Sandunan ƙarshe mai tsawo-ƙima-da-kuɗi na nufin ROI mai kyau.

ROI a ƙimomin ƙarshe daban-daban

Idan ƙimar ƙarshe ta kasanceROI

Ana riƙe kuɗin shiga na kuɗi a ƙimar da ke sama; ƙimar ƙarshe kawai tana canzawa a cikin layuka.

Ƙa’ida

ROI = (ƙimar ƙarshe + kuɗin shiga na kuɗi − jarin farko) ÷ jarin farko × 100%. Riba = ƙimar ƙarshe + kuɗin shiga na kuɗi − jarin farko.

Misalin aiki

Darajar farko $10,000, ƙimar ƙarshe $12,500, kuɗin shiga na kuɗi $500: riba = $12,500 + $500 − $10,000 = $3,000, da kuma ROI = $3,000 ÷ $10,000 × 100% = 30.00%.

Amfani da iyakoki

ROI ba ya lissafin tsawon lokacin da aka saka jari a cikin jari, don haka ba zai iya kwatanta jarin da aka riƙe na tsawon lokaci daban-daban ba - yi amfani da ribar shekara-shekara don hakan. Ba a haɗa haraji, kuɗaɗe, da hauhawar farashi ba sai dai idan an riga an nuna su a cikin ƙimar da kuka shigar.

FAQ

Ta yaya kalkuleta ROI ke aiki?

ROI = (ƙimar ƙarshe + kuɗin shiga na kuɗi − jarin farko) ÷ jarin farko × 100%. Yana amsa tambaya ɗaya: ga kowace dala da aka saka, nawa ya dawo sama da hannun jarin farko?

Me yasa ROI dina ya zama mara kyau?

Mara kyau ROI yana nufin ƙimar ƙarshe da kuɗin shiga na kuɗi ya ƙasa da jarin farko - kun sami ƙasa da abin da kuka saka. Girman asarar shine adadin riba da aka nuna a matsayin adadi mara kyau.

Zan iya kwatanta jarin biyu da ROI kawai?

Sai dai idan an riƙe su na tsawon lokaci ɗaya. Ribar 30% a cikin shekara ɗaya ta fi 30% a cikin shekaru biyar. Don lokutan riƙewa daban-daban, kwatanta ribar da aka samu a shekara-shekara maimakon haka.

Shin ya kamata a haɗa kuɗin shiga?

Haka ne, idan kun karɓa - rabon riba, riba, ko haya da aka biya a lokacin riƙewa wani ɓangare ne na ribar. Kada ku ninka ribar da aka riga aka nuna a ƙimar ƙarshe.

Shin ROI ya haɗa da haraji ko kuɗaɗe?

A'a, sai dai idan ƙimar da kuka shigar ta riga ta nuna su. Kuɗaɗen dillali, haraji akan riba, da hauhawar farashin duk suna rage ainihin ribar.

Shin babban ROI koyaushe ya fi kyau?

Ba lallai ba ne. ROI bai faɗi komai game da haɗari, canjin yanayi, ko tsawon lokacin da aka ɗaure kuɗin ba. Babban ROI akan saka hannun jari mai haɗari, mara ruwa ba daidai yake da ƙaramin ROI akan wanda aka amince da shi ba.
