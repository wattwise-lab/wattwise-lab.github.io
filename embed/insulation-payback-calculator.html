Formula

Use E = A(Uₒ−Uₙ)D×24/(1000η). A is area in m²; Uₒ and Uₙ are old and new transmittance in W/m²·K; D is annual heating degree days in K·day; η is system efficiency. E is annual energy saving in kWh.
