Duk alkaluman tattalin arziki

Aunadarajar

US MPG yana amfani da galan US (3.785411784 L); UK MPG yana amfani da galan imperial (4.54609 L) — adadin UK koyaushe yana da girma ga mota ɗaya.

Man fetur don tafiye-tafiye a wannan ƙimar

An auna sanduna zuwa tafiyar 600 km. Man fetur don tafiya = ƙimar (L/100km) × nisan tafiya ÷ 100.

Ƙa’ida

L/100 km = lita ÷ kilomita × 100. US MPG yana canza kilomita da lita zuwa mil da galan US: MPG (US) = (km ÷ 1.609344) ÷ (L ÷ 3.785411784). UK MPG yana amfani da 4.54609 L a kowace galan maimakon haka. Km/L = kilomita ÷ lita.

Daidaidarajar
1 mil1.609344 km
1 US galan3.785411784 L
1 UK (imperial) galan4.54609 L

Misalin aiki

600 km akan 45 L: 45 ÷ 600 × 100 = 7.5 L/100km; 600 ÷ 45 = 13.333333 km/L. Mil = 600 ÷ 1.609344 = 372.822715; US galan = 45 ÷ 3.785411784 = 11.887742, don haka US MPG = 372.822715 ÷ 11.887742 = 31.361944. UK galan = 45 ÷ 4.54609 = 9.898616, don haka UK MPG = 372.822715 ÷ 9.898616 = 37.664125.

Amfani da iyakoki

Siffar tanki ɗaya daga tanki zuwa tanki tana da hayaniya: matakin cikawa, hanya, yanayi, kaya, da salon tuƙi duk suna motsa shi. Yi amfani da man fetur da aka ƙara a cikin tazara mai kama da ta cikakken tanki, kamar yadda kayan aikin tushe suka ba da shawara, kuma matsakaicin tankuna da yawa don adadi mai inganci. Ana auna alkaluman masana'anta akan zagayowar gwaji kuma yawanci suna doke sakamakon gaske. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6.

FAQ

Menene kyakkyawan adadi na L/100km?

Aƙalla: ƙarƙashin 6 yana da araha, 6–9 yana da matsakaicin kewayon motar mai ta iyali, sama da 9 yana da ƙishirwa. Dizal, hybrids, da motocin lantarki ba su da kwatance kai tsaye a wannan sikelin.

Me yasa UK MPG ya fi US MPG girma?

Mota ɗaya, babbar galan: galan imperial (4.54609 L) yana ɗaukar kusan kashi 20% na man fetur fiye da galan US (3.785411784 L), don haka tazara ɗaya a kowace lita tana ƙaruwa a cikin UK MPG.

Ta yaya zan canza L/100km zuwa MPG?

US MPG = 235.214583 ÷ L/100km. Ga 7.5 L/100km hakan yana ba da 31.361944, daidai da misalin da aka yi aiki.

Shin ya kamata in auna tanki ɗaya ko da yawa?

Da yawa. Tanki ɗaya yana da sauƙin karkatar da shi ta yadda famfon ke tsayawa. Matsakaici akan aƙalla cikawa uku masu kama da juna.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da farashin mai?

A'a - wannan shafin yana auna amfani kawai. Mai lissafin farashin mai (wanda aka haɗa a ƙasa) yana mayar da amfani zuwa kuɗi.
