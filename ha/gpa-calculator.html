GPA Kalkuleta

Ma'aunin maki na credit GPA akan sikelin 4.0 har zuwa darussa huɗu.

Ƙa’ida

GPA = jimlar (maki na maki × maki na kwas) ÷ jimillar maki. Kowane kwas ana auna shi da maki nasa, don haka kwas ɗin maki na maki na 4 yana ƙidaya sau biyu fiye da kwas ɗin maki na 2.

Misalin aiki

Maki 3.7 (3 maki), 3.3 (3 maki), 4.0 (4 maki) da 3.0 (2 maki): maki sune 11.1 + 9.9 + 16.0 + 6.0 = 43.0, sama da maki 12, don haka GPA = 43.0 ÷ 12 = 3.5833. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saita maki da ke sama don sake maimaita wannan sakamakon, sannan maye gurbin ƙimar da naka.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan shafin yana amfani da maki maki da ka shigar akan sikelin 4.0; baya canza maki haruffa, saboda makarantu suna amfani da juzu'i daban-daban. Ba ya amfani da nauyi na girmamawa/AP, maye gurbin kwas mai maimaitawa, ko ƙa'idodin wucewa/faduwa - yi amfani da manufar cibiyar ku kafin shigar da maki. An cire kwas ɗin da aka bar babu komai ko tare da maki 0. An zagaye nuni zuwa wurare na adadi 4.

FAQ

Ta yaya zan shigar da maki na harafi?

A fara canza shi ta amfani da sikelin makarantarku (misali, A = 4.0, B = 3.0 akan ma'aunin 4.0 da yawa), sannan a shigar da maki na maki da suka biyo baya. Wannan shafin bai yi hasashen canjin ba.

Me yasa ake auna nauyin GPA akan maki?

Darasi mai darajar maki da yawa yana wakiltar ƙarin aiki, don haka makinsa yana ƙidaya daidai gwargwado. Matsakaicin maki mai sauƙi zai ba da maki na 2 da darasin bashi na 4 daidai gwargwado, wanda ba haka GPA ke aiki ba.

Me zai faru idan ina da darussa sama da huɗu?

Haɗa wannan lissafin a duk darussa: GPA har yanzu jimillar maki ne ÷ jimillar maki. Shigar da huɗu a lokaci guda kuma a haɗa, ko a ƙara jimillar daga gudu daban-daban.

Me ke faruwa da darasin da babu komai?

Ba a yin watsi da shi gaba ɗaya ba — ba ya ƙara maki ko maki.

Shin wannan yana kula da makin da aka riga aka auna (girmamawa/AP) GPA?

Sai dai idan kun shigar da makin da aka riga aka auna. Shafin da kansa ba ya amfani da ƙarin nauyi.
