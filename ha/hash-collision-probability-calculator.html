Yiwuwar Hash Collision

Kimanta karo na ranar haihuwa a cikin sararin hash iri ɗaya.

Ƙara zuwa wurin aiki

Ƙimomin shigarwa

Ana lissafi a na’urarka. Sakamako kimantawa ne, ba shawarar ƙwararre ba.

Kimanin yuwuwar karo

—

Wannan ƙididdiga ta shafi hash masu zaman kansu da rarrabuwar yiwuwar iri ɗaya kawai. Har zuwa hash 10,000, ana lissafa ninkin yiwuwar rashin karo a sararin log; ga adadi mafi girma, ana amfani da kimanta ranar haihuwa. Idan adadin hash ya fi adadin sakamakon da zai yiwu, tabbas za a samu karo. Wannan ba hujjar tsaron kriptografi ba ce kuma ba samfurin karo da maharin ya haddasa ba ne.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

n shine ƙidayar hash kuma b shine faɗin a cikin rago. Don n ≤ 10,000, P = 1 − ∏(1 − k/2^b), k = 0…n−1. Babban ƙidaya yi amfani da P ≈ 1 − exp[−n(n−1)/(2×2^b)]. Idan n > 2^b ne, P = 1.
