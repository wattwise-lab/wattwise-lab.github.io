Rushewa

darajar

Kudin mai idan aka kwatanta da kudin haya da filin ajiye motoci

An auna sanduna zuwa mafi girma daga cikin farashin biyu. An shigar da kudin haya da filin ajiye motoci a matsayin adadi ɗaya.

Kudin a nisan tafiya daban-daban

Nisadarajar

Tattalin arzikin mai iri ɗaya da farashin mai, tare da adadin haya da filin ajiye motoci iri ɗaya a kowane nisa, don haka bangaren mai shine abin da ke canzawa.

Ƙa’ida

Man da aka yi amfani da shi = nisan ÷ 100 × lita a kowace 100 km. Kudin mai = man da aka yi amfani da shi × farashin kowace lita. Kudin tafiya = kudin mai + kudin haya da filin ajiye motoci.

Misalin aiki

Nisa 500 km, tattalin arziki 7.5 L/100 km, farashi 8 kowace lita, kuɗin fito da wurin ajiye motoci 100: man fetur da aka yi amfani da shi = 500 ÷ 100 × 7.5 = 37.5 L, farashin mai = 37.50 × 8 = 300.00, farashin tafiya = 300.00 + 100 = 400.00, ko 0.80 kowace km. Farashin mai a nan misali ne kawai na tsoho - shigar da farashin da kuke tsammanin biya.

Amfani da iyakoki

An cire raguwar farashin abin hawa, kulawa, inshora, zirga-zirga, hauhawar farashin mai da canje-canje a kan hanya. Ana ɗaukar tattalin arzikin mai a matsayin wanda ba ya canzawa, kodayake yawan amfani na gaske ya bambanta da sauri, kaya, yanayi da salon tuki. Kuɗaɗen fito da wurin ajiye motoci shigarwa ɗaya ce ta haɗaka: ƙara duka biyun tare kafin shiga. Babu farashin mai da aka gabatar a matsayin matsakaicin kasuwa.

FAQ

Ta yaya lissafin farashin mai yake aiki?

Kudin tafiya = nisan ÷ 100 × lita a kowace 100 km × farashi a kowace lita + kuɗin haya da filin ajiye motoci.

Wane farashin mai ya kamata in shigar?

Farashin da kuke tsammanin biya a kan hanya, a cikin kuɗin da kuke so. Farashi ya bambanta da tashar da yanki, don haka misali tsoho shine mai riƙe wuri kawai.

Yaya game da tafiyar dawowa?

Nisan nisan nisan nisan nisan kafin shiga shi, ko kuma gudanar da lissafin sau ɗaya kuma ninki biyu na bangaren mai. Kudin haya da filin ajiye motoci don dawowa suna tafiya a cikin filin da aka haɗa.

Me yasa ainihin amfani da man fetur na ya fi wannan?

Yawancin lokaci ana auna alkaluman tattalin arziki a cikin yanayi na yau da kullun. Ciniki a cikin birni, kaya masu nauyi, akwatunan rufi, yanayin sanyi da manyan gudu duk suna ƙaruwa da amfani.

Shin wannan ya shafi motocin lantarki?

A'a. Wannan lissafin mai ne na ruwa-mai a lita a kowace 100 km. EV yana buƙatar adadi na amfani da makamashi a cikin kWh a kowace 100 km da farashin wutar lantarki a maimakon haka.
