Rushewa

Adadin

Rage girma shine raguwar da kuke tsammani, an shigar da shi kai tsaye - ba a annabta shi daga ma'aunin sassauci ba. Duba FAQ don yadda yanayin tushe ya yiwa wannan shigarwa alama.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sanduna zuwa babban riba. Gajeren sandar sabon riba yana nufin karuwar tana asarar kuɗi duk da hauhawar farashi.

Canjin riba a raguwar girma daban-daban

Rage girmaSaurin riba

Farashi, farashi, tallace-tallace, da karuwar farashi ana riƙe su a ƙimar da ke sama; Ragewar girma kawai yana canzawa a cikin layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Sabon farashi = farashi × (1 + ƙaruwa%). Sabon girma = tallace-tallace × (1 − raguwar girma%). Sabon riba = (sabon farashi − farashi) × sabon girma. Ribar yanzu = (farashi − farashi) × tallace-tallace. Canjin riba = sabon riba − riba ta yanzu.

Misalin aiki

Farashi $199, farashi $110, raka'o'in tallace-tallace 1,000, ƙaruwa 8%, raguwar girma 5%: sabon farashi = $199 × 1.08 = $214.92, sabon girma = 1,000 × 0.95 = raka'o'in 950, sabon riba = ($214.92 − $110) × 950 = $99,674, ribar yanzu = ($199 − $110) × 1,000 = $89,000, don haka ribar ta ƙaru da $10,674.

Amfani da iyakoki

Farashin raka'a ya kasance iri ɗaya kuma raguwar girma kai ne ka shigar, ba annabta ba. Kuɗaɗen da aka ƙayyade, amsoshin masu fafatawa, canjin abokin ciniki akan lokaci, da canje-canjen buƙata ba a yi su da ƙira ba - a ɗauki sakamakon a matsayin kwatancen yanayi, ba hasashen yanayi ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Canjin riba = (farashi × (1 + karuwa%) − farashi) × tallace-tallace × (1 − raguwar girma%) − (farashi − farashi) × tallace-tallace. Sabon riba a kowace raka'a ya ninka sabon girma, an rage ribar yau.

Menene ma'anar shigar da raguwar girma?

Shigar da kaso na adadin tallace-tallace da kake tsammanin za ka rasa bayan hauhawar farashi. Tsarin ya shafi wannan kaso kai tsaye ga tallace-tallace na yanzu. Ba ya kimanta buƙata ko ninka ma'aunin farashi ta hanyar canjin farashi.

Shin karuwar farashi zai iya rasa kuɗi?

Ee. Idan girman da aka rasa ya fi ƙarin riba a kowace raka'a, sabon riba ya faɗi ƙasa da ribar yanzu. Wannan shine ainihin cinikin da wannan kalkuleta ya fallasa.

Me yasa ake riƙe da farashin raka'a akai-akai?

Canje-canjen girma na iya motsa farashin raka'a a zahiri, amma wannan samfurin yana ware cinikin farashi-girma. Idan farashin na'urarka ya canza tare da girma, daidaita shigarwar farashi kuma kwatanta yanayi.

Yaya game da farashin da aka ƙayyade?

Sun soke daga canjin: duka yanayi suna ɗauke da farashi iri ɗaya, don haka bambancin riba yana faruwa ne ta hanyar gefen naúrar da girma kawai.

Shin zan yi amfani da wannan azaman shawarar farashi da kansa?

A'a. Haɗa shi da binciken abokin ciniki, farashin masu fafatawa, da duba gefe - raguwar girma da kuka shigar shine shigarwar da ba a tabbatar da ita ba, don haka gwada ƙima da yawa.
