Formula

Annual time value = people × hourly cost × meeting minutes ÷ 60 × weekly meetings × working weeks. Cost per meeting = people × hourly cost × minutes ÷ 60. Cost per week = cost per meeting × weekly meetings. Na the figures wey you enter we dey use.

Example wey show the calculation

For 9 people at $180/hour for 60 minutes: cost per meeting = 9 × 180 × 60 ÷ 60 = $1,620. At 3 meetings per week, na $4,860 per week. Over 48 working weeks, yearly time value na $1,620 × 3 × 48 = $233,280, or 1,296 person-hours per year. Use Reset example above to get this result again.

Where e fit work and limits

This na opportunity-cost estimate from the average hourly cost wey you enter. E no necessarily be cash wey you fit avoid paying: salaried staff still get pay whether dem attend or not. Preparation time dey inside only if you add am to meeting duration. Room, tools and travel costs no dey inside. If you want total staff cost, use fully loaded hourly cost: salary plus other employment costs.

Questions wey people dey ask often

If I cancel the meeting, na cash wey I save?

No be always. Na value of staff time wey become free. Cash savings go happen only if that time would otherwise require paid overtime or contractor work.

The hourly rate suppose include extra employment costs?

Yes, if you want total staff cost. Use fully loaded rate: salary plus benefits, payroll taxes and overhead. No use only base pay.

Wetin about preparation time?

Add am to meeting duration, or calculate preparation separately and add the two results.

Why 48 working weeks?

Na example assumption: 52 weeks minus holidays and leave. Change am to match your own calendar.

If meeting shorter, the saving go reduce in the same proportion?

For this model, yes. Cost dey change directly with minutes, people and frequency. If you halve any of dem, cost go halve too.
