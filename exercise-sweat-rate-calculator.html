Formula

Estimate L = A−B+D−U and R = L/t. A and B are pre- and post-exercise mass in kg; D and U are drink and urine volumes in L; t is hours. The approximation treats a kilogram of mass change as a litre of fluid; R is in L/h.

Scope and limitations

Approximation treats 1 kg mass change as 1 L and omits respiratory/metabolic changes. This is not a fluid or sodium intake prescription.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
