Formula

For height at least 5 feet (60 inches or 152.4 cm), reference weight in kg = base + 2.3 × (height in inches − 60). The base is 50 kg for men and 45.5 kg for women. Height in inches = height in cm ÷ 2.54. This page does not extrapolate below 152.4 cm.

Worked example

Height 170 cm, female: 170 ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 inches, which is 6.93 inches above 5 feet. Reference weight = 45.5 + 2.3 × 6.93 = 45.5 + 15.94 = 61.4 kg (135.4 lb). The male base at the same height gives 65.9 kg, and the BMI at 61.4 kg and 170 cm is 21.3. Use Reset example above to reproduce this result.

Scope and limitations

Devine is a historical height-and-sex reference equation associated with drug-dosing literature. It does not account for body composition, age, pregnancy or individual health. This adult educational tool does not select a clinical dosing weight, calculate a dose or determine a healthy target. Below 152.4 cm it shows no estimate, rather than inventing a constant minimum weight.

FAQ

Is this my ideal weight?

No. It is a height-based reference number from a dosing formula. What weight suits an individual depends on factors this page does not model.

Why do men and women differ?

The formula uses different bases — 50 kg for men, 45.5 kg for women — with the same 2.3 kg per inch above 5 feet.

What happens below 5 feet?

This page does not calculate below 5 feet (152.4 cm). Returning the same base weight for every shorter adult would conceal the formula limitation. Use an individual clinical assessment when a dosing or health decision is needed.

How does this relate to BMI?

The table shows the BMI at the reference weight for your height, so you can see where the reference number sits in the standard BMI ranges.

Is my data stored?

No. This calculation runs locally in your browser and the values you enter are not sent to a CalcMintly database.

Should I aim for this number?

Treat it as a reference point for discussion, not a goal. Any weight goal should account for personal circumstances the formula cannot see.
