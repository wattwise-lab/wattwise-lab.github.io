UnitValue wey e get

All four units describe the same area. The mu value use the definition wey dey inside the formula table below.

Formula

Convert source value to square metres, then divide by target unit square-metre factor: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

UnitSquare metres for each unit
Square metre (m²)1
Square foot (ft²)0.09290304
Acre4,046.8564224
Mu666.6667

The square-foot and acre factors na exact international definitions. Mu factor 666.6667 m² na the common Chinese definition (1 mu = 2000/3 m², rounded here). Local land units and land-registration units fit use other definitions.

Example wey show the calculation

100 m² ÷ 0.09290304 m²/ft² = 1,076.391042 ft². The same 100 m² na 100 ÷ 4,046.8564224 = 0.024711 acre, and 100 ÷ 666.6667 = 0.150000 mu (display show 0.15 after rounding; value before rounding na 0.14999999 because dem don round the mu factor itself).

Where e fit work and limits

Negative area no valid. Display round to 6 decimal places, but the underlying calculation no dey rounded. Mu definition fit differ by place and time. For legal or land-registration use, confirm the definition wey your authority use — this page use 666.6667 m² for each mu throughout.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin be mu?

Chinese area unit. This page use common modern definition 1 mu = 666.6667 m² (2000/3 m²). Older or local definitions differ, so check before you use am for land documents.

How many square feet dey inside square metre?

1 m² = 10.76391041671 ft², because 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m² exactly.

How big acre be?

One acre = 4,046.8564224 m², about 0.4047 hectares. American football field (without end zones) na about 0.756 acre.

Why mu result dey show 0.15 instead of 0.14999999?

Display rounding to 6 decimal places. Full-precision value dey kept for reverse conversion shown in chips.

I fit convert hectares?

Not on this page: four units here na the ones original tool offer. Convert hectares to square metres first (1 ha = 10,000 m²) and enter that value.
