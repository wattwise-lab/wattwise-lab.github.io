Kwatancen Yanayi

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da R = v²sin(2θ)/g da t = 2v sinθ/g. v shi ne saurin jefawa a m/s, θ kusurwar dagawa ce a digiri, g kuma hanzarin nauyi ne a m/s². Nisan R yana cikin m, lokacin tashi t kuma yana cikin s, idan tsayin farawa da sauka iri daya ne kuma babu juriya ta iska.
