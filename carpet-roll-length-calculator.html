Formula

Compare L₁ = ceil(W/R)(L+t) and L₂ = ceil(L/R)(W+t), then choose the smaller requirement. L and W are room length and width, R is carpet roll width, and t is extra trim on each strip, all in m. ceil rounds the strip count up.
