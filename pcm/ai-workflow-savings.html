Net value wey accumulate as time dey pass

Case wey you wan compareValue wey e get

Each row multiply monthly net value by number of months, assuming same usage and fee throughout. Setup or training costs no dey included.

Formula

Monthly net value = people × monthly runs × minutes saved ÷ 60 × hourly cost − monthly tool fee. Hours saved = people × runs × minutes saved ÷ 60.

Example wey show the calculation

8 people, 20 runs for each person every month, 18 minutes saved for each run, cost for each hour $120, fee $999. Hours saved = 8 × 20 × 18 ÷ 60 = 48 hours. Gross value = 48 × $120 = $5,760. Net = $5,760 − $999 = $4,761 every month, or $595.13 for each person. If usage stay the same for 12 months, na $57,132 before any setup or training cost.

Where e fit work and limits

Value of time no automatically mean cash wey you save. If the free hours enter work wey no get much value or person just idle, full hourly cost go exaggerate the benefit. This model no include how many people adopt am, review and correction time, quality of output, setup and training costs, or extra per-seat fees beyond the single fee wey you enter. Measure minutes saved from timed runs before and after the tool; no just use vendor claim.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How una calculate am?

Hours saved na people times runs times minutes saved, divide by 60; gross value na hours times hourly cost; net value remove the monthly tool fee.

Na cash in bank be the net value?

Only if dem redeploy the freed time to billable or valuable work. If dem absorb am elsewhere, the value na capacity gained, not cash saved — set hourly cost to the value you actually recover.

Make the fee be for each person?

This page take single monthly fee. If your plan charge per seat, enter total monthly fee for the whole team, or divide: enter per-seat fee times team size as the fee.

How I fit measure minutes saved?

Time the same task with the tool and without am for several real runs. Include review and correction time, then use the median saving — no use only the single run wey fast pass.

Wetin happen if usage drop after first month?

Then monthly net go fall follow am. Re-run page with actual run count after one month of real use instead of trusting launch-month figure.

Wetin dem no include?

Setup, training, integration, data cleaning, quality review and any change for output quality no dey included. Tool wey save time but need plenty correction fit save less than the raw minutes suggest.
