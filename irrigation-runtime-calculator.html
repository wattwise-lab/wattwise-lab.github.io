Formula

Use V = Ad/e and t = V/q. A is area in m², d is chosen water depth in mm, e is application efficiency as a fraction, and q is total emitter flow in L/min. V is litres and t is minutes.

Scope and limitations

Assumes constant total emitter flow and the entered application efficiency. The water-depth target is chosen by you; crop needs, soil behavior and weather are not assessed.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
