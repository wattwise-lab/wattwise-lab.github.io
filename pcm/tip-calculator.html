Formula

Tip = bill × tip rate. Total = bill + tip. Total per person = total ÷ number of people. Tip per person = tip ÷ number of people.

Example wey show the calculation

Bill na $100, tip rate na 15%, for 2 people: tip = $100 × 0.15 = $15.00, total = $115.00, and total per person = $115.00 ÷ 2 = $57.50 (tip na $7.50 per person).

Where e fit work and limits

Dis version dey apply tip to di bill wey you enter; e no dey separate amounts before and after tax. If your bill already include tax and you prefer to tip on di amount before tax, enter dat pre-tax amount instead. Rounding each person share to cents fit leave small remainder.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How dis tip calculator dey work?

Tip = bill × tip rate; total per person = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people. Everybody dey pay equal share.

Dem dey calculate tip before or after tax?

Na di exact bill amount wey you type. If you want tip before tax, enter di amount before tax. If your bill already include tax, di tip for here go apply to di taxed total.

Wetin if people no split di bill equally?

Dis calculator dey split di bill equally. If people no dey split equally, calculate each person own bill share plus tip separately; use di per-person figures as starting point.

Why amounts for each person sometimes no add up exactly?

Na rounding to cents: if you split $115.00 among three people, each person go pay $38.33, wey total $114.99. For practice, one person go cover di cent wey remain.

I fit tip 0%?

Yes. Enter 0 and di total for each person go just be di bill divided by di number of people.

Dis one include service charges?

No. Any compulsory service charge wey already dey di bill na part of di bill amount. Any extra tip dey on top of am.
