Refund details & other costs

Calculated per original paid order. Refund rate is the share of orders receiving a refund; refund and recovery percentages apply to each refunded order.

Product refunds, shipping refunds, recovered product-cost value and returned processing fees are independent assumptions for a refunded order. Collected tax is neither revenue nor a refund loss. Recovery is not cash; review combinations of partial refunds and high recovery carefully.

Contribution = discounted product revenue + customer shipping − expected revenue refunds − product cost + recovered product-cost value − packaging − outbound shipping − other variable costs − payment fees + returned fees − expected return handling − ads. Monthly profit = contribution × orders − fixed costs. Required orders = round up [(fixed costs + monthly profit goal) ÷ contribution].

Scope: a planning estimate for one product or a fixed basket, not an accounting statement, cash-flow forecast or tax return. Income tax, duties, chargebacks, subscriptions and FX losses are not inserted automatically. Add applicable nonrecoverable costs to other variable or fixed costs without double counting. Fees are not rounded transaction by transaction; invoice rounding may differ.
