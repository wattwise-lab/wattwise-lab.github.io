Ƙa’ida

Matsakaicin gudu = nisa ÷ lokaci. Mita a kowace daƙiƙa = km/h ÷ 3.6. Sauri a kowace kilomita = 60 ÷ gudu a cikin km/h, a cikin mintuna a kowace kilomita.

Misalin aiki

100 km a cikin awanni 2: matsakaicin gudu = 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h. A cikin mita a kowace daƙiƙa shine 50 ÷ 3.6 = 13.89 m/s. Sauri = 60 ÷ 50 = 1.20 mintuna a kowace kilomita — wato minti 1 minti 12 daƙiƙa a kowace kilomita.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan matsakaicin gudu ne a duk tafiyar: bai faɗi komai game da saurin da kuka yi a kowane lokaci ba. Tashoshi, zirga-zirga, da tuddai duk an naɗe su cikin matsakaici. Ana yin watsi da alkibla - wannan shine gudu (scalar), ba gudu a matsayin vector ba, kodayake su biyun suna raba suna a amfani da su na yau da kullun. Lokaci dole ne ya wuce sifili; lokaci sifili ba ya ba da gudu mai ma'ana.

FAQ

Matsakaicin gudu ko saurin gaggawa?

Matsakaicin: jimlar nisa a kan jimlar lokaci. Ma'aunin gudu naka yana nuna saurin gaggawa, wanda zai iya zama mafi girma ko ƙasa a kowane lokaci. Tafiya mai tsayawa na iya samun karatun ma'aunin gudu mai girma da matsakaicin ƙasa.

Me yasa za a raba da 3.6 don m/s?

Domin 1 km/h = 1,000 mita a kowace daƙiƙa 3,600, da kuma 1,000 ÷ 3,600 = 1 ÷ 3.6. Don haka 50 km/h ÷ 3.6 = 13.89 m/s.

Menene saurin gudu, kuma me yasa mintuna a kowace kilomita?

Sauri shine juyi na gudun: lokaci a kowace nisan raka'a. Masu gudu da masu tafiya suna amfani da shi saboda "6 mintuna a kowace kilomita" ya fi sauƙin riƙewa fiye da "10 km/h".

Zan iya shigar da mil da mintuna?

Ba kai tsaye a nan ba: maida farko (1 mil = 1.60934 km, mintuna ÷ 60 = awanni). Mai sauya saurin gudu a wannan shafin yana canza sakamakon daga baya.

Shin tafiya da dawowa tana canza wani abu?

A'a, idan ka shigar da jimillar nisa da jimillar lokaci. Lura da tarko: fita 60 km/h da 40 km/h baya ba ya zuwa matsakaicin 50 km/h - ƙafar da ke jinkirin ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo, don haka matsakaicin gaskiya akan nisa daidai shine 48 km/h.

Idan lokaci sifili ne fa?

Raba ta sifili ba shi da amsa, don haka shafin yana neman lokaci sama da sifili maimakon nuna rashin iyaka.
