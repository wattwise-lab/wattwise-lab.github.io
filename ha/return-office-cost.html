Kudin Komawa Ofis

Kimanta farashin kowane wata na ranakun ofis: sufuri, ƙarin abinci, da lokacin tafiya wanda aka kimanta a farashin awa ɗaya.

Rushewa

Abudarajar

Kudin kuɗi shine sufuri tare da ƙarin abinci, lokutan kwanakin ofis. Darajar lokaci shine lokutan tafiya sau ƙimar awa ɗaya. Jimlar ta haɗa duka biyun.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sanduna zuwa mafi girman sashi, yana nuna ko sufuri, abinci, ko lokaci ya mamaye farashin wata-wata.

Kudin a ƙididdigar ranakun ofis daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowace layi tana amfani da jigilar ku ta yau da kullun, abinci, mintuna, da ƙimar sa'a kuma tana bambanta ne kawai a ranakun ofis, don haka farashin yana daidai da ranakun ofis.

Ƙa’ida

Kudin wata-wata = kwanakin ofis × (sufurin yau da kullun + ƙarin abinci + mintunan tafiya ÷ 60 × ƙimar awa). Kudin kowace rana ofis = sufuri na yau da kullun + ƙarin abinci + mintunan tafiya ÷ 60 × ƙimar awa.

Misalin aiki

Kwanakin ofis 12, sufuri $55, ƙarin abinci $35, mintunan tafiya 100, ƙimar awa $80. Kowace rana: $55 + $35 + 100 ÷ 60 × $80 = $90 + $133.33 = $223.33. Kowane wata = 12 × $223.33 = $2,680. Rabin kuɗi = 12 × $90 = $1,080; rabin lokaci = 12 × $133.33 = $1,600; lokacin tafiya = awanni 20 a wata.

Amfani da iyakoki

Sakamakon ya haɗa kuɗaɗen kuɗi da ƙimar lokaci: ba cikakken kuɗin da aka kashe ba ne, kuma rabin kuɗin kawai shine $1,080 a cikin misalin da aka yi aiki. Shigar da kuɗaɗen bayan duk wani biyan kuɗi, ko kuma an yi ƙarin bayani game da rabin kuɗin. Fa'idodin aiki, haɗin gwiwa, yanayin ofis, da tasirin lafiya a kowane bangare suna waje da wannan samfurin, kamar yadda tufafi, safa a cikin abin hawa suka wuce shigarwar sufuri, da filin ajiye motoci sai dai idan an haɗa su a ciki.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Kowace rana ta ofis tana kashe kuɗin sufuri na yau da kullun tare da ƙarin abinci da mintuna na tafiya da aka raba sau 60 darajar kowace awa; ninka ta kwanakin ofis a kowane wata.

Shin jimlar kuɗin da na kashe ne kawai?

A'a. Rabin kuɗin da na kashe ne kawai (sufuri da abinci) ke fita daga walat ɗinku. Lokacin rabin ƙimar yana tafiya a sa'o'i a ƙimar kowace awa - saita ƙimar kowace awa zuwa sifili don adadi na kuɗi kawai.

Me ake ɗauka a matsayin ƙarin abinci?

Kashe kuɗin abinci ya fi abin da za ku kashe wajen aiki daga gida: siyan abincin rana, kofi, da kayan ciye-ciye, da kuma abincin gida da ba za ku ci ba. Kada ku ƙididdige cikakken kuɗin abincin rana idan za ku ci abincin rana ko ta yaya.

Shin sufuri zai kasance bayan an biya kuɗin?

Ee. Shigar da abin da kuke biya bayan duk wani tallafin tafiya na ma'aikaci ko rangwamen izinin shiga, ko kuma an ƙara farashin.

Me zai faru idan tafiyar tawa ta yi amfani?

Sannan kimanta kowane minti a cikakken kuɗin sa'a ya wuce farashin. Rage darajar sa'a don lokacin tafiya, ko kuma ƙidaya rabon da ba shi da amfani na tafiyar a cikin mintuna kawai.

Shin wannan yana nufin cewa aikin ofis bai cancanci ba?

A'a. Yana da farashi a gefe ɗaya na shawarar. Haɗin gwiwa, jagoranci, gani, da fifikon kai fa'idodi ne na gaske waɗanda wannan tsarin kuɗi da lokaci ba ya farashi.
