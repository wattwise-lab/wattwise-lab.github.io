Duk ma'auni na wannan zafin jiki

Sikelidarajar

Duk layuka uku suna bayyana yanayin zafi iri ɗaya. Ana ƙin ƙimar da ke ƙasa da sifili, ba a canza ta ba.

Ƙa’ida

Canza yanayin zafin tushen zuwa Celsius, sannan a yi amfani da lissafin ma'aunin manufa.

CanzawaƘa’ida
°F to °C°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
°C to °F°F = °C × 9/5 + 32
°C to KK = °C + 273.15
K to °C°C = K − 273.15

Sifili cikakke shine 0 K = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F. Duk wani shigarwar da ke nuna ƙimar Kelvin mara kyau ba zai yiwu ba a zahiri kuma an ƙi shi.

Misalin aiki

25 °C × 9/5 + 32 = 77 °F, da kuma 25 + 273.15 = 298.15 K. Sikelin ya haɗu a −40: −40 × 9/5 + 32 = −40 °F.

Amfani da iyakoki

Bambance-bambancen zafin jiki ba iri ɗaya bane da cikakken yanayin zafi: hauhawar 1 °C daidai yake da hauhawar 1 K amma hauhawar 1.8 °F. Wannan shafin yana canza cikakken yanayin zafi, ba bambance-bambance ba. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6. Zagaye na iya shafar adadi na ƙarshe da aka nuna, kamar yadda kayan aikin tushe ya lura.

FAQ

Menene cikakken sifili?

0 K = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F — yanayin zafi mafi sanyi. Wannan shafin yana ƙin yarda da duk wani abu mai sanyi a matsayin shigarwa mara inganci.

Me yasa °C da °F suke haɗuwa a −40?

Magance C = C × 9/5 + 32 yana ba da C = −40, kawai zafin jiki mai lamba ɗaya a kan sikelin biyu.

Shin 0 °C iri ɗaya ne da 32 °F?

Ee: ruwa yana daskarewa a 0 °C = 32 °F = 273.15 K a matsin lamba na yanayi.

Ta yaya zan canza bambancin zafin jiki?

Don bambanci, sauke matakan +32/+273.15: 10 °C na ɗumama = 18 °F na ɗumama = 10 K na ɗumama.

Me yasa 273.15 ba 273 ba?

273.15 shine ainihin ma'aunin Celsius da Kelvin bisa ga ma'anar kelvin.
