UnitValue wey e get

All four units na di same volume dem describe. Gallons for here na US liquid gallons, no be UK imperial gallons.

Formula

First convert di original value to litres, then divide by di litre factor for di unit wey you want: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

UnitLitres for each unit
Litre (L)1
Millilitre (mL)0.001
Cubic metre (m³)1,000
US liquid gallon (gal)3.785411784

Di US gallon factor na exact by definition. UK imperial gallon big pass (4.54609 L), and dem no use am for here.

Example wey show the calculation

1 L ÷ 3.785411784 L/gal = 0.264172 US gal, and 1 L = 1,000 mL = 0.001 m³. For di other direction, 10 US gal × 3.785411784 = 37.854118 L.

Where e fit work and limits

Di page no dey accept negative volumes. Dem round di displayed values to 6 decimal places; di actual calculation no dey rounded. If your recipe or tank dey use imperial gallons, multiply di US-gallon result by 0.832674 to get imperial gallons (because 3.785411784 ÷ 4.54609 = 0.832674).

Questions wey people dey ask often

Dis na US or UK gallon?

Na US liquid gallon (3.785411784 L). UK imperial gallon na 4.54609 L, about 20% bigger. To mix dem up na di most common volume-conversion error.

How many litres dey inside one cubic metre?

Exactly 1,000 litres.

How many millilitres dey inside one litre?

Exactly 1,000 mL.

Wetin about fluid ounces?

No be for dis page: 1 US fluid ounce = 0.0295735295625 L. First convert fluid ounces to litres, then enter dat value.

Temperature dey change di result?

No. Dis na only unit conversion. Real liquids dey expand with temperature, but dat one na physical measurement matter, no be unit conversion.
