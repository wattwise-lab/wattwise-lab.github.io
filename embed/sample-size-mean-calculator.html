Formula

Use n₀ = (zσ/E)². σ is assumed population deviation, E is desired half-width, and z is the two-sided normal critical value. For finite population N, use n = n₀/[1+(n₀−1)/N]; otherwise keep the uncorrected value. Round up to a whole sample count.
