Duk raka'a don wannan ƙimar

Naúrardarajar

Duk raka'a huɗu suna bayyana girma ɗaya; galan a nan galan ruwa ne US, ba galan imperial ba UK.

Ƙa’ida

A mayar da ƙimar tushe zuwa lita, sannan a raba ta da ma'aunin lita na na'urar da aka nufa: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

NaúrarLita a kowace raka'a
Lita (L)1
Millilitre (mL)0.001
Mita mai siffar cubic (m³)1,000
US galan mai ruwa (gal)3.785411784

Ma'aunin galan US daidai yake da ma'anarsa. Galan UK imperial ya fi girma (4.54609 L) kuma ba a amfani da shi a nan.

Misalin aiki

1 L ÷ 3.785411784 L/gal = 0.264172 US gal, da kuma 1 L = 1,000 mL = 0.001 m³. Akasin haka, 10 US gal × 3.785411784 = 37.854118 L.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ana ƙin ƙarar da ba daidai ba a matsayin mara inganci. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6; lissafin da ke ƙasa ba a zagaye shi ba. Idan girke-girke ko tankin ku yana amfani da galan na imperial, ninka sakamakon galan na US da 0.832674 don samun galan na imperial (tunda 3.785411784 ÷ 4.54609 = 0.832674).

FAQ

Shin wannan galan na US ne ko UK?

US galan ruwa (3.785411784 L). Galan UK imperial shine 4.54609 L, kusan 20% ya fi girma - haɗa su shine kuskuren da aka fi sani da sauya girma.

Lita nawa a cikin mita mai siffar cubic?

lita na 1,000, daidai.

Mililita nawa a cikin lita?

1,000 mL, daidai.

Yaya batun oza mai ruwa?

Ba a wannan shafin ba: 1 US oza mai ruwa = 0.0295735295625 L. Da farko a mayar da oza mai ruwa zuwa lita sannan a shigar da wannan ƙimar.

Shin zafin jiki yana canza sakamakon?

A'a - wannan shine juyar da naúrar tsarkakakke. Ruwan gaske yana faɗaɗa tare da zafin jiki, amma wannan tambayar aunawa ce ta zahiri, ba juyawa naúrar ba.
