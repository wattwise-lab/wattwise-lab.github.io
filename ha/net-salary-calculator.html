Rushewa

Adadin

Duk alkaluma suna kowane wata sai dai idan an yi musu alama a kowace shekara. Matsakaicin Albashi shine ƙimar da ta dace da ka shigar - ba ƙimar doka ce ta kowace hukuma ba.

A kallo ɗaya

Matsakaicin Albashi da raguwa daidai yake da jimlar Albashi. Tsawon sandar cire kuɗi yana nufin ƙasa da rabin jimlar Albashi ya isa gare ku.

Matsakaicin Albashi a ƙimar haraji mai inganci daban-daban

Matsakaicin Albashi Mai InganciMatsakaicin Albashi / Wata

Matsakaicin Albashi da sauran raguwa ana riƙe su a ƙimar da ke sama; kawai ƙimar haraji tana canzawa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

An kiyasta harajin = jimlar albashi × ƙimar haraji mai tasiri. Albashi mai yawa = jimlar albashi − harajin da aka kiyasta − gudummawar zamantakewa − wasu raguwa. Albashi mai yawa a kowace shekara = jimlar albashi a wata × 12.

Misalin aiki

Jimlar albashi $6,000 a wata, ƙimar haraji mai tasiri 15%, gudummawar zamantakewa $500, wasu raguwa $0: ƙimar haraji = $6,000 × 0.15 = $900, jimlar albashi = $6,000 − $900 − $500 − $0 = $4,600 a wata, ko $55,200 a kowace shekara.

Amfani da iyakoki

Tsarin haraji ya bambanta dangane da wuri da yanayin mutum: ba a tsara ma'auni, alawus, maki, gudummawar ma'aikaci, da fa'idodi ba. Shigar da ƙimar inganci daga lissafin albashin ku ko lissafin albashi kuma tabbatar da mahimman ƙididdiga akan lissafin albashi na hukuma. Wannan kimantawa ce, ba takardar biyan albashi ko haraji ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya lissafin albashin da aka yi amfani da shi yake aiki?

Albashi mai yawa = jimlar albashi - harajin da aka kiyasta - gudunmawar zamantakewa - wasu ragi, inda aka kiyasta harajin = jimlar albashi × ƙimar harajin da aka yi amfani da shi.

Wane ƙimar haraji mai tasiri ya kamata in shigar?

Ainihin harajin ku ya raba ta hanyar jimlar albashin ku daga takardar biyan albashi ta kwanan nan, ko ƙimar da sashen albashin ku ya tabbatar. Babu ƙimar ƙasa ko yanki da aka riga aka cika, saboda ƙimar doka da maƙallan sun bambanta sosai da za a ɗauka.

Me yasa albashin da aka yi amfani da shi ba shi da kyau a cikin lissafina?

Ragewa ya wuce jimlar albashi a ƙimar da aka shigar. Duba adadin ƙimar da ragi - sakamakon da ba shi da kyau yana nuna matsalar shigarwa, ba ainihin takardar biyan albashi ba.

An haɗa gudummawar ma'aikata?

A'a. Ragewa daga jimlar albashin ku kawai ake ƙididdigewa. Gudummawar da ma'aikaci ke bayarwa wani abu ne daban kuma ba ya rage albashin ku a nan.

Shin wannan yana magance kari ko ƙarin lokaci?

Sai dai idan kun ninka su cikin jimlar albashin wata-wata da kuka shigar. Ya kamata a daidaita ko a ƙididdige albashin da ba na yau da kullun ba don takamaiman watan.

Zan iya amfani da wannan don shigar da haraji?

A'a. Kiyasin ɗaukar gida ne. Shigarwa ya dogara da cikakkun ƙa'idodi, alawus, da maki.
