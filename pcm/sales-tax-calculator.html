Formula

Tax = subtotal × tax rate. Total = subtotal + tax. Na so we dey calculate am.

Example wey show the calculation

Subtotal $100, tax rate 8.25%: tax = $100 × 0.0825 = $8.25, and total = $100 + $8.25 = $108.25.

Where e fit work and limits

Rates, items wey tax cover, sourcing rules and local extra taxes dey vary by jurisdiction. Some items fit no get tax or fit get different rate. Confirm the rate wey apply to the actual transaction — this calculator dey apply one rate to the whole subtotal.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How the sales tax calculator dey work?

Tax = subtotal × tax rate; total = subtotal + tax. We apply one rate to the whole subtotal wey you enter.

Which tax rate I suppose enter?

Na the rate wey apply to the transaction — e dey vary by jurisdiction and sometimes by item type. Confirm am from the seller's checkout or your local tax authority; we no assume a rate here.

What if some items no need tax?

Enter only subtotal wey tax apply to. Remove exempt items from the subtotal, or calculate dem separately and add dem to the total afterwards.

This one handle prices wey already include tax?

No — e dey add tax to subtotal wey no include tax. If your price already include tax, the tax part na price − price ÷ (1 + rate), and na different calculation be that.

Why the total dey round to cents?

We show money totals to two decimal places. If your jurisdiction dey round tax per line item instead of on the subtotal, result fit differ by a cent or two.

Na filing or compliance calculation be this?

No. Na estimate for one transaction. Filing, registration and sourcing rules no dey within its scope.
