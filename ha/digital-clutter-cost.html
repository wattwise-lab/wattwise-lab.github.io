Rushewa

Kudin ajiya vs ƙimar lokacin bincike

An auna sanduna zuwa babban ɓangaren. Lokacin bincike shine farashin dama da aka kimanta akan adadin sa'a, ba tsabar kuɗin da kuka biya ba.

Kudin shekara-shekara a mintuna daban-daban na bincike na mako-mako

Minti na bincike na mako-makodarajar

Kudin ajiya iri ɗaya da ƙimar sa'a; mintunan bincike na mako-mako ne kawai ke canzawa.

Ƙa’ida

Darajar shekara-shekara = kuɗin ajiya na wata-wata × 12 + mintunan bincike na mako-mako ÷ ​​60 × 52 × ƙimar sa'a. Matsakaicin wata-wata = ƙimar shekara-shekara ÷ 12. Biyan kuɗi na tsaftacewa = lokutan tsaftacewa × ƙimar sa'a ÷ matsakaicin wata-wata.

Misalin aiki

Ajiyar wata-wata 68, 45 mintunan bincike a kowane mako, ƙimar awa 80, tsaftacewa 6 awanni: ajiya = 68 × 12 = 816.00; lokacin bincike = 45 ÷ 60 × 52 = 39 awanni a kowace shekara, wanda aka kimanta a 39 × 80 = 3,120.00; farashin shekara = 816.00 + 3,120.00 = 3,936.00. Ƙimar tsaftacewa = 6 × 80 = 480.00; matsakaicin wata-wata = 3,936.00 ÷ 12 = 328.00; biya = 480.00 ÷ 328.00 = 1.46 watanni. Waɗannan misalai ne na shigarwa - ƙimar awa-awanku hukunci ne, ba ƙimar kasuwa ba.

Amfani da iyakoki

Lokacin bincike kuɗi ne na dama, kuma samfurin bai tabbatar da cewa tsaftacewa zai kawar da shi ba: tsaftacewa na iya rage lokacin bincike ba tare da cire shi ba, kuma adadin diyya yana ɗauka cewa an dawo da cikakken matsakaicin wata-wata. Kuɗin ajiya kuɗin kuɗi ne; ƙimar lokacin bincike ba kuɗi ba ne sai dai idan an yi amfani da lokacin da aka keɓe a zahiri don samar da aiki. Darajar sa'a ya kamata ta nuna darajar lokacin ku a gare ku, ba da'awar albashi ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Darajar shekara-shekara = kuɗin ajiya na wata-wata × 12 + mintunan bincike na mako-mako ÷ ​​60 × 52 × ƙimar sa'a. Biyan kuɗi na tsaftacewa yana raba ƙimar lokacin tsaftacewa da matsakaicin wata-wata.

Shin adadin lokacin bincike kuɗi ne na gaske?

A'a. Kudin dama ne: ƙimar lokacin da aka kashe wajen bincike, a adadin sa'a da kuka shigar. Yana zama kuɗi ne kawai idan kun yi amfani da lokacin da aka keɓe don samun kuɗi ko adanawa.

Wane darajar sa'a ya kamata in shigar?

Duk abin da lokacinka ya kai ka ga wannan shawara — shawarar yanke hukunci ce, ba albashi ko ƙimar kasuwa ba. Gwada ƙima mai ƙanƙanta da babba don ganin yadda amsar ke motsawa.

Shin tsaftacewa za ta biya da gaske a cikin watannin da aka nuna?

Sai dai idan tsaftacewa ta cire lokacin bincike da aka yi amfani da shi. Tsarin bai tabbatar da cewa tsaftacewa zai kawar da lokacin bincike ba; ɗauki fansa a matsayin adadi idan yana aiki.

Ta yaya zan kimanta mintunan bincike na mako-mako?

Bi diddigin mako na yau da kullun da gaske: neman fayiloli, hotuna, imel da kalmomin shiga. Littafin tarihin mako guda ya fi adadin zagaye da aka yi tsammani.
