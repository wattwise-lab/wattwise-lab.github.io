Rushewa

Abudarajar

Ana nuna maki na sassa kafin a matse su. Sakamakon ƙarshe shine jimlar da aka manne zuwa kewayon 0–100.

A kallo ɗaya

An auna sanduna zuwa maki 40 don haka za a iya kwatanta abubuwan da aka haɗa; an rufe girman a maki 30, don haka sandar sa ba za ta taɓa kaiwa saman wannan sikelin ba.

Me zai motsa maki?

Yanayidarajar

Kowane layi yana canza shigarwa ɗaya daga yanayinka kuma yana riƙe sauran, don haka zaka iya ganin wane ci gaba ne ya fi muhimmanci. Waɗannan maki ne na zato, ba hasashe ba.

Ƙa’ida

Maki = matsewa(4 × bayyananniyar doka + 3 × digitization + min(30, mita × mintuna ÷ 600 × 3) − 2 × haɗari, 0, 100). Duk ƙimar uku suna kan sikelin 0–10 kuma ana matse su zuwa wannan kewayon kafin a ci maki.

Dokokin maki (cikakken saitin ƙa'ida)

BangarenDokarMatsakaicin
Tsabtace dokaMaki 4 a kowace maki (0–10)40
Tsarin dijitalMaki 3 a kowace maki (0–10)30
Ƙarar girmaMita × mintuna ÷ 600 × 3, an iyakance shi a 30 — mintuna 600 (10 awanni) a kowane wata yana samun maki 3, don haka awanni 100 suna samun iyaka30
HadariRage maki 2 a kowace maki (0–10)−20
JimillaJimilla, sannan a manne zuwa 0–100100

Ƙungiyoyin da aka yi amfani da su a hukuncin: 70–100 ɗan takara mai ƙarfi, 40–69 sake dubawa da farko, ƙasa da 40 rashin daidaito. Ƙungiyoyin sune karatun wannan shafin nasa na maki, ba iyaka mai inganci ba.

Misalin aiki

Mita 80 tana gudana a kowane wata, mintuna 15 a kowace gudu, bayyananniyar doka 8, digitization 7, haɗari 4. Maki na doka = 4 × 8 = 32. Maki na digitization = 3 × 7 = 21. Ƙara = 80 × 15 ÷ 600 × 3 = 6, ƙasa da maƙallin 30. Rage haɗari = 2 × 4 = 8. Jimilla = 32 + 21 + 6 − 8 = 51 daga 100 — wani yanayi mai gauraya a cikin rukunin bita na 40–69. Lokacin kowane wata akan aikin shine 80 × 15 = mintuna 1,200 (awanni 20). Lura: shafin tushe ya yiwa wannan sakamakon alama a cikin mintuna; Naúrar maki ce daga 100, kuma wannan shafin yana gyara wannan lakabin.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan jerin abubuwan da aka tsara musamman ne, ba sakamakon kimiyya da aka tabbatar ba kuma ba hasashen nasarar sarrafa kansa ba ne. Nauyin (4, 3, ƙimar girma 30, haɗari 2) da kuma madannin 70/40 zaɓuɓɓuka ne na tunani da aka yi wa wannan shafin; canza su yana canza maki. Ba ya tantance farashin aiwatarwa, ƙimar keɓancewa, ingancin bayanai, sarrafa canji, ko ƙuntatawa na ƙa'idoji - babban maki har yanzu yana buƙatar gwaji, kuma ƙaramin maki har yanzu yana iya zama darajar sarrafa kansa don dalilai na bin ƙa'idodi ko ɗaukar hoto.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige maki?

Lokutan bayyana dokoki 4, da lokutan dijital 3, da maki na girma (lokutan mita da aka raba da 600, lokutan 3, an rufe su a 30), an rage lokutan haɗari 2, tare da jimlar da aka matse tsakanin 0 da 100. Cikakken teburin doka yana cikin sashin Tsarin.

Me ƙungiyoyin mawaƙa ke nufi?

Ana karanta 70–100 a matsayin ɗan takara mai ƙarfi, 40–69 a matsayin bita da farko, ƙasa da 40 a matsayin mara ƙarfi. Waɗannan su ne ƙungiyoyin wannan shafin don karanta maki, ba ƙa'idodi da aka tabbatar daga waje ba.

Me yasa aka rufe ƙarar a 30?

Don haka ƙarar da ba ta da ƙarfi ba za ta iya wuce ƙa'idodi marasa tabbas ko shigarwar da ba a san ta ba. Aikin da ake gudanarwa akai-akai amma ake sarrafa shi ta hanyar kiran hukunci bai kamata ya wuce aiki mai haske, mai dijital, mai ƙaramin girma ta hanyar ƙara kawai ba.

Me yasa ake cire haɗari har zuwa maki 20 kawai?

Domin haɗari yana ɗaya daga cikin abubuwa huɗu, ba na veto ba. Aikin da ke da haɗari sosai har yanzu yana iya samun maki a cikin rukunin bita idan komai yana da ƙarfi - amma a lokacin ne ya kamata a yi la'akari da ƙirar ɗan adam, maimakon cikakken sarrafa kansa.

Ta yaya zan kimanta tsabtar doka da kuma dijital da gaskiya?

Tsabtace dokoki 10 yana nufin an rubuta matakan yanke shawara kuma mutane biyu za su kai ga sakamako iri ɗaya; digitization 10 yana nufin shigarwa da fitarwa sun riga sun kasance bayanai masu tsari. Idan ɗayan yana buƙatar hukunci ko sarrafa takarda, rage ƙimar sa kuma ku lura da dalili.

Shin babban maki yana tabbatar da tanadi?

A'a. Ya ce aikin yana kama da wanda za a iya sarrafa shi ta atomatik, ba wai aikin zai biya ba. Kuɗi, sarrafa keɓancewa, da kulawa har yanzu suna buƙatar kimantawarsu kafin su aiwatar.
