Formula

Flat interest is I = Pjn/12; reducing-balance payment is A = P/a(j/12,n). P is principal, j is the nominal annual interest fraction, and n is months. a(r,n) sums the monthly discount factors at rate r.

Scope and limitations

Same quoted rates do not mean equal borrowing costs. Fees and lender rounding are omitted.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.
