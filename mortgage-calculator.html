What is included

The monthly total combines principal and interest with the property tax, insurance, PMI, HOA, and extra-principal assumptions you enter. PMI is included only while the starting LTV is above 80% in this simplified model.

Not included

Closing costs, lender fees, points, changing escrow amounts, adjustable rates, tax deductions, appreciation, maintenance, and refinancing costs. Confirm final numbers with the lender and closing documents.
