Duk raka'a don wannan ƙimar

Naúrardarajar

Duk raka'a biyar suna bayyana adadin bayanai iri ɗaya a ƙarƙashin tushen da kuka zaɓa; sauya tushen yana canza kowane layi.

Ƙa’ida

Baiti = ƙimar × tushematakin tushe; ƙimar manufa = bytes ÷ tushematakin manufa, inda matakan suke B=0, KB=1, MB=2, GB=3, TB=4.

A takaice dai, KiB/MiB/GiB sune raka'o'in tushe-1024 kuma KB/MB/GB sune raka'o'in tushe-1000 SI. A aikace, yawancin tsarin aiki suna da lakabin tushe-1024 kamar KB/MB/GB; wannan shafin yana bin wannan lakabin gama gari kuma yana ba ku damar canza tushe a sarari maimakon ɓoye bambancin.

Misalin aiki

1 GB a tushe 1024 = 1 × 1024 = 1,024 MB, = 1,024 × 1,024 = 1,048,576 KB, = 1,073,741,824 bytes. Daidai 1 GB a tushe 1000 = 1,000 MB daidai. Tuki na 2.5 TB a tushe 1024 shine 2.5 × 1,024 = 2,560 GB.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ana ƙin girman da ba daidai ba a matsayin mara inganci. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6; lissafin da ke ƙasa ba a zagaye shi ba. Ba a tsara ƙarfin tuki da aka tsara, tsarin fayil, da kuma takaddamar suna KiB-vs-KB ba - wannan shafin yana canza lambar da kuka shigar, babu wani abu.

FAQ

Shin ya kamata in yi amfani da tushe 1024 ko 1000?

Yi amfani da 1000 don takamaiman bayani game da SI/masana'anta (drive da aka sayar a matsayin 1 TB yawanci yana nufin 10^12 bytes) da 1024 don girman da yawancin tsarin aiki ke bayarwa. Idan kuna cikin shakku, duba wanne tushen ku ya yi amfani da shi - gibin ya kai kusan 10% a sikelin TB.

Menene bambanci tsakanin GB da GiB?

GiB tushe ne ba tare da wata shakka ba 1024 (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes); GB shine sashin SI tushe-1000. Kayan aiki da yawa suna rubuta GB lokacin da suke nufin GiB, shi ya sa wannan shafin ya sanya tushen zaɓi a bayyane.

Me yasa tuƙin 1 TB dina yake nuna ƙasa da 1 TB?

Wani ɓangare na wannan bambancin tushe: 10^12 bytes = 931.32 GiB a tushe 1024. Tsarin fayil na sama yana lissafin sauran.

Nawa MB a cikin GB?

1,024 MB a tushe 1024, ko 1,000 MB a tushe 1000 - amsar ta dogara ne akan tushe, don haka koyaushe a faɗi shi.

Shin wannan yana aiki akan bits?

A'a — bytes kawai. 1 byte = 8 bit; da farko juya bit zuwa bytes (raba da 8) sannan ka shigar da wannan ƙimar.
