Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da cibiyar Wilson C = (p+z²/(2n))/(1+z²/n) da rabin faɗin H = z√[p(1−p)/n+z²/(4n²)]/(1+z²/n). p nasarori ne da aka raba ta gwaji, n gwaje-gwaje ne, kuma z shine ƙimar mahimmanci ta al'ada mai gefe biyu. Iyakoki sune C−H da C+H, waɗanda aka nuna a matsayin kashi-kashi.
