All four units describe the same speed; the mile and knot factors below are exact definitions.

Formula

Convert the source value to metres per second, then divide by the target unit's m/s factor: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

UnitMetres per second per unit
Kilometre per hour (km/h)0.2777777778 (1/3.6)
Metre per second (m/s)1
Mile per hour (mph)0.44704
Knot (kn)0.5144444444 (1852/3600)

1 mph = 0.44704 m/s exactly (from 1 mile = 1,609.344 m). 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = 1,852 m per hour, exactly.

Worked example

100 km/h = 100 × (1/3.6) = 27.777778 m/s, and 27.777778 ÷ 0.44704 = 62.137119 mph. The same speed is 27.777778 ÷ 0.5144444444 = 53.995680 knots.

Scope and limitations

Negative speeds are rejected as invalid (direction is not modelled; use a signed value only if you track direction yourself). Display values are rounded to 6 decimal places; the underlying calculation is not rounded.

FAQ

How fast is 100 km/h in mph?

62.137119 mph — the worked example above shows each step.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile per hour. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 m, so 1 knot = 0.5144444444 m/s. It is the standard unit at sea and in aviation.

Why is mph not a round number in m/s?

Because the mile (1,609.344 m) and the hour do not divide evenly: 1,609.344 ÷ 3,600 = 0.44704 m/s exactly.

Is km/h to mph just multiply by 0.62?

Approximately: the exact factor is 0.6213711922. Multiplying by 0.62 gives 62 mph for 100 km/h — close, but 0.137 mph low.

Can I convert pace (minutes per km)?

No — pace is the inverse of speed and needs its own calculation. This page converts speed units only.
