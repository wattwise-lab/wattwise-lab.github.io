Formula

Area = length × width. Perimeter = 2 × (length + width). Purchase area = area × (1 + waste rate).

Worked example

Length 6 m, width 4 m: area = 6 × 4 = 24 m², perimeter = 2 × (6 + 4) = 20 m. At an 8% waste allowance, waste area = 24 × 0.08 = 1.92 m², so purchase area = 25.92 m² (279 ft²). These are the example defaults above; replace them with your own measurements.

Scope and limitations

This covers one rectangle only. Split irregular spaces into measured rectangles and add them; verify package coverage and cut-pattern waste for your material (flooring, tiles and wallpaper all waste differently). No price is calculated and no package size is assumed.

FAQ

How does the square area calculator work?

Area = length × width; purchase area = area × (1 + waste rate). Perimeter = 2 × (length + width).

What if my room is not a rectangle?

Split irregular spaces into measured rectangles and add them. L-shaped rooms are two rectangles, not one averaged rectangle.

Why is there a waste allowance for area?

Cuts, offcuts, pattern matching and damaged pieces all consume material beyond the exact floor or wall area. The percentage depends on the material and layout.

What is the perimeter for?

Perimeter is useful for edging, skirting, trim and border materials, which are bought by length rather than area.

Can I use feet instead of metres?

Yes, as long as length and width use the same unit: the area comes out in that unit squared. The breakdown also shows the purchase area in ft² when you enter metres.

Does CalcMintly store my inputs?

No. This calculation runs locally in your browser and the values you enter are not sent to a CalcMintly database.
