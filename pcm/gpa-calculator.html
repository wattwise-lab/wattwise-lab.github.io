Formula

GPA = sum of (grade points × course credits) ÷ total credits. Na the credits dey determine each course weight, so a 4-credit course go count twice as much as a 2-credit course.

Example wey show the calculation

Grades 3.7 (3 credits), 3.3 (3 credits), 4.0 (4 credits) and 3.0 (2 credits): points na 11.1 + 9.9 + 16.0 + 6.0 = 43.0, over 12 credits, so GPA = 43.0 ÷ 12 = 3.5833. Use Reset example above to reproduce this result, then replace values with your own.

Where e fit work and limits

This page dey use grade points wey you enter on top 4.0 scale; e no dey convert letter grades, because schools dey use different conversions. E no dey apply honours/AP weighting, repeated-course replacement, or pass/fail rules — apply your institution policy before you enter grade points. Course wey dem leave blank or get 0 credits excluded. Display rounded to 4 decimal places.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How I fit enter letter grade?

Convert am using your school scale first (for example, A = 4.0, B = 3.0 on many 4.0 scales), then enter resulting grade points. This page no dey guess conversion.

Why credits dey affect the weight of GPA?

Course wey worth more credits represent more work, so im grade count proportionally more. Simple average of grades go give 2-credit and 4-credit course equal weight, which no be how GPA dey work.

Wetin if my courses pass four?

Join the points and credits from all courses: GPA still na total points ÷ total credits. You fit enter four courses each time, then add the separate totals together.

Wetin go happen to course wey I leave blank?

We no go count am at all; e no go add points or credits.

This one fit handle weighted GPA for honours/AP courses?

Only if you enter already-weighted grade points. Page itself no dey apply extra weighting.
