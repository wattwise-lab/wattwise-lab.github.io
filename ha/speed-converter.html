Duk raka'a don wannan ƙimar

Naúrardarajar

Duk raka'a huɗu suna bayyana gudu ɗaya; ma'aunin mil da ƙulli da ke ƙasa ma'anoni ne daidai.

Ƙa’ida

Maida ƙimar tushe zuwa mita a kowace daƙiƙa, sannan a raba ta da ma'aunin m/s na na'urar da aka nufa: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

NaúrarMita a kowace daƙiƙa a kowace raka'a
Kilomita a kowace awa (km/h)0.2777777778 (1/3.6)
Mita a kowace daƙiƙa (m/s)1
Mil a kowace awa (mph)0.44704
Kulli (kn)0.5144444444 (1852/3600)

1 mph = 0.44704 m/s daidai (daga 1 mil = 1,609.344 m). 1 kulli = 1 mil na ruwa a kowace awa = 1,852 m a kowace awa, daidai.

Misalin aiki

100 km/h = 100 × (1/3.6) = 27.777778 m/s, da kuma 27.777778 ÷ 0.44704 = 62.137119 mph. Gudu iri ɗaya shine 27.777778 ÷ 0.5144444444 = 53.995680 knots.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ana ƙin gudu mara kyau a matsayin mara inganci (ba a yi koyi da alkibla ba; yi amfani da ƙimar da aka sanya hannu kawai idan ka bi diddigin alkibla da kanka). Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa 6 wurare na adadi; lissafin da ke ƙasa ba a zagaye shi ba.

FAQ

Yaya saurin 100 km/h yake a mph?

62.137119 mph — misalin da aka yi aiki a sama yana nuna kowane mataki.

Menene kulli?

Mil ɗaya na ruwa a kowace awa. Mil ɗin ruwa daidai yake da 1,852 m, don haka 1 knot = 0.5144444444 m/s. Shine ma'aunin da aka saba amfani da shi a teku da kuma a fannin jiragen sama.

Me yasa mil (mph) ba lamba ce mai zagaye a m/s ba?

Domin mil (1,609.344 m) da sa'ar ba sa raba daidai: 1,609.344 ÷ 3,600 = 0.44704 m/s daidai.

Shin km/h zuwa mph kawai ana ninka shi da 0.62?

Kimanin: ainihin ma'aunin shine 0.6213711922. Ninkuwa da 0.62 yana ba da 62 mph don 100 km/h — kusa, amma 0.137 mph ƙasa.

Zan iya canza saurin (minti a kowace km)?

A'a — saurin gudu shine akasin saurin gudu kuma yana buƙatar lissafin kansa. Wannan shafin yana canza na'urorin gudu kawai.
