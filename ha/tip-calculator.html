Rushewa

Adadin

Ana amfani da tip ɗin ga adadin lissafin da kuka shigar. Ba a raba adadin kafin haraji da bayan haraji a nan ba.

A kallo ɗaya

An zana sandar tip akan lissafin don haka ana iya ganin rabon tip na jimlar.

Jimlar kowane mutum a farashin tip daban-daban

Kudin tipKowane mutum

Ana riƙe lissafin kuɗi da girman jam'iyya a ƙimar da ke sama; ƙimar tip kawai tana canzawa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Tip = lissafin kuɗi × ƙimar tip. Jimilla = lissafin kuɗi + tip. Jimilla ga kowane mutum = jimillar ÷ adadin mutane. Tip ga kowane mutum = tip ÷ adadin mutane.

Misalin aiki

Lissafi $100, ƙimar tip 15%, 2 mutane: tip = $100 × 0.15 = $15.00, jimilla = $115.00, da jimillar kowane mutum = $115.00 ÷ 2 = $57.50 (tip $7.50 ga kowane mutum).

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan sigar ta yi amfani da tip ɗin ga lissafin da aka shigar kuma ba ta bambanta adadin kafin haraji da bayan haraji ba. Idan lissafin ku ya riga ya haɗa da haraji kuma kuna son ba da tip akan adadin kafin haraji, shigar da adadin kafin haraji. Mayar da hankali zuwa cents ga kowane mutum zai iya barin ƙaramin saura.

FAQ

Ta yaya kalkuleta tip ɗin yake aiki?

Tip = lissafin × ƙimar tukwici; jimillar kowane mutum = (lissafi + tip) ÷ adadin mutane. Kowa yana biyan daidai rabo.

Shin tip ɗin yana ƙididdige tip ɗin kafin haraji ko bayan haraji?

A kan adadin lissafin da kuka shigar, daidai kamar yadda aka buga. Idan kuna son tip ɗin kafin haraji, shigar da adadin kafin haraji; idan lissafin ku ya riga ya haɗa da haraji, tip ɗin a nan yana kan jimlar da aka biya haraji.

Me zai faru idan mutane suka rabu ba daidai ba?

Wannan kalkuleta yana raba daidai. Ga rarrabuwar da ba ta daidaita ba, ƙididdige rabon kowanne mutum na lissafin kuɗi da tukwici daban da adadin kowane mutum a matsayin wurin farawa.

Me yasa adadin kowane mutum wani lokacin ba ya haɗuwa daidai?

Rarraba zuwa cents: raba $115.00 hanyoyi uku yana ba da $38.33 ga kowane mutum, jimillar $114.99. Mutum ɗaya yana rufe sauran cents a aikace.

Zan iya bayar da tukuici 0%?

Ee — shigar da 0 kuma jimlar kowane mutum kawai lissafin ne da aka raba da girman jam'iyya.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da kuɗin sabis?

A'a. Kudin sabis na tilas da ya riga ya kasance akan lissafin wani ɓangare ne na adadin lissafin; duk wani ƙarin tukwici yana kan sa.
