Girman da ya dace da sharar gida

An auna sanduna zuwa mafi girman juzu'i biyu. Yanka sharar shine ƙarin da kuka yi oda a saman madaidaicin juzu'i mai kusurwa huɗu.

Girman oda a rangwamen sharar daban-daban

Ba da izinin sharar gidadarajar

Tsawon, faɗi da zurfin iri ɗaya; rangwamen sharar kawai yana canzawa. Yi amfani da wannan don ganin yadda umarnin yake da mahimmanci ga izinin da kuka zaɓa.

Ƙa’ida

Girman da aka ƙayyade = tsawon × faɗi × zurfin. Girman sharar = girman da aka ƙayyade × ƙimar sharar. Girman oda = girman da aka ƙayyade × (1 + ƙimar sharar).

Misalin aiki

Tsawon 10 m, faɗi 4 m, zurfin 0.15 m: girman da aka ƙayyade = 10 × 4 × 0.15 = 6 m³. A cikin izinin sharar gida na 8%, ƙarar sharar gida = 6.00 × 0.08 = 0.48 m³, don haka ƙarar oda = 6.48 m³ (228.84 ft³). Waɗannan su ne misalan da aka saba gani a sama; maye gurbinsu da ma'auninku.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan ya ƙunshi zuba mai kusurwa huɗu kawai. Bambancin ƙananan matakai, ƙaura mai ƙarfi, siffofi marasa tsari, sassan gangara, mafi ƙarancin lodi na mai samarwa da zagaye zuwa sashin oda na mai samarwa duk zasu iya canza odar. Ba a ƙididdige farashi ba: shigar da babu farashi a nan, don haka kwatanta ambaton mai samarwa da ƙarar odar da kanka.

FAQ

Me yasa za a ƙara izinin sharar gida kwata-kwata?

Domin zuba mai gaske ba kasafai yake daidai da daidai girman murabba'i ba: zubar da ruwa, raguwar ƙasa mara daidaito da gibin tsari duk suna cinye ƙarin siminti. Kudin shine ma'aunin ku, ba kuɗin mai samarwa ba.

Me zai faru idan siffara ba murabba'i ba ce?

Raba shi zuwa murabba'ai masu aunawa, ƙididdige kowanne, sannan ƙara yawan da aka tara kafin a shafa sharar. Kada a tilasta siffa mara tsari zuwa murabba'i ɗaya kuma a yi fatan rage sharar zai shanye bambancin.

Ta yaya zan auna zurfin?

Auna kauri da aka gama a wurare da dama kuma a yi amfani da ƙimar wakilci. Idan zurfin ya bambanta sosai, a raba zuba zuwa yankuna masu zurfin daban-daban maimakon matsakaici a makance.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da mafi ƙarancin nauyin mai samarwa?

A'a. Idan odar ku ta ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin mai samarwa, mai samarwa zai iya caji mafi ƙarancin. Duba hakan kafin yin oda, musamman ga ƙananan zuba.

Zan iya canza sakamakon zuwa ƙafafu masu siffar cubic ko lita?

Ee: 1 m³ = 35.3147 ft³ = lita 1,000. Teburin rarrabuwa yana nuna tsari a cikin dukkan raka'a uku.

Shin wannan yana gaya min farashin simintin da za a kashe?

A'a, babu wani shigarwar farashi a wannan shafin. Ninki girman oda ta hanyar farashin da mai samar da kayanka ya ambata a kowane raka'a da kanka; farashi ya bambanta ta hanyar gauraya, wuri da sharuɗɗan isarwa.
