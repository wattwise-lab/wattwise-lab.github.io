Formula

Energy (kWh) = calls × Wh for each call × PUE ÷ 1,000. Emissions (kg CO₂e) = energy × grid grams for each kWh ÷ 1,000. Emissions for each call (g CO₂e) = Wh for each call × PUE × grid grams for each kWh ÷ 1,000.

Example wey show the calculation

Calls na 100,000; energy for each call na 0.5 Wh; PUE na 1.2; grid intensity na 450 gCO₂e/kWh. Energy = 100,000 × 0.5 × 1.2 ÷ 1,000 = 60 kWh. Emissions = 60 × 450 ÷ 1,000 = 27 kg CO₂e. For each call: 0.5 × 1.2 × 450 ÷ 1,000 = 0.27 g CO₂e; for each 1,000 calls: 270 g CO₂e. All dis inputs na examples. Replace energy for each call and grid figures with your own before you rely on the result.

Where e fit work and limits

Dis na model for a scenario, no be formal carbon-accounting result. E cover only inference energy wey you describe. Training, model development, hardware manufacturing, networking, cooling wey PUE no cover and embodied emissions no dey inside. Energy for each call dey change with model, hardware, batching and prompt length. Grid intensity dey change with place and time too. Na why you must supply both inputs yourself; no single published figure fit every case. We no provide live emissions data or certified accounting.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How una calculate am?

Energy na calls times Wh for each call times PUE, divided by 1,000 to get kWh. Emissions na energy times grid grams for each kWh, divided by 1,000 to get kilograms of CO₂e.

Why I fit edit carbon factor and energy for each call?

Because dem na assumptions, no be fixed constants. Another model, GPU or region fit give different values. Na why dis page collect dem as inputs wey you must supply, and mark the defaults as examples only.

Wetin PUE mean for here?

Power usage effectiveness na total facility energy divided by IT energy. PUE of 1.2 mean 20% extra energy for cooling and other facility loads on top of inference energy. If e na 1.0, we count no extra energy.

Dis include energy to train the model?

No. E cover only inference calls for your scenario. Training, fine-tuning and hardware manufacturing dey separate. Dem fit be much larger for some cases, but dis model no count dem.

Why result dey rise directly with call count?

Every row for the volume table use the same figure for each call. If you double calls, estimate go double. Real systems fit batch or cache requests, so the relationship fit change. Na another reason to replace the example energy for each call with measured data.

I fit use dis for official emissions report?

No. Na planning estimate from your own assumptions. Formal report need measured energy data, documented grid factor for the correct region and period, plus the reporting rules wey your organisation follow.
