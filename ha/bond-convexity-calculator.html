Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da K = Σ[k(k+1)CFₖ/(1+y/m)^(k+2)]/(Pm²). CFₖ shine biyan kuɗi a lokacin takardar kuɗi k, P shine farashin takardar kuɗi, y shine ƙaramin rabon amfanin shekara-shekara, kuma m shine biyan kuɗi kowace shekara. K yana kimanta lanƙwasa farashin gida dangane da yawan amfanin ƙasa.

Auna lanƙwasa a cikin dangantakar farashi da yawan amfanin lamuni mai ƙayyadadden lokaci.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kimantawa ta ilimi ta amfani da zato; babu farashin rayuwa, ƙa'idodin haraji ko shawarwarin saka hannun jari. Dawowar na iya zama mara kyau kuma ba a ba da garantin ba. Yana ɗaukar daidai lokacin kupon kuma babu zaɓuɓɓukan da aka haɗa.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
