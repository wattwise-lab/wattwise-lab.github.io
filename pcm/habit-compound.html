Formula

Total practice hours = daily minutes ÷ 60 × planned days × completion%. Equivalent workdays = total practice hours ÷ workday length. Na the day length wey you enter we dey use.

Example wey show the calculation

30 minutes per day, 365 planned days, 80% completion, 8-hour workday: scheduled hours = 30 ÷ 60 × 365 = 182.5 hours; completed hours = 182.5 × 0.80 = 146 hours; missed hours = 182.5 − 146 = 36.5 hours; equivalent workdays = 146.00 ÷ 8 = 18.25 days. These na example inputs — completion na your own estimate, no be measured rate.

Where e fit work and limits

Equivalent workdays divide hours by entered day length; dem na scale comparison, not claim about output. Time accumulated no dey establish learning quality, mastery or compound performance gains: hour of focused practice and hour of distracted repetition count same here. Completion treated as flat rate across whole period, so streaks, breaks and improving consistency not modelled.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How una calculate am?

Total practice hours = daily minutes ÷ 60 × planned days × completion%. Equivalent workdays = total practice hours ÷ workday length. Na the day length wey you enter we dey use.

Wetin completion rate mean?

Share of planned days you actually do habit, averaged over whole period. 80% over 365 days mean about 292 days completed, no be particular pattern of streaks and breaks.

More hours always mean better results?

Not necessarily. Time accumulated no dey establish learning quality, mastery or compound performance gains — how you practise matter as much as how long.

Wetin equivalent workdays mean?

Na comparison to help you picture the time: divide all the hours by the workday length wey you enter. 146 hours na 18.25 days if each workday get eight hours.

Wetin if I no dey do my habit every day?

First change am to daily average. For example, 3 sessions of 60 minutes each week na about 25.7 minutes per day (180 ÷ 7).

CalcMintly dey store values wey I enter?

No. This calculation dey run locally inside your browser; values wey you enter no dey reach any CalcMintly database.
