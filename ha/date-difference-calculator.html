Kwanaki (ƙarshe - farawa)—
Makonni da kwanaki—
Bayanin Kalanda—
Kwanaki masu haɗawa (an ƙidaya maki biyu na ƙarshe)—

Adadin kanun labarai ba ya haɗa da ranar ƙarshe (1 Janairu zuwa 2 Janairu rana ce ta 1). Ƙara 1 don ƙidayar da ya haɗa, wanda ke ƙidaya maki biyu na ƙarshe.

Ƙa’ida

Bambancin Kalanda = ranar ƙarshe − ranar farawa, ana kimanta ta amfani da kwanakin kalanda na gida. Bayanan shekaru-watanni-kwana suna ƙidaya shekarun kalanda da aka kammala, sannan watanni da aka kammala, sannan sauran kwanakin, kamar yadda aka yi lissafin shekaru.

Misalin aiki

Daga 1 Janairu 2026 zuwa 31 Disamba 2026: kwanaki 364, daidai makonni 52, da kuma bayanin kalanda na watanni 11 da kwanaki 30. Adadin da ya haɗa, wanda aka ƙidaya duka 1 Janairu da 31 Disamba, kwanaki 365 ne. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ana kwatanta dukkan kwanakin kalanda na gida kawai; lokutan rana da yankunan lokaci ana yin watsi da su. Kalanda kanta tana kula da shekarun tsalle - 2026 ba shekarar tsalle ba ce, yayin da tazara tsakanin 29 Fabrairu 2028 zai haɗa da wannan ranar. Tsawon wata ya bambanta, don haka bayanin shekaru-watanni-kwana bayanin kalanda ne, ba ranakun da aka raba da 30 ba. Babu ranakun hutu na jama'a da ke cikin wannan ƙidayar; don ranakun mako kawai, yi amfani da kalkuleta na ranar aiki.

Yana amfani da kalandar Gregorian mai saurin aiki da lissafi na kwanan wata kawai, ba tare da la'akari da lokacin adana hasken rana ba. Cikakkun shekaru da watanni suna amfani da ranar asali ta wata, an matse su zuwa ranar ƙarshe mai inganci idan ya cancanta. Sauran tarurrukan cika shekaru na iya bambanta; wannan ba ƙayyadadden shekaru na shari'a bane.

FAQ

Shin ranar ƙarshe ta haɗa?

A'a, a cikin ƙidayar kanun labarai. Daga 1 Janairu zuwa 2 Janairu rana ce ta 1. Ana nuna ƙidayar haɗawa (duka ƙarshen) daban kuma koyaushe tana ɗaya.

Me zai hana a raba kwanaki da 30 na tsawon watanni kawai?

Domin watanni suna da tsawon kwanaki 28–31. Rabawa da 30 yana ba da kimantawa wanda ke raguwa; rarrabuwar kalanda a nan tana ƙidaya watanni da aka kammala daidai.

Yaya ake tafiyar da shekarun tsalle-tsalle?

Ta hanyar kalandar gaske. 29 Fabrairu da ke faɗuwa cikin tazara yana ƙara kwana ɗaya ga ƙidayar ta atomatik.

Me zai faru idan ranar ƙarshe ta kasance kafin ranar farawa?

Ƙidayar rana ba ta da kyau kuma shafin yana nuna ta. Sannan za a nuna bayanin kalanda don tazara a ɗayan gefen, wanda aka yiwa alama kamar haka.

Shin wannan yana ƙidayar ranakun hutu?

A'a. Yana ƙidayar ranakun kalanda ne kawai. Don cire ƙarshen mako, yi amfani da kalkuleta na ranar aiki.
