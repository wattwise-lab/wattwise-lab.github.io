Rushewa

darajar

Kudin: wannan lokacin idan aka kwatanta da cikakken shekara a daidai lokacin amfani da shi na yau da kullun

An auna sanduna zuwa babban farashi. Sandar cikakken shekara tana ɗaukar amfani iri ɗaya kowace rana; hasashe ne, ba hasashen lissafin kuɗi ba.

Kuzari da farashi a tsawon lokaci daban-daban

Lokacidarajar

Watt iri ɗaya, awanni, farashi da yawa; adadin kwanaki kawai ke canzawa. Ana amfani da ƙimar da kuka shigar a duk tsawon lokacin - farashin da aka tsara ko lokacin amfani ba a yi shi da ƙira ba.

Ƙa’ida

Kuzari (kWh) = watts ÷ 1,000 × awanni a kowace rana × kwanaki × adadin na'urar. Kudin = kuzari × farashi ga kowace kWh.

Misalin aiki

Ƙarfi 1,500 W, awanni 4 a kowace rana, kwanaki 30, na'urar 1, ƙimar 0.80 a kowace kWh (misali ƙimar da kuka maye gurbin da naku): kuzarin yau da kullun = 1,500 ÷ 1,000 × 4 = 6 kWh, don haka kuzarin lokaci = 6 × 30 = 180 kWh da farashi = 180.00 × 0.80 = 144.00. Farashin yau da kullun shine 4.80. Farashin a nan shine kawai misalin tsoho - ainihin ƙimar lissafin ku shine abin da ke da mahimmanci.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wutar Lantarki mai jiran aiki, zagayowar aiki (kayayyakin da ke kunnawa da kashewa), ƙimar da aka daidaita, haraji, kuɗin buƙata da farashin lokacin amfani ba a yi su da ƙira ba: ana amfani da ƙimar da ba ta da matsala guda ɗaya ga duk kuzari. Idan na'urar ku ta zagaya, shigar da matsakaicin jan wutar lantarki, ba ƙarfin wutar lantarki mafi girma ba. Ba a gabatar da wani farashi a matsayin matsakaicin kasuwa ba - ƙimar da aka saba amfani da ita misali ne da za a maye gurbinta.

FAQ

Ta yaya lissafin kuɗin wutar lantarki ke aiki?

Kudin = watts ÷ 1,000 × sa'o'i a kowace rana × kwana × ƙidayar na'ura × farashi a kowace kWh. Awa ɗaya kilowatt (kWh) ana amfani da watts 1,000 na awa ɗaya.

Wane farashi ya kamata in shigar?

Farashin kowace-kWh daga lissafin ku ko jadawalin kuɗin ku, gami da abin da kawai kuke son kimantawa ta rufe. Idan lissafin ku ya ƙara haraji ko kuɗin da aka ƙayyade daban, wannan ƙiyasin ba zai dace da jimlar lissafin ba.

Yaya game da wutar jiran aiki?

Ba a haɗa da jadawalin jiran aiki ba sai kun ƙara shi: ko dai ƙara watts na jiran aiki zuwa shigarwar wutar lantarki na sa'o'in da na'urar ta haɗa, ko kuma gudanar da lissafi na biyu don amfani da jiran aiki.

Na'urar tawa tana kunnawa da kashewa - wane ƙarfi zan shigar?

Matsakaicin jan hankalinsa ne a kan sa'o'in da ka shiga, ba mafi girman watt ɗin da ke kan lakabin ba. Firji mai ƙimar 150 W na iya zama matsakaici ƙasa da haka a cikin yini.

Me yasa kudirin nawa ya bambanta da wannan kiyasin?

Matsakaicin farashin, farashin lokacin amfani, ƙayyadadden kuɗin yau da kullun, haraji da sauran kayan aiki akan lissafin ɗaya duk suna waje da wannan lissafin na'ura ɗaya.
