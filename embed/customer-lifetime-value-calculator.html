Formula

Use LTV = Rm/c−S. R is monthly revenue per customer, m is the contribution-margin fraction, c is the monthly churn fraction, and S is one-time servicing cost. Constant churn gives an expected lifetime of 1/c months; this model does not discount future contributions.

Scope and limitations

Assumes constant churn and margin, no expansion and no discounting. Small churn changes can produce large value changes.
