Formula

Tip = bill × tip rate. Total = bill + tip. Per-person total = total ÷ number of people. Tip per person = tip ÷ number of people.

Worked example

Bill $100, tip rate 15%, 2 people: tip = $100 × 0.15 = $15.00, total = $115.00, and per-person total = $115.00 ÷ 2 = $57.50 (tip $7.50 per person).

Scope and limitations

This version applies the tip to the entered bill and does not separately identify pre-tax and post-tax amounts. If your bill already includes tax and you prefer to tip on the pre-tax amount, enter the pre-tax amount instead. Rounding to cents per person can leave a small remainder.

FAQ

How does the tip calculator work?

Tip = bill × tip rate; per-person total = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people. Everyone pays an equal share.

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

On the bill amount you enter, exactly as typed. If you want a pre-tax tip, enter the pre-tax amount; if your bill already includes tax, the tip here is on the taxed total.

What if people split unevenly?

This calculator splits evenly. For uneven splits, compute each person's share of bill plus tip separately from the per-person figures as a starting point.

Why do per-person amounts sometimes not add up exactly?

Rounding to cents: splitting $115.00 three ways gives $38.33 per person, totaling $114.99. One person covers the remaining cent in practice.

Can I tip 0%?

Yes — enter 0 and the per-person total is simply the bill divided by the party size.

Does this include service charges?

No. A mandatory service charge already on the bill is part of the bill amount; any extra tip is on top of it.
