Rushewa

Adadin

Jimlar riba ita ce kuɗin shiga ban da farashin kayan da aka sayar kawai — ba a cire kuɗin aiki a nan ba.

A kallo ɗaya

Sandunan biyu tare suna daidai da kuɗin shiga. Tsawon ma'aunin ribar da aka samu yana nufin ƙarin riba.

Gefen da ke matakan samun kudin shiga daban-daban

Kuɗin shigaJimlar riba

Kudin kayan da aka sayar ana riƙe su ne a ƙimar da ke sama; kawai canjin kuɗin shiga yana faruwa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Ribar da aka samu = kuɗin shiga - farashin kayan da aka sayar. Jimlar riba = jimlar riba ÷ kuɗin shiga × 100%. Rabon COGS = farashin kayan da aka sayar ÷ kuɗin shiga × 100%.

Misalin aiki

Kuɗin shiga $100,000, farashin kayan da aka sayar $60,000: jimlar riba = $100,000 − $60,000 = $40,000, jimlar riba = $40,000 ÷ $100,000 × 100% = 40.00%, da rabon COGS = 60.00%.

Amfani da iyakoki

Jimlar riba ba ta haɗa da kuɗaɗen aiki kamar albashi, haya, tallatawa, riba, da haraji ba. Jimlar riba mai kyau har yanzu tana iya tare da asarar aiki da zarar an ƙididdige waɗannan kuɗaɗen - yi amfani da kalkuleta na riba don cikakken hoton.

FAQ

Ta yaya kalkuleta na riba ke aiki?

Jimlar riba = kuɗin shiga - farashin kayan da aka sayar; jimlar riba = jimlar riba ÷ kuɗin shiga × 100%. Yana auna abin da ya rage bayan farashin kai tsaye na abin da kuka sayar.

Menene bambanci da jimlar riba?

Jimlar riba tana cire komai, gami da kuɗaɗen aiki, riba, da haraji. Jimlar riba tana tsayawa ne akan farashin kayan da aka sayar, don haka ya fi girma kuma ba ya faɗi komai game da sama.

Shin jimlar riba za ta iya zama mara kyau?

Haka ne, idan farashin kayan da aka sayar ya wuce kuɗin shiga - kowace tallace-tallace tana asarar kuɗi kafin a ƙididdige duk wani kuɗin aiki. Wannan yana buƙatar gyara farashi ko gyara don samun kuɗi, ba rage farashi a wani wuri ba.

Me ake ɗauka a matsayin farashin kayan da aka sayar?

Kuɗin kai tsaye na samarwa ko siyan abin da kuka sayar: kayan aiki, aiki kai tsaye, da kuɗin siyan jumla. Hayar da tallatawa kuɗaɗen aiki ne, ba COGS ba.

Me yasa riba ke canzawa tare da kuɗin shiga kawai?

Domin COGS ana riƙe shi a cikin jadawalin yanayi. A zahiri COGS yawanci yana ƙaruwa da girma, don haka karanta wannan teburin a matsayin abin da zai faru idan, ba hasashen ba.

Shin riba mafi girma koyaushe ta fi kyau?

Gabaɗaya, amma ba a kowane farashi ba: ƙara farashi don ɗaga riba na iya rage yawan riba don rage jimlar riba. Dole ne a karanta kashi na riba da jimlar riba tare.
