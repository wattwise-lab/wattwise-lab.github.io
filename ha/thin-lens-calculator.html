Nisan hoton da aka sanya hannu

—

Gilashin sirara mai laushi a cikin iska; nisan hoto mai kyau gaskiya ne, mara kyau kuma kama-da-wane ne. An cire bambance-bambance da kauri na ruwan tabarau.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da 1/f = 1/dₒ+1/dᵢ da m = −dᵢ/dₒ. f shine tsawon mai da hankali, dₒ shine nisa tsakanin abu, kuma dᵢ shine nisa tsakanin hoto a cikin naúrar guda ɗaya. Tsawon mai da hankali da nisan ainihin abu suna da kyau; m shine girman gefe.

Amfani da iyakoki

Gilashin sirara mai laushi a cikin iska; nisan hoto mai kyau gaskiya ne, mara kyau kuma kama-da-wane ne. An cire bambance-bambance da kauri na ruwan tabarau.
