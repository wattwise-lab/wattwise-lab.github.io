Rushewa

Abudarajar

Gudummawar wata-wata riba ce a kowane oda lokutan oda na wata-wata ban da farashin da aka ƙayyade na wata-wata. Daidai-daidai yana raba farashin farawa da wannan gudummawar.

A kallo ɗaya

Sanduna suna kwatanta farashin farawa da cikakken shekara na gudummawa. Idan sandar shekara ta share sandar farawa, daidaiton riba zai faɗi cikin watanni 12.

Matsayi mai yawa akan lokaci

Yanayidarajar

Kowace layi tana da gudummawar da aka tara ba tare da an rage farashin farawa ba, ana ɗaukar umarni iri ɗaya, riba, da farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi a duk tsawon lokacin. Watannin farko ba su da kyau har sai an dawo da farashin farawa.

Ƙa’ida

Watanni don karya daidai = farashin farawa ÷ (riba a kowace oda × oda na wata-wata − farashin da aka ƙayyade na wata-wata). Umarni don dawo da farashin = farashin farawa ÷ riba a kowace oda. Jimilla bayan watanni N = gudummawar wata-wata × N − farashin farawa.

Misalin aiki

Farawa $20,000, farashin da aka ƙayyade $1,200 a kowane wata, riba $85 a kowane oda, 60 oda a kowane wata. Gudummawar wata = $85 × 60 − $1,200 = $5,100 − $1,200 = $3,900. Watanni don karya daidai = $20,000 ÷ $3,900 = watanni 5.13. Umarni don dawo da kuɗin = $20,000 ÷ $85 = 235.29 oda. Jimilla bayan watanni 12 = $3,900 × 12 − $20,000 = $26,800.

Amfani da iyakoki

Gudummawar wata-wata dole ne ta kasance mai kyau don duk wani karyewar-komai ya wanzu; idan farashin da aka ƙayyade ya wuce jimlar gudummawar, samfurin ya ba da rahoton karyewar-komai maimakon lokacin da ba shi da kyau. Tallace-tallace da riba zato ne, ba buƙatu mai tabbas ba ne, kuma samfurin yana ɗauka cewa sun kasance iri ɗaya - ba a tsara yanayin yanayi, sauye-sauyen farashi, da ci gaba ba. Kuɗaɗen kuɗi, haraji, lokacin biyan kuɗi, maida kuɗi, da ƙimar lokacin mai kafa ba a haɗa su cikin ƙayyadadden farashi ko riba a kowace oda.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige karyewar-komai?

Gudummawar wata-wata riba ce ga kowane oda oda na wata-wata ban da ƙayyadadden farashin wata-wata; watanni don karyewar ana raba farashin farawa da wannan gudummawar.

Me zai faru idan gudummawar sifili ce ko mara kyau?

To babu wani daidaito: harkar kasuwanci tana asarar kuɗi ko kuma tana tsayawa cak duk wata, kuma ba a taɓa dawo da kuɗin fara kasuwanci ba. Gyara gudummawar farko — ƙarin oda, ƙarin riba ga kowane oda, ko ƙarancin farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi.

Shin daidaiton ciniki yana nufin riba daga nan?

Yana nufin an biya kuɗin farawa. Daga wannan watan zuwa gaba, gudummawar kowane wata riba ce kafin haraji da kuma kafin lokacin mai kafa, idan babu ɗayan da aka ƙididdige a cikin abubuwan da aka shigar.

Shin lokacin mai kafa yakamata ya kasance cikin farashi mai ƙayyadadden farashi?

Idan kuna son amsar gaskiya, eh — daraja sa'o'in a ƙimar da ta dace kuma ku haɗa su, ko kuma daidaiton ciniki yana nuna aiki wanda kawai ke aiki ta hanyar ba da gudummawar aiki mara biya.

Me yasa ƙidayar oda take da ƙayyadadden yawa?

235.29 oda shine amsar lissafi; A aikace yana nufin 236 na tsari shine wanda ke rage jimlar jimillar kuɗin farawa, wani ɓangare na watan hutu.

Me ba a haɗa ba?

Kuɗi, haraji, mayar da kuɗi, yanayin yanayi, da haɗarin buƙata. Yi la'akari da ƙidayar watan a matsayin adadi na tsare-tsare a tallace-tallace da aka ɗauka, sannan a sake gudanar da shi a rabin tallace-tallace don ganin matsalar da ke ƙasa.
