Rushewa

Adadin

Ana amfani da rangwamen da aka ƙayyade bayan rangwamen kashi, kuma farashin ƙarshe ya faɗi ƙasa da sifili - ba zai taɓa zama mara kyau ba.

A kallo ɗaya

Sandunan biyu tare daidai suke da farashin asali. Babban sandar tanadi yana nufin rangwame mai tasiri mai zurfi.

Farashin ƙarshe a rangwame daban-daban

RangwameFarashin ƙarshe

Farashin asali da ƙarin rangwame ana riƙe su ne a ƙimar da ke sama; kashi-kashi na rangwame kawai ke canzawa a layuka.

Ƙa’ida

Farashin ƙarshe = farashin asali × (1 − ƙimar rangwame) − ƙarin rangwame mai ƙayyadadden lokaci, an yi ƙasa da sifili. Adadin da aka adana = farashin asali − farashin ƙarshe.

Misalin aiki

Farashin asali $100, rangwame 20%, ƙarin rangwame $5: farashin ƙarshe = $100 × (1 − 0.20) − $5 = $80 − $5 = $75, don haka kuna adana $25, rangwame mai tasiri na 25.00%.

Amfani da iyakoki

Haraji, jigilar kaya, ƙa'idodin kashe kuɗi mafi ƙaranci, odar rangwame, da keɓancewa na iya canza jimlar biyan kuɗi. Idan shago ya yi amfani da rangwame mai ƙayyadadden lokaci kafin rangwamen kashi, sakamakon ya bambanta kaɗan - wannan kalkuleta yana amfani da kashi da farko, kamar yadda aka bayyana a cikin dabarar.

FAQ

Ta yaya kalkuleta mai rangwame ke aiki?

Farashin ƙarshe = farashin asali × (1 − ƙimar rangwame) − ƙarin rangwame mai ƙayyadadden lokaci, an yi ƙasa da sifili. Kashi yana farawa, sannan adadin da aka ƙayyade.

A wane tsari ake amfani da rangwamen?

Kashi da farko, sannan rangwame mai ƙayyadadden lokaci. Juya odar yana canza sakamakon lokacin da duka biyun suke, don haka duba odar shagon idan jimlar ta bambanta da ƙaramin adadin.

Shin farashin ƙarshe zai iya ƙasa da sifili?

A'a. Idan rangwamen ya wuce farashin, sakamakon yana ƙasa da $0.00 kuma an ƙayyade tanadin a farashin asali.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da haraji?

A'a. Adadin da za a biya haraji ya dogara ne da ƙa'idodin gida da kuma nau'in rangwame ko takardar rangwame. Yana iya bambanta da adadin da aka rage. Tabbatar da tushen harajin da ya dace kafin amfani da kalkuleta na harajin tallace-tallace.

Menene rangwamen da ya dace?

Jimlar tanadi da aka raba ta da farashin asali. Tare da rangwamen 20% da $5 a kan $100, tanadin da ya dace shine 25%, ba 20% ba.

Yaya game da mafi ƙarancin kashe kuɗi ko abubuwan da aka cire?

Ba a yi masa ƙira ba. Idan rangwamen ya shafi sama da ƙayyadadden kashe kuɗi kawai ko kuma ya cire wasu abubuwa, jimlar biyan kuɗi za ta bambanta.
