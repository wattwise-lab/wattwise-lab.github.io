Where your BMI fall

Adult categories: underweight below 18.5; healthy weight from 18.5 to less than 25; overweight from 25 to less than 30; obesity from 30. Di bar use scale of 40.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²). BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25. Ponderal Index = weight (kg) ÷ height³ (m³). Di reference weight interval start at 18.5 × height² and end just below 25 × height². Thresholds wey show don round; di upper threshold no count inside.

Example wey show the calculation

At 175 cm and 72.6 kg: BMI = 72.6 ÷ 1.75² ≈ 23.7, BMI Prime ≈ 0.95, and Ponderal Index ≈ 13.5 kg/m³. Di reference weight thresholds na 18.5 × 1.75² = 56.65625 kg and 25 × 1.75² = 76.5625 kg, with di upper threshold excluded. After rounding for display: 56.7–<76.6 kg. Where e fit work and limits

Di CDC categories wey we use here apply to adults aged 20 and older. BMI na screening measure, no be diagnosis or direct body-fat measurement. E no separate muscle from fat or account for pregnancy and individual health situations. Children and teenagers need assessment wey follow age and sex. Classification use BMI before rounding, even if di displayed number round reach one boundary.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin the adult categories mean?

Di intervals na below 18.5, from 18.5 to less than 25, from 25 to less than 30, and 30 or more. These na screening labels for adults aged 20 and older, no be individual diagnoses.

If BMI no fall for healthy range, e mean say something wrong?

No — not by itself. BMI na screening number, no be diagnosis. E no talk anything about body composition, fitness, or health on its own.

Wetin be BMI Prime?

BMI Prime na BMI divided by 25. Value of 1 reach di upper boundary and dey outside di healthy-weight interval wey we use here.

Why metric equivalent differ small from wetin I enter?

Converting feet/inches/pounds to centimetres/kilograms dey round to one decimal place. Underlying calculation dey use full converted values, so display rounding no dey change result.

Una dey store my data?

No. This calculation dey run locally inside your browser; values wey you enter no dey reach any CalcMintly database.

I fit use am for pikin?

No. Children BMI dey interpret against age- and sex-specific growth percentiles, wey this page no model.
