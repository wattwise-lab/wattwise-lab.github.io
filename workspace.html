How to use your calculator workspace

  1. Load a ready-made layout, or remove a calculator and add another. You can add the same calculator twice to compare scenarios.
  2. Replace the example values. Desktop shows a two-column grid; on phones, the numbered buttons switch calculators without losing inputs. Move cards with the arrows or drag handle.
  3. Under a result, choose another calculator and its destination field, then select Send result. Only the current unrounded number is copied. No unit conversion or ongoing synchronization happens automatically.
  4. Save my layout remembers calculator choices and order on this device. Refreshing loads fresh example values, not your previous inputs. Removing a card can be undone until you remove another or replace the layout.

Example: discount → tax → currency

In Shopping & bills, a price of 100 with a 20% discount and no extra discount gives 80. Send 80 to Sales Tax → Subtotal. At 10% tax the total is 88. Send 88 to Currency Converter → Amount, then enter your provider’s exchange rate and fee. With rate 1 and fee 0%, the result stays 88.

Choose compatible fields and use a consistent currency. Rates are entered by you, not fetched live. These are separate estimates; using calculators together does not make them a financial recommendation or a complete project plan. Open each calculator’s full guide for its formula and exclusions.
