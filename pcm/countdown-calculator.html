Formula

Days remaining = target local calendar date − from local calendar date. Weeks = whole weeks wey dey inside that count; days wey remain dey show separately.

Example wey show the calculation

From 28 October 2026 to target 1 January 2027: 65 days, which be 9 weeks and 2 days. 1 January 2027 na Friday. Set from date to 2026-10-28 to reproduce this result; leaving am blank count from today instead.

Where e fit work and limits

This na calendar-day count, no be live hours-and-minutes countdown, and e dey depend on device local date when from date leave blank. Leap years handled by calendar: count between two dates automatically include 29 February in leap years. Times of day and time zones ignored; both dates treated as whole local calendar days.

E use proleptic Gregorian calendar and date-only arithmetic, independent of daylight saving time. Complete years and months use di original day of month, but move am to di last valid day if necessary. Other anniversary conventions fit different; dis no be legal age determination.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Na live countdown be this?

No. E dey count whole calendar days between two dates. E no dey tick down in hours, minutes or seconds.

Wetin if target date don pass?

Count negative and page report am as days ago — for example, −10 days shown as '10 days ago'.

Wetin 'from today' dey use?

Your device local date. If you need reproducible result, enter from date explicitly instead of leaving am blank.

E dey handle leap years?

Yes. Because e subtract real calendar dates, 29 February inside interval counted like any other day.

Why weeks and days instead of months?

Months get different lengths, so day count convert to weeks exactly but to months only approximately. Weeks and days exact; months no shown.
