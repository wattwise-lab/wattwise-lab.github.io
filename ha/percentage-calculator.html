Ƙa’ida

Kashi na tushe = ƙimar tushe × kaso ÷ 100. Ƙima bayan ƙaruwa = tushe × (1 + kaso ÷ 100). Ƙima bayan raguwa = tushe × (1 − kaso ÷ 100).

Misalin aiki

15% na 280 = 280 × 15 ÷ 100 = 42. Bayan ƙaruwar 15%: 280 + 42 = 322. Bayan raguwar 15%: 280 − 42 = 238. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake maimaita wannan sakamakon, sannan maye gurbin ƙimar da naka.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kashi na iya zama mara kyau ko sama da 100; raguwar da ta fi 100% tana ba da sakamako mara kyau, wanda yake daidai a lissafi amma ƙila ba zai zama ma'ana ga adadin da ake bayyanawa ba. Sakamakon an zagaye shi zuwa wurare na adadi na 2 don nunawa. Wannan shafin yana ƙididdige kaso kawai - ba ya ƙara haraji, kuɗaɗe, ko haɗa abubuwa sai dai idan waɗannan ɓangare ne na kaso da kuka shigar.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige kaso na ƙima?

Ninki tushe da kaso kuma a raba da 100. Misali, 15% na 280 shine 280 × 15 ÷ 100 = 42.

Menene ma'anar 'bayan ƙaruwa'?

Tushen tare da kaso na adadin: 280 ya karu da 15% shine 280 + 42 = 322.

Shin kaso zai iya wuce 100?

Eh. 150% na 280 shine 420, kuma ƙarin 150% yana ba da 700. Lissafi iri ɗaya ne; duba ko hakan yana da ma'ana ga yanayin ku.

Me zai faru idan kaso ya kasance mara kyau?

Kaso mara kyau yana juya alkiblar: −15% na 280 shine −42, don haka 'ƙarin' na −15% hakika raguwa ne zuwa 238.

Me yasa ake zagaye sakamakon?

An zagaye nuni zuwa wurare na adadi 2. Ba a zagaye ninkawa a ƙasa ba kafin a ƙididdige alkaluman ƙaruwa da raguwa.
