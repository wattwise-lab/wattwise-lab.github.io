Formula

Use OR = ad/(bc) and bounds exp[ln(OR)±1.96√(1/a+1/b+1/c+1/d)]. a and b are group A event and non-event counts; c and d are group B counts. The logarithmic interval uses a normal approximation and requires positive counts.

Scope and limitations

Use positive cell counts. The large-sample interval can be unreliable for small samples; no continuity correction is applied.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.
