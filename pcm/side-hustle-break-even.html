How the result break down

Di itemValue wey e get

Monthly contribution na profit per order times monthly orders minus monthly fixed costs. To get break-even time, divide startup cost by that contribution.

Quick look at the result

Bars dey compare startup cost with one full year's contribution. If the year bar pass the startup bar, break-even go happen within 12 months.

Net position as time dey pass

Case wey you wan compareValue wey e get

Each row na cumulative contribution minus startup cost, if orders, profit and fixed costs stay the same throughout. Early months go negative until you recover startup cost.

Formula

Months to break even = startup cost ÷ (profit per order × monthly orders − monthly fixed costs). Orders to recover cost = startup cost ÷ profit per order. Net after N months = monthly contribution × N − startup cost.

Example wey show the calculation

Startup $20,000, fixed costs $1,200 per month, profit $85 per order, 60 orders per month. Monthly contribution = $85 × 60 − $1,200 = $5,100 − $1,200 = $3,900. Months to break even = $20,000 ÷ $3,900 = 5.13 months. Orders to recover cost = $20,000 ÷ $85 = 235.29 orders. Net after 12 months = $3,900 × 12 − $20,000 = $26,800.

Where e fit work and limits

Monthly contribution must pass zero for break-even to dey. If fixed costs pass gross contribution, the model go show no break-even instead of negative time. Sales and margins na assumptions, no be guaranteed demand, and the model assume dem stay constant — we no model seasons, customer loss, price changes or growth. Financing costs, tax, payment timing, refunds and founder's time no dey inside unless you put dem for fixed costs or profit per order.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How una dey calculate break-even?

Monthly contribution na profit per order times monthly orders minus monthly fixed costs; months to break even na startup cost divided by that contribution.

Wetin if contribution na zero or negative?

Then no break-even dey: the hustle dey lose money or stand still every month, so you no go recover startup cost. Fix contribution first — more orders, higher profit per order or lower fixed costs.

Break-even mean profit go dey from then on?

E mean say you don recover startup cost. From that month onwards, monthly contribution na profit before tax and before founder's time, if neither dey inside your inputs.

Make founder's time dey inside fixed costs?

If you want honest answer, yes — value the hours at reasonable rate and include dem. If not, break-even go make hustle wey only work with unpaid labour look better than e be.

Why the order count get fraction?

235.29 orders na the arithmetic answer; for real life, e mean na the 236th order go push cumulative total past startup cost, part-way through the break-even month.

Wetin dem no include?

Financing, tax, refunds, seasonality and demand risk no dey inside. Use the month count as planning figure at the sales wey you assume, then calculate again at half the sales to see the downside case.
