Value for different numbers of work-from-home days

Case wey you wan compareValue wey e get

Every row dey reuse your daily commute figures and hourly value; na only remote days dey change. Home costs na fixed monthly amount, so few remote days dey look worse per day.

Formula

Monthly value = remote days × (daily commute spending + commute minutes ÷ 60 × hourly value) − extra home costs. Cash saving = remote days × daily commute spending − extra home costs.

Example wey show the calculation

Commute $45 per day, 90 minutes per day, hourly value $80, 12 remote days, extra home costs $300. Per day: $45 + 90 ÷ 60 × $80 = $45 + $120 = $165. Gross = 12 × $165 = $1,980. Total = $1,980 − $300 = $1,680 per month. For cash-only (hourly value zero): 12 × $45 − $300 = $240 per month. Time wey you recover = 12 × 1.5 = 18 hours.

Where e fit work and limits

Set hourly value to zero if you want compare cash alone. Time wey you recover no automatically turn to money wey you fit spend. If housework or rest take the time, pricing am at full hourly cost go overstate the benefit. Extra home costs na one monthly figure — put all extra heating, electricity, equipment and food costs inside, or cash saving go too high. Remove any employer subsidy from commute spending. The model no cover career, collaboration or wellbeing effects, whether good or bad.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How una calculate am?

Each remote day worth daily commute spending plus commute minutes divided by 60 times hourly value. Multiply am by remote days, then subtract extra monthly home costs.

Time value na actual money?

Na only if the time wey you recover dey earn or save money. For cash-only view, set hourly value to zero — the worked example's cash-only figure na $240, no be $1,680.

Wetin count as extra home costs?

Na extra heating, electricity, internet, equipment and food costs wey working from home cause for the month. If you for pay most of the cost anyway, no count that part.

Why few work-from-home days look bad?

Because home costs na fixed monthly costs, while savings dey increase with days. One remote day for one month hardly fit cover a full month's extra home costs with cash saving alone.

I suppose count going and coming for transport?

Yes: minutes and spending suppose cover the full trip go and come back for the day, no be one leg. If you halve either input, you go halve the daily value.

This one include wellbeing or effect on career?

No. Less commuting fit improve wellbeing and focus, while less office presence fit affect visibility and collaboration. Those na real but outside this money-and-time model.
