Contribution = product revenue after discount + customer delivery fee − expected revenue refunds − product cost + product-cost value wey you recover − packaging − sending cost − other variable costs − payment fees + fees wey dem return − expected return handling − adverts. Monthly profit = contribution × orders − fixed costs. Orders needed = round up [(fixed costs + monthly profit target) ÷ contribution].

We no automatically add income tax, duties, payment disputes, subscriptions or exchange-rate losses. Add costs wey you no fit recover to other variable or fixed costs, without counting twice.
