Formula

Use B = (T+nH+pdF)(1+w). T is group transport cost, n is nights, H is group lodging cost per night, p is people, d is days, F is daily spending per person, and w is the buffer fraction. Costs use the same currency.

Scope and limitations

Transport and accommodation are group costs; daily spending is per person. Use one currency throughout. No exchange rates or live travel prices are supplied.

Inputs are checked for missing values and allowed ranges. A result outside the finite numeric range is not displayed. Rounding affects display, while the calculation uses the model’s numeric precision.

Check your units and assumptions before relying on a result. Changing a label does not convert a currency or physical unit.
