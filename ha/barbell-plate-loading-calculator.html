Faran-balan a kowane gefe

—

Yana ɗaukar isassun nau'ikan da suka dace na kowane girma. An cire maƙallan; an haɗa nauyinsu a cikin nauyin sandar idan ana buƙata.

Abubuwan da aka shigar a cikin lafiya suna nan a cikin wannan shafin. An kashe raba hanyar haɗi.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Kowane gefe yana buƙatar S = (T−B)/2. T shine jimlar nauyin da aka nufa kuma B shine nauyin sandar a cikin naúrar ɗaya. Zaɓi nau'ikan farantin da suka dace don isa ga S a kowane gefe tare da ƙananan faranti. Ana tsammanin kowane girman farantin da aka shigar yana da isassun nau'ikan da suka dace.

Nemo nau'ikan faran-balan masu daidaito daga saitin adadin da ake da su.

Amfani da iyakoki

Yana ɗaukar isassun nau'ikan da suka dace na kowane girma. An cire maƙallan; an haɗa nauyinsu a cikin nauyin sandar idan ana buƙata.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
