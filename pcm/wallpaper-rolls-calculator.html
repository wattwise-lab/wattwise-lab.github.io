Formula

For r = 0, cut length na c = h+t; for r > 0, use c = r ceil((h+t)/r). Rolls na N = ceil[ceil(P/w)/floor(L/c)]. h na wall height, t na trim, r na pattern repeat, P na wall perimeter, w na roll width, and L na roll length, all for m. ceil dey round up and floor dey round down.
