Formula

Use Wilson centre C = (p+z²/(2n))/(1+z²/n) and half-width H = z√[p(1−p)/n+z²/(4n²)]/(1+z²/n). p is successes divided by trials, n is trials, and z is the two-sided standard-normal critical value. Bounds are C−H and C+H, displayed as percentages.

Scope and limitations

Independent binary trials; confidence describes the method’s repeated-sample coverage, not a probability assigned to a fixed true proportion.
