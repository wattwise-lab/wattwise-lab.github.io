Bayanin canzawa

Abudarajar

Canjin maki adadi ne na sa'o'i da aka ƙayyade daga UTC. Ba sa sanya ƙa'idodin adana hasken rana, waɗanda ke canzawa ta wurin wuri da kwanan wata.

Ƙa’ida

Lokacin manufa = lokacin tushe − tushen UTC offset + manufa UTC offset, an naɗe zuwa sa'o'i 24. A cikin mintuna: mintuna na manufa = sa'a × 60 + minti − tushen daidaitawa × 60 + manufa daidaitawa × 60; Idan sakamakon ya kasance mara kyau, ƙara 1,440 (24 awanni) kuma a ƙidaya kwana ɗaya da ya gabata; idan ya kai 1,440 ko fiye, a cire 1,440 a ƙidaya kwana ɗaya bayan haka.

Matsakaici a cikin jerin abubuwan da aka zaɓa misalai ne na lokaci-lokaci na wurare da aka ambata. Wurare da yawa suna canzawa da awa ɗaya yayin adana hasken rana, kuma gwamnatoci lokaci-lokaci suna canza ƙa'idodin matsakaici - dole ne a tabbatar da matsakaici da ya shafi takamaiman rana tare da tushe na hukuma don wannan wuri da kwanan wata.

Misalin aiki

09:00 a UTC+8 shine 9 × 60 = minti 540; 540 − 480 (UTC+8) + (−300) (UTC−5) = −240 mintuna. Ƙara 1,440 yana ba da minti 1,200 = 20:00, kwana ɗaya da ya wuce — don haka 09:00 a Beijing shine 20:00 ranar da ta gabata a lokacin daidaito na New York.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan shafin yana canza lokutan rana ta amfani da ƙayyadaddun UTC kawai. Bai san kwanakin ba, sauye-sauyen ceton rana, ko canje-canjen tarihi na daidaitawa, kuma ba zai iya gaya muku ko wani wuri yana lura da adana hasken rana a wani kwanan wata da aka bayar ba. Don tsara lokaci, tabbatar da daidaitawar ainihin ranar tare da tushe na hukuma. Ana ba da lokaci ga minti; ba a sarrafa daƙiƙa ba.

FAQ

Shin wannan yana kula da adana hasken rana?

A'a. sauye-sauyen lambobi ne da aka ƙayyade. Wuri akan adana hasken rana yawanci yana gaba da daidaitaccen daidaitawar sa - misali New York shine UTC−4 akan adana hasken rana maimakon UTC−5. Duba madaidaicin kwanan wata na ainihin ku.

Me yasa sakamakon ya ce 'kwana ɗaya da wuri'?

Domin cire ragowar zai iya tura lokacin kafin tsakar dare. 09:00 a Beijing har yanzu shine daren da ya gabata a New York - canjin rana yana bayyana hakan maimakon nuna 20:00 a hankali.

Me game da yankunan rabin sa'a?

An haɗa su: UTC+05:30 (Indiya) da UTC+09:30 (Adelaide, misali) suna cikin jerin zaɓuka, kuma dabarar tana aiki cikin mintuna don haka suna canzawa daidai.

Zan iya canza cikakken kwanan wata da lokaci?

Ba a wannan shafin ba: yana canza lokacin rana da canjin rana. Don taron da aka yi kwanan wata, yi amfani da canjin rana zuwa ranar ku da hannu kuma ku tabbatar da adana hasken rana don wannan ranar.

Me yasa aka jera London a matsayin lokacin yau da kullun?

Landan ita ce UTC+00:00 a lokacin hunturu da kuma UTC+01:00 (Lokacin bazara na Burtaniya) a lokacin rani. Jerin abubuwan da aka zaɓa yana ba da daidaitaccen rangwame; zaɓi +01:00 don kwanakin bazara.
