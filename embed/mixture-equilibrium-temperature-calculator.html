Formula

Use T = (mₐcₐTₐ+mᵦcᵦTᵦ)/(mₐcₐ+mᵦcᵦ). m denotes mass, c specific heat, and subscripts identify the materials. Tₐ and Tᵦ are initial temperatures in °C; equilibrium temperature T uses the same scale. Assume no heat loss or phase change.
