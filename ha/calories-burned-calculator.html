Kalori ta hanyar lokaci, nazari

Sanduna suna aunawa a layi tare da tsawon lokaci saboda dabarar tana layi a cikin mintuna. An auna sanduna zuwa ƙimar minti 60.

Ƙa’ida

Kalori = MET × 3.5 × nauyi (kg) ÷ 200 × mintuna. Ɗaya daga cikin MET shine kuɗin kuzarin zama a hankali; ana ɗaukar aikin da aka kimanta 6 MET a matsayin sau shida na wannan kuɗin.

Misalin aiki

Aiki 6 MET, nauyi 65 kg, tsawon lokaci 45 mintuna: kalori = 6 × 3.5 × 65 ÷ 200 × 45 = 6.825 × 45 = 307.1 kcal. Wannan shine 6.83 kcal a minti daya da kuma 409.5 kcal a kowace awa a daidai wannan ƙarfi da nauyi. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitin da ke sama don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ƙimar MET matsakaicin yawan jama'a ne na wani aiki, ba ma'aunin zaman ku ba: motsa jiki, fasaha, kayan aiki, ƙasa, da kuma ilimin halittar mutum ɗaya duk suna canza ƙimar gaskiya. Tsarin yana ɗaukar ƙarfin da ya dace na tsawon lokacin, don haka tazara, dakatawa, da ƙoƙarin canzawa ana raba su da matsakaicin lokaci. Hakanan yana ƙididdige jimillar kashe kuɗi, gami da kuzarin da za ku yi amfani da shi a lokacin hutu a cikin mintuna ɗaya.

FAQ

Menene MET?

Yawan kuɗin kuzarin hutawa. Zama a hankali shine 1 MET; ana ɗaukar aikin da aka ƙima 6 MET a matsayin wanda ya ninka kuɗin kuzari sau shida a minti ɗaya.

Me yasa wannan kimantawa ce kawai?

Domin kuwa adadin MET matsakaici ne a tsakanin mutane, kuma ingancinka, lafiyarka, da kayan aikinka sun bambanta da wannan matsakaicin.

Shin ya haɗa da adadin kuzari na hutawa?

Eh — sakamakon shine jimillar kashe kuɗi, wanda ya haɗa da kuzarin da za ku yi amfani da shi a lokacin hutu a waɗannan mintunan.

Me zai faru idan ƙoƙarina ya bambanta?

Tsarin yana ɗaukar ƙarfin da ba ya canzawa. Don zaman gauraye, raba lokacin da ƙarfi kuma ƙara sassan, ko kuma a ɗauki sakamakon a matsayin matsakaicin ƙarfin.

An adana bayanai na?

A'a. Wannan lissafin yana aiki a cikin gida a cikin burauzarka kuma ƙimar da ka shigar ba a aika ta zuwa ga bayanan CalcMintly ba.

Ta yaya zan zaɓi ƙimar MET?

Yi amfani da ƙimar lissafi da aka buga MET don aikin da aka yi - irin wannan aikin da aka yi da ƙarfi ko kuma tare da kaya yana ɗaukar mafi girma MET.
