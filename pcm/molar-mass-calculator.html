Formula

Molar mass for g/mol = Σ (number of atoms of each element × its atomic weight for di table). For Ca(OH)2, di subscript 2 multiply both O and H: Ca + 2 × O + 2 × H. Di fixed table use rounded values like H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007 and O 15.999.

Example wey show the calculation

With dis table, H2O get molar mass 2 × 1.008 + 15.999 = 18.015 g/mol. Its calculated mass fractions na 2.016 ÷ 18.015 ≈ 11.19% hydrogen and 15.999 ÷ 18.015 ≈ 88.81% oxygen. Other checks from di table na CO2 = 12.011 + 2 × 15.999 = 44.009 g/mol and NaCl = 22.990 + 35.45 = 58.440 g/mol.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin mole mean for chemistry?

One mole contain exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ elementary entities wey you specify. Molar mass express di mass of one mole for g/mol; di values wey show use dis calculator rounded atomic-weight table.

Why water na 18.015 instead of 18?

Hydrogen and oxygen use average atomic weights of 1.008 and 15.999 for dis table. So 2 × 1.008 + 15.999 = 18.015 g/mol. Dis na approximate conventional value; sample wey get enriched isotope fit different.

How brackets dey work?

Number after closing bracket dey multiply everything inside am. For Ca(OH)2, the 2 doubles both O and H: Ca + O2 + H2, giving 40.078 + 2 × 15.999 + 2 × 1.008 = 74.092 g/mol.

Wetin if the calculator no recognise my element?

Di supported symbols dey listed above. Unknown symbol go give error; di calculator no guess mass or invent value for element wey e no support.

How I fit use molar mass for real work?

To change mass to moles, divide grams by molar mass. To change moles to mass, multiply. For example, 36.03 g of water na 36.03 ÷ 18.015 = 2.00 moles.

Molar mass na the same as molecular weight?

The numbers dey match, but units differ. Molecular weight na relative number without unit. Molar mass na that number in g/mol. People often dey use both terms as if dem mean the same thing.
