Bayar da lamuni na shekara-shekara

—

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato; babu farashin rayuwa, ƙa'idodin haraji ko shawarwarin saka hannun jari. Dawowar na iya zama mara kyau kuma ba a tabbatar da ita ba. Yana amfani da lokutan takardar shaidar daidai da sulhu a ranar takardar shaidar; ba ya haɗa da ribar da aka tara da ta lalace.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Warware P = Σ C/(1+y/m)^k + F/(1+y/m)^N don y. P shine farashin kasuwa, F shine ƙimar fuska, C shine kowane biyan kuɗi na kupon, m shine biyan kuɗi a kowace shekara, kuma N shine jimlar lokutan kupon. y shine ƙaramin rabon yawan amfanin shekara-shekara.

Warware ribar takardar shaidar da aka ƙayyade daga farashin takardar shaidar kwanan wata.

Amfani da iyakoki

Kimanta ilimi ta amfani da zato; babu farashin rayuwa, ƙa'idodin haraji ko shawarwarin saka hannun jari. Dawowar na iya zama mara kyau kuma ba a tabbatar da ita ba. Yana amfani da lokutan takardar shaidar daidai da sulhu a ranar takardar shaidar; ba ya haɗa da ribar da aka tara da ta lalace.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
