Rushewa

Abudarajar

Ajiye kuɗi shine kuɗin tafiya wanda aka adana an rage ƙarin kuɗin gida. Ana dawo da ƙimar lokaci sau da yawa ƙimar kowace awa. Jimillar ta haɗa duka biyun.

A kallo ɗaya

Sanduna suna kwatanta rabin kuɗin da rabin lokacin. Sandunan kuɗi suna ƙasa da sifili don nunawa lokacin da aka adana kuɗi mara kyau - teburin yana nuna ainihin adadi.

Darajar da aka samu a ƙidayar rana ta nesa daban-daban

Yanayidarajar

Kowane layi yana sake amfani da ƙididdigar tafiyar ku ta yau da kullun da ƙimar sa'a kuma yana bambanta kwanaki na nesa kawai. Kuɗin gida na wata-wata ne kuma an gyara su, don haka kwanaki kaɗan na nesa suna kama da mafi muni a kowace rana.

Ƙa’ida

Darajar wata-wata = kwanaki na nesa × (kuɗin tafiyar yau da kullun + mintunan tafiya ÷ 60 × ƙimar sa'a) − ƙarin kuɗin gida. Ajiye kuɗi = kwanaki na nesa × kashe kuɗin tafiya na yau da kullun − ƙarin kuɗin gida.

Misalin aiki

Tafiya $45 kowace rana, mintuna 90 kowace rana, ƙimar sa'a $80, kwanaki na nesa 12, ƙarin farashin gida $300. Kowace rana: $45 + 90 ÷ 60 × $80 = $45 + $120 = $165. Jimilla = 12 × $165 = $1,980. Jimilla = $1,980 − $300 = $1,680 kowace wata. Kuɗi kawai (ƙimar awa sifili): 12 × $45 − $300 = $240 kowace wata. Lokacin da aka dawo da shi = 12 × 1.5 = 18 awanni.

Amfani da iyakoki

Saita ƙimar awa zuwa sifili don kwatancen kuɗi kawai: lokacin da aka dawo da shi ba kudin shiga da za a iya kashewa ta atomatik ba ne, kuma kimanta shi da cikakken kuɗin awa ɗaya ya fi ƙarfin fa'idar idan ayyukan gida ko hutu suka mamaye shi. Ƙarin kuɗin gida adadi ɗaya ne na wata-wata - dumama, wutar lantarki, kayan aiki, da ƙarin abinci dole ne su kasance a ciki ko kuma an yi ƙarin bayani game da tanadin kuɗi. Ya kamata a kashe kuɗin tafiya bayan duk wani tallafin ma'aikaci, kuma tsarin bai shafi tasirin aiki, haɗin gwiwa, ko walwala a kowane bangare ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Kowace rana mai nisa tana da darajar kashe kuɗin tafiya ta yau da kullun tare da mintunan tafiya da aka raba da sau 60 darajar kowace sa'a; ninka ta kwanaki masu nisa kuma a cire ƙarin kuɗin gida na wata-wata.

Shin ƙimar lokaci ta gaske ce?

Sai dai idan lokacin da aka dawo da shi ya sami riba ko adana kuɗi. Don ganin kuɗi kawai, saita ƙimar awa zuwa sifili - adadin kuɗin da aka yi amfani da shi na misalin da aka yi aiki shine $240, ba $1,680 ba.

Me ake ƙididdigewa azaman ƙarin kuɗin gida?

Ƙara yawan dumama, wutar lantarki, intanet, kayan aiki, da abinci da ake samu ta hanyar aiki daga gida, na tsawon wata. Idan za ku biya mafi yawan kuɗin, kada ku ƙididdige shi.

Me yasa 'yan kwanaki kaɗan na nesa ba su da kyau?

Domin farashin gida yana kowane wata kuma yana da tsayayye yayin da tanadi ke da ma'auni na kwanaki. Rana ɗaya ta nesa a wata ba ta cika biyan cikakken wata na ƙarin kuɗin gida akan kuɗi kawai ba.

Shin ya kamata in ƙirga tafiyar ta hanyoyi biyu?

Ee: mintuna da kashe kuɗi ya kamata su zama cikakken tafiya ta zagaye na rana, ba ƙafa ɗaya ba. Rage ko dai shigarwar ya ninka ƙimar yau da kullun.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da jin daɗi ko tasirin aiki?

A'a. Rage zirga-zirga na iya inganta jin daɗi da mayar da hankali, yayin da ƙarancin kasancewar ofis na iya shafar gani da haɗin gwiwa. Waɗannan gaskiya ne amma a waje da wannan tsarin kuɗi da lokaci.
