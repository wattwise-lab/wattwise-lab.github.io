Formula

Change di original temperature to Celsius, then use di equation for di scale wey you want.

ConversionFormula
°F to °C°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
°C to °F°F = °C × 9/5 + 32
°C to KK = °C + 273.15
K to °C°C = K − 273.15

Absolute zero na 0 K = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F. Any input wey mean negative Kelvin no possible physically, so di page no dey accept am.

Example wey show the calculation

25 °C × 9/5 + 32 = 77 °F, and 25 + 273.15 = 298.15 K. Di scales meet at −40: −40 × 9/5 + 32 = −40 °F.

Where e fit work and limits

Temperature difference no be di same as absolute temperature: rise of 1 °C na di same as rise of 1 K, but rise of 1.8 °F. Dis page dey convert absolute temperatures, no be differences. Dem round di displayed values to 6 decimal places. Rounding fit change di last decimals wey you see, as di source tool note.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin be absolute zero?

0 K = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F — na di coldest temperature wey possible. Dis page no dey accept anything colder.

Why °C and °F dey meet at −40?

If you solve C = C × 9/5 + 32, you go get C = −40, di only temperature wey get di same number for both scales.

0 °C na the same as 32 °F?

Yes: water dey freeze at 0 °C = 32 °F = 273.15 K under standard atmospheric pressure.

How I fit convert temperature difference?

For temperature difference, remove di +32/+273.15 offsets: 10 °C warming = 18 °F warming = 10 K warming.

Why na 273.15 and no be 273?

273.15 na di exact offset between Celsius and Kelvin scales, as di definition of kelvin set am.
