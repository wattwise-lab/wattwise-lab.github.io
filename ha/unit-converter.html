Duk raka'a don wannan ƙimar

Naúrardarajar

Duk raka'a bakwai suna bayyana tsayi iri ɗaya; inci da abubuwan ƙafa da ke ƙasa ma'anoni ne daidai.

Ƙa’ida

Ƙimar manufa = shigarwa × ma'aunin tushen-raka'a ÷ ma'aunin na'urar manufa, inda abubuwa suke mita a kowace naúra.

NaúrarMita a kowace raka'a
Mita (m)1
Kilomita (km)1,000
Santimita (cm)0.01
Milimita (mm)0.001
Inci (in)0.0254
Kafa (ƙafa)0.3048
Mil (mi)1,609.344

An ayyana inci a matsayin daidai 0.0254 m, don haka ƙafa (12 inci) da mil na ƙasa da ƙasa (5,280 ƙafa) daidai suke.

Misalin aiki

1 m ÷ 0.3048 m/ft = 3.280840 ft, da 1 m ÷ 0.0254 m/in = 39.370079 in. Gudun 5 km shine 5,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = 3.106856 mil.

Amfani da iyakoki

An ƙi tsayin da ba daidai ba a matsayin marasa inganci. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa wurare na adadi na 6; lissafin da ke ƙasa ba a zagaye shi ba. Wannan shafin yana amfani da mil na duniya; ba a yi amfani da tsohon mil na binciken US ba (kimanin sassan 2 a kowace miliyan fiye da haka).

FAQ

Kafa nawa ne a cikin mita?

3.28083989501 ƙafa, an nuna shi azaman 3.280840 bayan zagayen nuni.

Shin inci daidai ne?

Ee: tunda 1959 inci na duniya an ayyana shi azaman daidai 25.4 mm.

Yadi fa?

Ba a jera shi daban ba: 1 yadi = 3 ƙafa = 0.9144 m. Shigar da ƙafa kuma a raba da 3, ko shigar da mita kuma yi amfani da sakamakon ƙafa.

Me yasa layin mil yake nuna adadi mai yawa na 1 m?

Domin 1 m ƙaramin yanki ne na mil (0.000621 mi); 6 wurare masu adadi suna sa a bayyane maimakon zagaye zuwa sifili.

Shin wannan yanki ko girma yana rufewa?

A'a — tsayi kawai. Yanki da girma suna da nasu masu juyawa a cikin kayan aikin da ke ƙasa.
