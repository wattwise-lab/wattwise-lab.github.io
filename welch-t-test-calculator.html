Welch Two-Sample t Test

Formula

Use t = (m₁−m₂)/√(s₁²/n₁+s₂²/n₂). m, s and n are each sample's mean, sample standard deviation and size; subscripts distinguish the samples. The two-sided p-value uses Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, without assuming equal variances.
