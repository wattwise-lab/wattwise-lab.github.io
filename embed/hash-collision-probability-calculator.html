Formula

n is the hash count and b is the width in bits. For n ≤ 10,000, P = 1 − ∏(1 − k/2^b), k = 0…n−1. Larger counts use P ≈ 1 − exp[−n(n−1)/(2×2^b)]. If n > 2^b, P = 1.
