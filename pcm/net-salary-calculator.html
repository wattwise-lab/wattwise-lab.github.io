Formula

Estimated tax = gross pay × effective tax rate. Net pay = gross pay − estimated tax − social contributions − other deductions. Net pay per year = net pay per month × 12. Na the effective rate wey you enter we dey use.

Example wey show the calculation

Gross pay na $6,000 monthly, effective tax rate na 15%, social contributions na $500 and other deductions na $0: estimated tax = $6,000 × 0.15 = $900, net pay = $6,000 − $900 − $500 − $0 = $4,600 per month, or $55,200 per year.

Where e fit work and limits

Tax rules dey vary by place and personal situation. We no model tax brackets, allowances, credits, employer contributions or benefits. Enter effective rate from your payslip or payroll calculation, and check important figures against official payroll calculation. Na estimate, no be payroll record or tax filing.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How this net salary calculator dey work?

Net pay = gross pay − estimated tax − social contributions − other deductions. For this model, estimated tax = gross pay × effective tax rate wey you enter.

Which effective tax rate I suppose enter?

Use actual tax divided by gross pay from recent payslip, or rate wey your payroll department confirm. We no fill any country or region rate in advance, because statutory rates and tax brackets differ too much to assume.

Why my pay after deductions dey negative?

Deductions pass gross pay for these figures. Check tax rate and deduction amounts. Negative result dey flag input problem, no be actual payslip.

Employer contributions dey inside?

No. We dey count only deductions from your gross pay. Employer-side contributions na separate cost; dem no reduce your net pay here.

This one handle bonus or overtime?

Only if you include dem in the monthly gross pay wey you enter. For irregular pay, use average or calculate the particular month separately.

I fit use this one file my tax?

No. Na take-home estimate. Filing depend on all the rules, allowances and credits for your jurisdiction.
