Formula

Use n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂. n₁ and n₂ are incident and transmitted refractive indices; θ₁ and θ₂ are angles from the normal. Angles are entered and displayed in degrees. If refraction has no real angle, the result identifies total internal reflection.
