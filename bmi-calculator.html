UNDERSTAND YOUR RESULT

Adult BMI categories and limitations

BMI is a screening measure for adults aged 20 and older, not a diagnosis or a direct measure of body fat.

< 18.5Underweight
18.5–24.9Healthy weight
25.0–29.9Overweight
30.0+Obesity

What BMI can tell you

BMI compares weight with height and is useful for population screening. A result outside the healthy range may be a reason to discuss broader health indicators with a clinician.

What BMI misses

It does not distinguish fat from muscle or account for body composition, pregnancy, medical history, laboratory results, or athletic training.
