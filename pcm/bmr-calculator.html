Formula

BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age (years) + 5 for men or − 161 for women. Na di Mifflin–St Jeor equation be dis.

Example wey show the calculation

Weight na 65 kg, height na 170 cm, age na 30, female: BMR = 10 × 65 + 6.25 × 170 − 5 × 30 − 161 = 650 + 1,062.5 − 150 − 161 = 1,401.5 kcal/day. Dat na 9,811 kcal every week and 42,045 kcal every 30 days. With di male constant (+5 instead of −161), di same inputs give 1,567.5 kcal/day. Use Reset example above to reproduce dis result.

Where e fit work and limits

BMR na resting expenditure only — e no include activity, digestion, and exercise, so e lower pass total daily expenditure. Mifflin–St Jeor equation na population estimate: dem fit am to group averages, so individual results dey vary with body composition, illness, medications, and other factors wey inputs no capture. E no be metabolic test and no be personal energy prescription.

Dis page na for educational estimates for adults. Di original Mifflin–St Jeor study use healthy adults wey age reach 19–78; take care if you apply am beyond dat group or to person own medical circumstances. Dis no be measured metabolic rate or instruction for calorie intake.

Mifflin and others (1990): equation for energy use at rest

Questions wey people dey ask often

Wetin be BMR?

Energy wey body dey use at complete rest to maintain basic functions. Any activity on top of rest dey add to am, na why total daily expenditure higher.

Why men and women dey get different results?

Mifflin–St Jeor equation dey add +5 for men and subtract 161 for women, wey reflect average differences for populations wey dem fit equation to. Na group adjustment, no be measurement of any individual.

BMR na the same as calories wey I need every day?

No. Daily need also include activity. Multiply BMR by activity factor — or use TDEE Calculator — for total daily estimate.

How accurate this estimate be?

Na estimate from population equation. Individual values fit differ materially; only metabolic test dey measure resting expenditure directly.

Una dey store my data?

No. This calculation dey run locally inside your browser; values wey you enter no dey reach any CalcMintly database.

Age dey affect am plenty?

Yes, through −5 × age term: each additional year dey lower estimate by 5 kcal/day at fixed weight and height.
