Ƙarar ruwa

Silinda mai zagaye madaidaiciya tare da ƙarshen lebur. An cire murfin ƙarshen da aka yanka, karkatarwa da kauri na bango.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da A = r²acos((r−h)/r)−(r−h)√(2rh−h²) da V = AL. r shine radius na ciki, h shine zurfin ruwa daga ƙasa, kuma L shine tsawon silinda, duk a cikin m. acos yana amfani da radians; V yana cikin m³.

Amfani da iyakoki
