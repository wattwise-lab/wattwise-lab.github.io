Formula

Integrate each polynomial term with ∫ aₙxⁿ dx = aₙx^(n+1)/(n+1), then evaluate F(b)−F(a). aₙ is a coefficient, n is its nonnegative integer power, and a and b are lower and upper limits. F is the summed antiderivative.
