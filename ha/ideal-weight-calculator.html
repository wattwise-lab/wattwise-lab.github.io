Nauyin Ma'auni ta jinsi, a tsayin ku

An auna sanduna zuwa babban ƙima. Bambancin tushe na 4.5 kg shine kawai tazara tsakanin sandunan a kowane tsayi.

Ƙa’ida

Don tsayi aƙalla ƙafa 5 (inci 60 ko 152.4 cm), nauyin tunani a cikin kg = tushe + 2.3 × (tsawo a cikin inci − 60). Tushen shine 50 kg ga maza da 45.5 kg ga mata. Tsawo a cikin inci = tsayi a cikin cm ÷ 2.54. Wannan shafin ba ya yin ƙarin bayani a ƙasa da 152.4 cm.

Misalin aiki

Tsawo 170 cm, mace: 170 ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 inci, wanda shine inci 6.93 sama da ƙafa 5. Nauyin da aka ambata = 45.5 + 2.3 × 6.93 = 45.5 + 15.94 = 61.4 kg (135.4 lb). Tushen namiji a tsayi ɗaya yana ba da 65.9 kg, kuma BMI a 61.4 kg da 170 cm shine 21.3. Yi amfani da misalin Sake saitawa a sama don sake haifar da wannan sakamakon.

Amfani da iyakoki

Devine wata tsohuwar ma'aunin lissafi ce bisa tsayi da jinsi, wadda ake ambata a rubuce-rubucen ƙayyade adadin magani. Ba ta la'akari da yanayin jiki, shekaru, ciki ko lafiyar mutum. Wannan kayan ilimi na manya ba ya zaɓar nauyin da za a yi amfani da shi wajen ƙayyade magani a asibiti, ba ya lissafa adadin magani, kuma ba ya ƙayyade nauyin lafiya da za a nema. Ga tsayin ƙasa da 152.4 cm, ba ya nuna kimantawa kuma ba ya ƙirƙirar wani mafi ƙarancin nauyi na dindindin.

FAQ

Shin wannan shine nauyina mafi kyau?

A'a. Lambar ma'auni ce ta tsayi daga dabarar allurai. Wane nauyi ya dace da mutum ya dogara da abubuwan da wannan shafin bai yi ba.

Me yasa maza da mata suka bambanta?

Tsarin yana amfani da tushe daban-daban - 50 kg ga maza, 45.5 kg ga mata - tare da 2.3 kg ɗaya a kowace inci sama da ƙafa 5.

Me ke faruwa a ƙasa da ƙafa 5?

Wannan shafin bai lissafa ƙasa da ƙafar 5 ba (152.4 cm). Dawo da nauyin tushe iri ɗaya ga kowane babba mai gajere zai ɓoye iyakokin dabarar. Yi amfani da kimantawa ta asibiti na mutum ɗaya lokacin da ake buƙatar shawarar allurai ko lafiya.

Ta yaya wannan yake da alaƙa da BMI?

Teburin yana nuna BMI a nauyin tunani don tsayinka, don haka zaka iya ganin inda lambar tunani take a cikin daidaitattun jeri na BMI.

An adana bayanai na?

A'a. Wannan lissafin yana aiki a cikin gida a cikin burauzarka kuma ƙimar da ka shigar ba a aika ta zuwa ga bayanan CalcMintly ba.

Shin ya kamata in yi nufin wannan lambar?

A ɗauke shi a matsayin wurin tunani don tattaunawa, ba manufa ba. Duk wani burin nauyi ya kamata ya yi la'akari da yanayin mutum da dabarar ba za ta iya gani ba.
