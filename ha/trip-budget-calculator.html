Kasafin kuɗi na rukuni tare da ma'aji

—

Sufuri da masauki kuɗi ne na rukuni; kashe kuɗi na yau da kullun ga kowane mutum ne. Yi amfani da kuɗi ɗaya a ko'ina. Ba a bayar da kuɗin musanya ko farashin tafiye-tafiye kai tsaye ba.

Kwatancen Yanayi

Har zuwa hotuna uku a cikin wannan shafin. Loda ɗaya yana maye gurbin shigarwar yanzu.

Ƙa’ida

Yi amfani da B = (T+nH+pdF)(1+w). T shine kudin jigilar rukuni, n shine dare, H shine kudin masauki na rukuni a kowace dare, p shine mutane, d shine kwanaki, F shine kashe kuɗi na yau da kullun ga kowane mutum, kuma w shine ɓangaren ma'ajiyar bayanai. Farashi yana amfani da kuɗi iri ɗaya.

Haɗa jigilar kaya da aka raba, zaman dare da kuma kashe kuɗin yau da kullun ga kowane mutum.

Amfani da iyakoki

Sufuri da masauki kuɗi ne na rukuni; kashe kuɗi na yau da kullun ga kowane mutum ne. Yi amfani da kuɗi ɗaya a ko'ina. Ba a bayar da kuɗin musanya ko farashin tafiye-tafiye kai tsaye ba.

Ana duba shigarwar don ƙimar da ta ɓace da kuma kewayon da aka yarda. Ba a nuna sakamako a wajen iyakataccen adadin lambobi ba. Zagaye yana shafar nuni, yayin da lissafin yana amfani da daidaiton lambobi na samfurin.

Duba raka'o'inku da zato kafin dogara da sakamako. Canza lakabin ba ya canza kuɗi ko naúrar zahiri.
