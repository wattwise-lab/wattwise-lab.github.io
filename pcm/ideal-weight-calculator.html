Formula

For height wey reach at least 5 feet (60 inches or 152.4 cm), reference weight in kg = base + 2.3 × (height in inches − 60). The base na 50 kg for men and 45.5 kg for women. Height in inches = height in cm ÷ 2.54. This page no extend the formula below 152.4 cm.

Example wey show the calculation

Height 170 cm, female: 170 ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 inches, which na 6.93 inches above 5 feet. Reference weight = 45.5 + 2.3 × 6.93 = 45.5 + 15.94 = 61.4 kg (135.4 lb). Male base at same height give 65.9 kg, and BMI at 61.4 kg and 170 cm na 21.3. Use Reset example above to reproduce this result.

Where e fit work and limits

Devine na old reference equation wey use height and sex, from literature about drug dosing. E no account for body composition, age, pregnancy or individual health. This tool for adult education no choose clinical dosing weight, calculate dose or set healthy target. Below 152.4 cm, e no show estimate; e no invent one fixed minimum weight.

Questions wey people dey ask often

Na this be my ideal weight?

No. Na height-based reference number from dosing formula. Wetin weight suit individual depend on factors this page no model.

Why the figures for men and women differ?

Formula use different bases — 50 kg for men, 45.5 kg for women — with same 2.3 kg per inch above 5 feet.

Wetin go happen below 5 feet?

This page no calculate below 5 feet (152.4 cm). To give every shorter adult the same base weight go hide the formula's limitation. Use individual clinical assessment when you need make dosing or health decision.

How this one relate to BMI?

Table show BMI at reference weight for your height, so you fit see where reference number sit for standard BMI ranges.

Una dey store my data?

No. This calculation dey run locally inside your browser; values wey you enter no dey reach any CalcMintly database.

I suppose target this number?

Treat am as reference point for discussion, not goal. Any weight goal suppose account for personal circumstances formula no fit see.
