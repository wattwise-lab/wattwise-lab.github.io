Sharar shekara-shekara ta amfani da shi a kowane wata

Amfani a kowane wata mai aikidarajar

Kuɗin wata-wata iri ɗaya, farashin madadin da watanni marasa aiki; amfani kawai a kowane wata mai aiki ya bambanta.

Ƙa’ida

Watanni masu aiki = 12 − watanni marasa aiki. Kudin madadin shekara-shekara = amfani ga kowane wata mai aiki × watanni masu aiki × farashin madadin. Bambanci = matsakaicin (kudin biyan kuɗi na shekara-shekara - farashin madadin shekara-shekara, 0). Kudaden watan rashin aiki wani ɓangare ne na kuɗin shekara-shekara, ba ƙarin hukunci ba.

Misalin aiki

Kudin wata-wata 68, amfani 2 a kowane watan amfani, madadin farashi 12 a kowane amfani da watanni 4 marasa amfani: kudin shekara = 68 × 12 = 816; watannin amfani = 12 − 4 = 8; madadin kudin = 2 × 8 × 12 = 192; bambanci = 816 − 192 = 624. Kudin watannin rashin amfani na 4 × 68 = 272 suna cikin kudin shekara tun farko. Waɗannan farashin misalai ne na ƙirƙira.

Amfani da iyakoki

Wannan kwatancen yana ɗauka cewa kun biya duk watannin 12 kuma kuna amfani da sabis ɗin a daidai lokacin da aka shigar a cikin watanni masu aiki kawai. Watanni marasa aiki suna rage amfani da shekara-shekara; an riga an haɗa kuɗin su kuma ba a ƙara su ba. Sauran fa'idodin biyan kuɗi, farashin sokewa da canjin farashi ba a yi su da tsari ba. Bambancin shine kwatantawa da biyan kowane amfani, ba garantin kuɗin da aka dawo da shi ta hanyar sokewa ba.

FAQ

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige wannan?

Rage farashin amfani da shekara ɗaya a madadin farashin ku daga kuɗin biyan kuɗi na shekara-shekara. Sakamakon ya gaza sifili.

Menene wata mai aiki?

Watan da kake amfani da sabis ɗin. Shigar da matsakaicin amfani na waɗannan watanni; watanni marasa aiki ba su da amfani.

An ƙirga kuɗin watan da ba a aiki ba sau biyu?

A'a. An nuna su a matsayin wani ɓangare na kuɗin shekara-shekara. Ba a ƙara su cikin bambancin ba.

Shin sokewa yana ceton duk wani bambanci?

Ba lallai ba ne. Kwatanta fa'idodin da kuke buƙata a zahiri, farashin madadin, duk wani kuɗin sokewa da sauran wa'adin kwangila.
