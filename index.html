DATA NOTE · SEPTEMBER 2026

The same appliance can cost four times more depending on where you live.

Run a 3.5 kW central air conditioner eight hours a day for 30 days and it uses an estimated 840 kWh. Applying EIA's 2024 statewide retail averages changes the bill impact dramatically.

The practical takeaway

Location matters, but your bill matters more. State averages mix residential, commercial, industrial and transportation customers, so use them only as a starting point.
