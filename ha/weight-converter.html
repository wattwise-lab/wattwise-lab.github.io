Duk raka'a don wannan ƙimar

Naúrardarajar

Duk raka'a huɗu suna bayyana nauyi iri ɗaya; fam ɗin da aka yi amfani da shi a nan shine fam ɗin avoirdupois na duniya.

Ƙa’ida

Canza ƙimar tushe zuwa kilogiram, sannan a raba ta da ma'aunin kilogiram na naúrar da aka nufa: target = value × source factor ÷ target factor.

NaúrarKilogram a kowace naúrar
Kilogram (kg)1
Gram (g)0.001
Fam (lb)0.45359237
Ounce (oz)0.028349523125

Ma'aunin fam daidai yake da yarjejeniyar ƙasa da ƙasa: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg. Oza shine 1/16 na wannan fam.

Misalin aiki

1 kg ÷ 0.45359237 kg/lb = 2.204623 lb, kuma 1 kg ÷ 0.028349523125 kg/oz = 35.273962 oz. Mutum 70 kg shine 70 ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.323584 lb.

Amfani da iyakoki

Ana ƙin nauyin da ba daidai ba a matsayin mara inganci. Ana zagaye ƙimar nuni zuwa 6 wurare na adadi; lissafin da ke ƙasa ba a zagaye shi ba. Wannan shafin yana amfani da fam da oza na avoirdupois (tsarin US/UK na yau da kullun), ba raka'o'in troy da ake amfani da su don karafa masu daraja ba.

FAQ

Fam nawa ne a cikin kilogram?

1 kg = 2.20462262185 lb, wanda aka nuna a matsayin 2.204623 bayan zagaye nuni.

Shin wannan fam ɗaya ne ake amfani da shi don zinare?

A'a. Zinariya tana amfani da fam na troy da oza na troy, waɗanda suka bambanta. Wannan shafin yana amfani da fam na avoirdupois don nauyin yau da kullun.

Oza nawa a cikin fam ɗaya?

16 oza = 1 fam, daidai.

Me yasa 6 wurare na decimal?

Don haka ƙananan juyawa (misali gram zuwa oza) suna kasancewa masu sauƙin karantawa ba tare da ɓoye ainihin ainihin ba.

Shin yana kula da duwatsu?

Ba kai tsaye ba: 1 dutse = 14 lb = 6.35029318 kg. Da farko canza duwatsu zuwa kilogiram kuma shigar da wannan ƙimar.
