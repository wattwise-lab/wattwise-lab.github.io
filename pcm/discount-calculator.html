Formula

Final price = original price × (1 − discount rate) − extra fixed discount, with zero as di lowest value. Amount wey you save = original price − final price.

Example wey show the calculation

Original price na $100, discount na 20%, extra discount na $5: final price = $100 × (1 − 0.20) − $5 = $80 − $5 = $75. So you save $25, and the effective discount na 25.00%.

Where e fit work and limits

Tax, shipping, minimum-spend rules, coupon order and exclusions fit change wetin you pay at checkout. If the shop remove fixed discount before percentage discount, the answer go differ small; this calculator dey remove percentage first, as the formula show.

Questions wey people dey ask often

How the discount calculator dey work?

Final price = original price × (1 − discount rate) − additional fixed discount, but e no fit go below zero. Na percentage we dey remove first, then the fixed amount.

Which order dem dey apply the discounts?

Percentage first, then fixed discount. If you reverse the order when both dey, the answer go change. Check the shop order if your total differ small.

Final price fit go below zero?

No. If the discounts pass the price, the answer go stop at $0.00, and the savings no go pass the original price.

This include tax?

No. Amount wey tax apply to depend on local rules and the kind of discount or coupon. E fit differ from the discounted amount wey you pay. Confirm the tax base wey apply before you use sales tax calculator.

Wetin be effective discount?

Total saving divided by original price. With 20% discount plus $5 off $100, effective saving na 25%, no be 20%.

Wetin about minimum spend or items wey no qualify?

We no model am. If the discount only work after you spend a minimum amount, or some items no qualify, the checkout total go differ.
